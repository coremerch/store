Design Pricing Cheat Sheet

pricing cheat sheet for freelance designer pricing ? 29 rules.

  1. Calculate your baseline rate by dividing your desired annual income by 1,000 hours (e.g., $75,000 ? 1,000 = $75/hour) before any expenses or taxes.
  2. Add 20-30% to your hourly rate for every overhead cost (software, hardware, insurance, accounting) not covered by clients.
  3. Use a flat project fee only when the scope is locked with a detailed contract and a 30% deposit upfront.
  4. Charge 1.5x your hourly rate for urgent rush projects with tight deadlines (same-day or 24-hour turnaround).
  5. Implement a 5-10% project buffer fee for scope creep to protect against unapproved additions or revisions.
  6. Offer tiered pricing for retainers: Bronze ($500/month for 10 hours), Silver ($1,200/month for 25 hours), Gold ($2,500/month for 50 hours).
  7. Set a minimum project fee of $500 for any design work under 10 hours to avoid nickel-and-diming clients.
  8. Increase rates by 10-15% every 6 months based on demand, portfolio growth, and client satisfaction ratings.
  9. Charge a 50% upfront deposit for new clients with no prior relationship to secure commitment and cover initial costs.
  10. Use the ?value-based pricing? method: research competitor rates, then price 10-20% higher if your work delivers 2x the results.
  11. Offer a 10% discount for annual contracts paid in full upfront to improve cash flow and client retention.
  12. Set a late fee of 1.5% per week on overdue invoices to encourage timely payments without damaging client relationships.
  13. Include a 15% rush fee for clients who request changes within 48 hours of delivery to prevent last-minute disruptions.
  14. Create a ?portfolio project? package priced at $2,000-$5,000 for speculative work used to attract high-value clients (non-billable but strategic).
  15. Use the ?cost-plus? method for print design: multiply material cost by 3 and add your hourly rate for assembly and oversight.
  16. Charge $150-$300 per hour for branding projects, justified by market research, strategy, and multi-format deliverables (logo, style guide, etc.).
  17. Set a $300-$800 ?discovery session? fee for clients who need a full brand audit or strategy before design work begins.
  18. Price website projects using a ?per page? model ($1,500-$3,500 per page) with a 50% deposit before design starts.
  19. Include a 20% ?maintenance fee? for post-launch support (updates, tweaks) in all web design contracts to generate recurring revenue.
  20. Offer a ?redesign only? package priced at 60% of a full project fee if the client provides existing brand assets and clear goals.
  21. Charge $75-$150 per hour for social media graphics when clients request 10+ posts per month with consistent branding.
  22. Set a $500 ?mood board? fee for clients who need direction before committing to a full branding project.
  23. Use a ?revision cap? of 3 rounds for flat-fee projects; charge $100 per additional revision to deter endless tweaks.
  24. Price packaging design at $2,500-$7,500 per design, factoring in die-line creation, mockups, and production-ready files.
  25. Charge $200-$400 per hour for UX/UI design, with a minimum project fee of $3,000 for wireframes, prototypes, and testing.
  26. Include a 10% ?kill fee? in contracts for clients who cancel mid-project, covering hours worked up to that point.
  27. Set a $1,000-$3,000 ?style guide development? fee for clients who need a cohesive visual identity across all assets.
  28. Price motion graphics at $150-$300 per hour or $5,000-$15,000 per minute of final output, depending on complexity.
  29. Use a ?tiered revision? system: 2 rounds included, $150 per round for revisions 3-5, $300 per round for 6+ rounds.
