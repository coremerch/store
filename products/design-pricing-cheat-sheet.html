Design Pricing Cheat Sheet

pricing cheat sheet for freelance designer pricing ? 30 rules.

  1. Set your hourly rate by calculating your desired annual salary, adding business expenses (software, hardware, subscriptions), and dividing by 2,080 (annual working hours), then multiply by 1.5 for a 50% profit buffer.
  2. Use the ?Value-Based Pricing Formula?: Research 3 competitors? quotes for a similar project, then price 10?20% higher if your work offers superior quality or turnaround time.
  3. Offer 3 pricing tiers (Basic, Pro, Enterprise) with clear deliverables and scope limits to upsell without negotiation, e.g., Basic: 1 round of revisions, Pro: 2 rounds + social media assets.
  4. Charge 50% upfront for new clients, 30% upon milestone delivery, and 20% at project completion to reduce non-payment risk and fund ongoing projects.
  5. Package recurring services (e.g., monthly social media designs) at a 15% discount vs. one-off pricing to secure steady income and discourage competitors from undercutting you.
  6. Implement a ?rush fee? of 20?50% for projects delivered in under 48 hours to compensate for lost flexibility and priority scheduling.
  7. Use the ?Square Foot? method for logo design: Set a base price ($200?$500) and add $100?$300 per additional logo variation or usage right (e.g., merch, international markets).
  8. Track ?billable hours? weekly and adjust rates annually based on your 80% utilization target (e.g., if you bill 32 hours/week, raise rates if you consistently hit 35+).
  9. Create a ?Project Cost Calculator? spreadsheet with columns for: client budget, estimated hours, your hourly rate, software costs, and desired profit margin to instantly generate quotes.
  10. Offer ?ala carte? add-ons post-project (e.g., $50 for extra social media formats) to increase revenue per client without overhauling your pricing structure.
  11. Use the ?Perceived Value? trick: Frame prices as ?Investment? (e.g., ?$2,000 Investment for a Brand Identity That Attracts High-End Clients?) to shift focus from cost to ROI.
  12. Charge 1.5x your standard rate for ?copycat? clients who ask for free revisions using competitor work as a reference?this filters out scope creepers early.
  13. Adopt the ?Retainer Model? for long-term clients: Offer 10?20 hours/month at a 20% discount versus hourly rates, with a 3-month minimum to stabilize cash flow.
  14. Set a ?minimum project fee? of $300?$500 for any design work to avoid wasting time on $50 logo requests that drain your energy.
  15. Use ?anchoring? in proposals: List a high-end option first (e.g., $5,000 for full brand suite) to make mid-tier options ($2,500) seem reasonable by comparison.
  16. Offer ?payment plans? for projects over $1,500, splitting costs into 3 or 4 equal installments to remove financial barriers for clients while securing recurring revenue.
  17. Charge ?late fees? of 1.5% per week on overdue invoices to incentivize timely payments and cover your administrative costs.
  18. Bundle services (e.g., ?Website + Branding + Social Media Kit? for $3,500) to increase average order value and reduce client churn by offering a ?one-stop shop?.
  19. Set ?portfolio project fees? for speculative work (e.g., $200 for a case study) to monetize free work while building your portfolio with paid clients.
  20. Use ?tiered retainers?: Bronze ($800/month: 5 social posts), Silver ($1,500: 10 posts + 2 graphics), Gold ($2,500: 15 posts + 5 graphics + 1 ad design) to cater to different client budgets.
  21. Charge ?usage fees? for logo designs: $100 for web-only, $300 for print, $500 for international use, and $1,000 for merchandise rights.
  22. Implement a ?kill fee? of 30?50% of the project cost if the client cancels after you?ve started work and provided initial concepts or research.
  23. Offer ?seasonal discounts? (e.g., 10% off holiday branding packages) to attract clients during slow periods without permanently devaluing your work.
  24. Use ?upsell timers?: Propose premium add-ons (e.g., animated logo for $200) during the final stages of a project when clients are most invested in the outcome.
  25. Set ?industry-specific rates?: Charge 30% more for clients in high-profit sectors (e.g., SaaS, real estate) to reflect the higher ROI of your designs for their business.
  26. Charge ?file delivery fees? of $50?$100 for additional formats (e.g., EPS, PDF/X) or custom sizes to cover your time spent prepping files.
  27. Offer ?rush hour? slots at 1.5x your rate for clients who need work done within 24?48 hours, but limit availability to 2?3 slots/month to avoid burnout.
  28. Use ?testimonial pricing?: Offer a 10% discount for clients who provide a video testimonial or case study, turning happy clients into marketing assets while incentivizing reviews.
  29. Set ?annual price locks? for retainer clients: Guarantee no price increases for 12 months if they commit to a 12-month contract, reducing client anxiety and securing long-term income.
  30. Charge ?consultation fees? of $100?$200 for initial strategy calls with clients who haven?t committed to a project, positioning yourself as a premium service provider.
