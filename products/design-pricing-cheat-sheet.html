Design Pricing Cheat Sheet

pricing cheat sheet for freelance designer pricing ? 29 rules.

  1. Always calculate your minimum hourly rate by dividing your desired annual income by 1,000 hours (effective billable hours per year).
  2. Charge at least 1.5x your hourly rate for rush or expedited projects to compensate for lost flexibility.
  3. Use a project-based pricing model for design systems, logos, or branding?never hourly?for predictable client budgets.
  4. Implement a 50% deposit before starting any project over $1,000 to protect against non-payment.
  5. Offer three pricing tiers (Basic, Pro, Deluxe) for website design to guide client choice and increase average project value.
  6. Set a $150?$300 one-time design audit fee for new clients to uncover quick-win opportunities before larger projects.
  7. Charge a 15% rush fee for any project due in less than 5 business days to reflect urgency premium.
  8. Use a retainer model (e.g., $2,000/month for 10 hours of design support) for ongoing clients to stabilize income.
  9. Always include a 10?15% buffer in quotes for scope creep?clients expect it and it prevents undercharging.
  10. Offer a ?Website Refresh? package at $1,200?$3,500 depending on page count and complexity to attract smaller businesses.
  11. Price logo redesigns starting at $800 with a 3-round revision limit to manage client expectations and scope.
  12. Charge $500?$1,200 for social media template kits (Canva/PPT) with unlimited customization within 7 days.
  13. Set a $250?$500 fee for one-off landing page design and include a 24-hour delivery guarantee to stand out.
  14. Use a sliding scale for e-commerce design: $3,000 for 5 products, $6,000 for 20 products, and $10,000+ for 50+ products.
  15. Offer a 10% discount for annual retainers paid upfront to incentivize long-term commitments and cash flow.
  16. Never negotiate your base rate?offer discounts only on add-ons or future services, not core deliverables.
  17. Include a 20% kill fee in contracts for projects canceled after 50% of the design work is complete.
  18. Charge $150?$300 per hour for 3D modeling or animation projects, reflecting the specialized skill and software costs.
  19. Price UX/UI audits at $400?$900 based on page count and deliver a 10-slide report with prioritized fixes.
  20. Set a $750?$1,500 flat fee for a complete brand identity package (logo, color palette, typography, style guide).
  21. Offer a ?Website Maintenance Plan? at $150/month including updates, backups, and security monitoring for existing clients.
  22. Charge $300?$800 for custom icon sets (20?50 icons) depending on complexity and delivery format (SVG, PNG, Figma).
  23. Use a value-based pricing model for high-impact projects like conversion-optimized landing pages?price at 1?3% of expected revenue lift.
  24. Set a $200?$500 premium for custom illustrations over stock assets to reflect originality and exclusivity.
  25. Offer a 5% discount for clients who provide clear, detailed briefs on the first attempt (reduces revisions).
  26. Price printed design materials (business cards, brochures) at 2x digital design fees to account for production and shipping costs.
  27. Charge $1,000?$3,000 for a full e-commerce website redesign, including Shopify integration and product photography guidelines.
  28. Set a $400 one-time fee for a 60-minute design consultation with a follow-up report and actionable next steps.
  29. Use a subscription model for stock asset access: $99/month for unlimited downloads and new releases from your curated library.
