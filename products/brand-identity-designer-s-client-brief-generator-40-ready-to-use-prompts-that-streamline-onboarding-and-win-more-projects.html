Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief Generator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts That Streamline Onboarding and Win More Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What three adjectives best describe the brand?s personality? Provide real-world examples that embody these traits.
  2. List the top three competitors and explain how this brand should differentiate visually and verbally from each.
  3. Describe the primary target audience using exact demographics, psychographics, and a fictional customer persona name.
  4. Identify the most important brand story or origin that should be reflected in the identity (e.g., founder?s journey, cultural roots, tech innovation).
  5. Specify any existing brand assets that must be preserved or integrated (e.g., logo variations, color codes, fonts, legacy imagery).
  6. Define the brand?s core values and rank them in order of importance to guide design decisions.
  7. Describe the desired emotional response the brand identity should evoke in customers (e.g., trust, excitement, nostalgia).
  8. Provide three visual mood boards or Pinterest links that represent the aesthetic direction the client wants to pursue.
  9. Specify the primary and secondary color palettes (include hex codes and Pantone equivalents if available).
  10. List the five most important brand messages that must be communicated through the identity design.
  11. Indicate whether the brand will be used primarily online, in print, or both?outline the key mediums and their technical constraints.
  12. Clarify the budget range for this project and whether it includes print production, web design, or marketing collateral.
  13. Identify any industry-specific regulations or guidelines that must be followed in the design (e.g., FDA labeling, accessibility standards).
  14. Describe the tone of voice for the brand?provide three sample sentences in the brand?s voice for different contexts (e.g., social media, packaging).
  15. List the key stakeholders who must approve the final identity, including their roles and approval timelines.
  16. Specify the preferred typography style (e.g., modern sans-serif, vintage serif, handwritten) and provide examples of fonts the client likes or dislikes.
  17. Indicate whether the brand will expand into new markets or products in the next 12?24 months?describe the anticipated scope.
  18. Provide examples of three logos or identities the client admires and explain why they resonate (e.g., simplicity, versatility, memorability).
  19. Clarify the timeline for this project, including key milestones (e.g., research phase, draft delivery, final approval, launch).
  20. Describe the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) in one sentence?this must be clearly reflected in the identity.
  21. Identify any cultural, religious, or ethical considerations that should influence the design (e.g., avoiding certain symbols, colors, or imagery).
  22. Specify the desired level of abstraction or literalness in the logo (e.g., abstract mark, wordmark, emblem, mascot).
  23. Provide examples of three brands whose identities the client wants to emulate in terms of tone, style, or execution.
  24. List the specific deliverables required (e.g., logo in vector and PNG formats, brand guidelines PDF, social media templates).
  25. Clarify whether the client has an in-house design team or prefers to work directly with you for all revisions.
  26. Identify any existing taglines, slogans, or mission statements that must be incorporated into the identity design.
  27. Describe the brand?s relationship with its audience?is it authoritative, friendly, playful, or professional? Provide examples of how this should be reflected.
  28. Provide the client?s website URL and social media handles to analyze current visual branding and consistency issues.
  29. Specify the preferred format for presenting design concepts (e.g., PDF, interactive prototype, physical mockups).
  30. List any specific icon styles or illustration approaches the client wants to use (e.g., minimalist, detailed, 3D, flat).
  31. Clarify whether the identity should feel retro, contemporary, futuristic, or timeless?provide visual references if possible.
  32. Identify the brand?s main communication channels (e.g., e-commerce, retail stores, trade shows) and their specific design requirements.
  33. Describe the client?s long-term vision for the brand?where do they see it in 5 years in terms of expansion, audience, and perception?
  34. Provide examples of three brands whose identities the client dislikes and explain the specific elements to avoid.
  35. Clarify the preferred use of space and layout (e.g., clean and minimal, dense and textured, asymmetrical) in all branded materials.
  36. List any specific textures, patterns, or materials that should be integrated into the identity (e.g., grainy paper, metallic foil, fabric textures).
  37. Specify the desired level of detail in the brand guidelines (e.g., strict rules for spacing, minimum logo sizes, color usage examples).
  38. Describe the brand?s hierarchy of visual elements?what should be emphasized first (e.g., logo, color, typography, imagery style)?
  39. Provide the client?s contact information for the main point of contact and any secondary stakeholders involved in the project.
  40. Clarify whether the client requires trademark or copyright registration assistance as part of the project scope.
