  12. What are the client?s competitors doing well in their branding that you should emulate?
  13. Describe the client?s current brand identity in 3 adjectives (e.g., outdated, inconsistent, sleek).
  14. What?s the client?s preferred color palette, and are there any colors they want to avoid?

  26. Does the client have a preferred project management tool (e.g., Trello, Asana, Monday)?
  27. What?s the client?s stance on AI-generated assets (e.g., allowed, not allowed, must disclose)?
  28. Does the client need responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop?
  29. What?s the client?s preferred method for receiving updates (e.g., live link, PDF, video walkthrough)?

  36. Does the client need a website audit as part of the project scope?
  37. What?s the client?s stance on user testing and A/B testing for the design?
  38. Does the client need a social media strategy or just the visual assets?
  39. What?s the client?s preferred method for handoff (e.g., cloud storage, USB drive, GitHub)?
