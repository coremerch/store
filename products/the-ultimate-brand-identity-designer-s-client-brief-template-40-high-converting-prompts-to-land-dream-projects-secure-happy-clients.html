The Ultimate Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief Template: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land Dream Projects & Secure Happy Clients

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the founder?s personal story or ?why? behind starting the business?
  2. List the top 3 adjectives the client wants people to feel when they interact with their brand.
  3. Who are the top 3 competitors the client admires and why?
  4. Describe the ideal customer persona in 3 sentences or less.
  5. What are the 5 most important values the brand must reflect?
  6. What tone of voice should the brand use?professional, friendly, bold, playful, or something else?
  7. Provide 3 real-world brands the client wants their brand to feel similar to in terms of visual style.
  8. What is the primary emotion the brand should evoke in its audience?
  9. What is the brand?s mission statement in one clear sentence?
  10. Are there any existing brand elements (colors, fonts, logos) that should be kept or built upon?
  11. What is the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)?
  12. What are the top 3 industry trends the brand should align with or stand out from?
  13. Who is the ideal customer?s biggest pain point that this brand solves?
  14. What type of imagery does the client want to use?photography, illustrations, abstract, minimalist?
  15. What are the 3 biggest fears or objections the client?s audience has about their product/service?
  16. Does the client want the brand to feel traditional, modern, futuristic, retro, or eclectic?
  17. What is the brand?s long-term vision in 5 years?
  18. What are the top 3 words the client does NOT want associated with their brand?
  19. What is the brand?s tagline or slogan in its current form (even if it?s a work in progress)?
  20. What is the client?s budget range for the branding project?
  21. What is the expected timeline for completing the branding project?
  22. Are there any cultural or regional sensitivities the brand needs to consider?
  23. What are the 3 most important deliverables the client expects from this project?
  24. Does the client have any brand guidelines or previous design work that must be reviewed?
  25. What is the brand?s elevator pitch in 10 words or less?
  26. What are the 3 most important brand colors the client envisions for the palette?
  27. Does the client want the brand to feel inclusive, exclusive, or neutral in its messaging?
  28. What is the brand?s stance on social or environmental issues (if any)?
  29. What are the 3 most important typography styles the client wants to explore?
  30. Does the client want the brand to feel luxury, budget-friendly, or mid-range?
  31. What is the brand?s current perception in the market (from the client?s perspective)?
  32. What are the 3 most important brand textures or patterns the client wants to incorporate?
  33. Does the client have any existing brand stories or narratives that should be highlighted?
  34. What is the brand?s target audience?s age range and primary location?
  35. What are the 3 most important brand symbols or icons the client wants to develop?
  36. Does the client want the brand to feel minimalist, maximalist, or somewhere in between?
  37. What is the brand?s current market position compared to competitors?
  38. What are the 3 most important brand messaging pillars (e.g., trust, innovation, reliability)?
  39. Does the client want the brand to feel playful, serious, or authoritative?
  40. What is the brand?s current visual identity missing that the client wants to address?
