Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Unlock Client Needs & Secure Projects Fast

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the one word your clients should use to describe your brand?
  2. List the top three adjectives your ideal customer uses to describe your business.
  3. Describe the emotions you want customers to feel when they see your brand.
  4. What problem does your brand solve for your customers?
  5. Who is your exact target audience? Include age, gender, occupation, and lifestyle.
  6. What are the three biggest misconceptions about your industry that you want to correct?
  7. Describe your brand?s personality in three sentences or less.
  8. What colors best represent your brand?s values and why?
  9. List the three brands you admire most and explain what you like about them.
  10. What type of typography aligns with your brand?s tone and message?
  11. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)?
  12. Describe your brand?s origin story in 50 words or less.
  13. What are the top three values your brand stands for?
  14. How do you want your brand to be perceived in social media conversations?
  15. What is your brand?s mission statement in one sentence?
  16. List three competitors and describe how your brand differs from theirs.
  17. What is the tone of voice your brand uses in communications (e.g., professional, friendly, quirky)?
  18. Describe the visual style you envision for your brand (minimalist, bold, vintage, etc.).
  19. What are the key messages you want to communicate in your brand?s tagline?
  20. List the three most important features or benefits of your product/service.
  21. What is the name of your brand, and does it have any special meaning or history?
  22. Describe the ideal customer journey from discovery to purchase.
  23. What are the three biggest challenges your brand faces in standing out?
  24. How do you want your brand to be remembered by customers?
  25. What type of imagery (photography, illustrations, etc.) best represents your brand?
  26. List three industry trends you want your brand to align with or avoid.
  27. What is your brand?s primary goal over the next 12 months?
  28. Describe the personality traits you want your brand to avoid at all costs.
  29. What is your brand?s elevator pitch (a 30-second summary of what you do)?
  30. List three visual elements (e.g., shapes, patterns, icons) that define your brand.
  31. What is the core message you want to repeat in all brand communications?
  32. Describe the first impression you want customers to have when they encounter your brand.
  33. What are the three most important attributes of your brand?s logo?
  34. How do you want your brand to evolve over the next five years?
  35. List three sensory elements (e.g., sounds, smells) that could enhance your brand experience.
  36. What is your brand?s stance on social or environmental issues?
  37. Describe how your brand will engage with customers on a regular basis (e.g., newsletters, events).
  38. What are the three biggest fears or objections your customers have about your brand?
  39. List three ways your brand can surprise and delight customers.
  40. What is the budget range for this branding project, and what does it include?
