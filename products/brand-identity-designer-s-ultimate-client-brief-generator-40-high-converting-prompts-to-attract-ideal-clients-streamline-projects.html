Brand Identity Designer's Ultimate Client Brief Generator: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Attract Ideal Clients & Streamline Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 38 prompts.

  1. What are the top three adjectives your client wants their brand to evoke?
  2. List the exact industry competitors your client admires and why.
  3. Describe the brand?s current visual identity (colors, fonts, logos) and what your client dislikes about it.
  4. What is the primary emotion your client wants customers to feel when interacting with their brand?
  5. Specify the exact target audience demographics (age, gender, location, income level) for this brand.
  6. What are the three core brand values your client wants to communicate through the identity?
  7. Describe the brand?s personality in three distinct sentences (e.g., ?professional yet approachable?).
  8. What is the client?s budget range for this project, and are there any budget constraints?
  9. What is the deadline for the brand identity deliverables, including phases if applicable?
  10. Does the client have existing brand guidelines or style references they want to incorporate?
  11. What is the client?s preferred communication style (e.g., formal, casual, collaborative)?
  12. List three brands your client considers successful and wants to emulate visually.
  13. What is the brand?s mission statement in one clear sentence?
  14. Does the client want the new identity to feel modern, classic, playful, or something else?
  15. What is the client?s preferred color palette (provide hex codes if available)?
  16. Does the client have a preferred typeface or font family for the brand?
  17. What is the client?s stance on minimalism versus maximalism in design?
  18. List three keywords that should be associated with the brand?s visual identity.
  19. Does the client want the brand identity to feel luxury-oriented or accessible?
  20. What is the client?s preferred logo format (wordmark, symbol, combination mark, etc.)?
  21. Does the client have any existing marketing materials that the new identity must align with?
  22. What is the client?s preferred style for imagery (photography, illustration, abstract, etc.)?
  23. List three design styles your client dislikes and why (e.g., ?grunge because it feels outdated?).
  24. Does the client want the brand identity to feel corporate, creative, or somewhere in between?
  25. What is the client?s preferred tone of voice for written content (e.g., friendly, authoritative, humorous)?
  26. Does the client want the brand identity to feel bold and loud or subtle and refined?
  27. List three adjectives that describe how the client does NOT want their brand to feel.
  28. What is the client?s preferred format for logo usage (digital, print, both)?
  29. Does the client have any specific cultural or regional design considerations?
  30. What is the client?s preferred approach to negative space in their visual identity?
  31. List three design trends your client wants to avoid in this project.
  32. Does the client want the brand identity to feel nostalgic, futuristic, or timeless?
  33. What is the client?s preferred method for presenting design concepts (digital mockups, physical samples, etc.)?
  34. Does the client have any accessibility requirements for the brand identity (e.g., color contrast, font size)?
  35. What is the client?s preferred process for feedback and revisions (e.g., iterative, one-round)?
  36. List three design elements your client wants to emphasize (e.g., gradients, textures, geometric shapes).
  37. Does the client want the brand identity to feel traditional or innovative?
  38. What is the client?s preferred format for delivering the final brand identity files (PDF, AI, EPS, etc.)?
