Freelance Pricing Power Sheet: From Student to Pro in 30 Days

pricing cheat sheet for design students ? 29 rules.

  1. List your prices in ranges (e.g., $50?$150 per logo) to attract more clients while keeping wiggle room.
  2. Charge 20?30% more for rush jobs to compensate for lost flexibility and overtime.
  3. Use a flat fee for small projects (e.g., $120 for a social media post bundle) to avoid scope creep.
  4. Break large projects into milestones with upfront payments (e.g., 40% deposit, 30% midpoint, 30% on delivery).
  5. Research competitor rates by checking Fiverr, 99designs, and Etsy for similar services before setting your prices.
  6. Offer a ?student discount? (15?20% off) to attract early clients and build your portfolio.
  7. Set a minimum project fee (e.g., $75) to avoid undervaluing your time on tiny tasks like quick edits.
  8. Use a time-tracking tool (like Toggl) to estimate project hours accurately and justify your rates.
  9. Charge extra for revisions beyond the agreed-upon 2?3 rounds (e.g., $30 per additional revision).
  10. Create package deals (e.g., ?Brand Identity Starter? for $350) to upsell and simplify client decisions.
  11. Add a 10?15% fee for non-standard requests (e.g., custom illustrations, animated logos).
  12. Require a 50% deposit for first-time clients to filter out tire-kickers and ensure commitment.
  13. Offer a ?quick sketch? service (e.g., $40 for a 1-hour logo concept) for clients on a tight budget.
  14. Use a tiered pricing model (Basic, Pro, Premium) to cater to different client needs and budgets.
  15. Add a ?rush fee? of $50?$100 for projects due in under 48 hours to prioritize your schedule.
  16. Bundle services (e.g., logo + business card design for $250) to increase average order value.
  17. Set a late payment fee (e.g., 1.5% of the invoice) to incentivize on-time payments from clients.
  18. Offer a ?referral bonus? (e.g., $20 off next project) to clients who send you new business.
  19. Use a contract template (like HelloSign or DocuSign) to outline payment terms, revisions, and deadlines upfront.
  20. Charge a ?kill fee? (e.g., 30% of the project cost) if the client cancels last-minute to compensate for lost time.
  21. Create a ?membership? option (e.g., $50/month for 2 design requests) for recurring revenue from loyal clients.
  22. Adjust prices seasonally (e.g., 10% off in January for ?New Year, New Brand? campaigns).
  23. Use a ?project deposit calculator? to determine fair upfront payments based on project complexity.
  24. Offer a ?portfolio refresh? package (e.g., $180 for 3 updated designs) to attract clients needing fresh work.
  25. Charge a ?domain/hosting fee? (e.g., $10/month) if you?re handling website setup for clients.
  26. Set a ?minimum design time? (e.g., 2 hours at $50/hour) for small tasks to avoid undercharging.
  27. Use a ?profit margin? of 50?70% on your time to account for taxes, software, and overhead costs.
  28. Create a ?limited-time offer? (e.g., $200 off for the first 10 sign-ups) to generate initial clients.
  29. Charge a ?consultation fee? (e.g., $60 for a 30-minute call) to qualify leads and avoid freebie seekers.
