client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What are the core values your business stands for, and how do you want customers to feel when they interact with your brand?
  2. Describe your ideal customer in one sentence, including their age, profession, and lifestyle habits.
  3. List three brands you admire and explain why their visual identity resonates with you.
  4. What is the primary emotion you want your brand to evoke in your target audience? (e.g., trust, excitement, nostalgia)
  5. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) in 10 words or less?
  6. What colors currently represent your brand, and which emotions do they convey to your audience?
  7. Do you have existing brand assets (logos, fonts, color codes, imagery) that must be incorporated into the new identity? If so, provide them.
  8. What is your brand?s tone of voice? (e.g., professional, playful, authoritative, friendly)
  9. Describe your brand?s personality in three adjectives (e.g., bold, minimalist, quirky).
  10. What are the top three adjectives you want people to associate with your brand?
  11. Who are your three biggest competitors, and what do you like or dislike about their visual identities?
  12. What industries or trends should your brand identity align with or distance itself from?
  13. Do you have a brand story or origin that should be reflected in the identity? If yes, share a 2-3 sentence summary.
  14. What is your brand?s mission statement, if any?
  15. What is your brand?s vision for the next 3-5 years?
  16. Do you have any cultural or social causes your brand supports that should be subtly reflected in the identity?
  17. What is your primary business goal for rebranding? (e.g., attract younger audience, appear more premium, modernize outdated look)
  18. What is your budget range for this project, including design and implementation?
  19. What is your preferred timeline for completing this project from start to launch?
  20. Do you have any specific deliverables in mind? (e.g., logo, color palette, typography system, brand guidelines, social media templates)
  21. Should the new identity be scalable for both digital and print applications? If so, provide examples of where it will be used most.
  22. Do you have a preferred design style? (e.g., vintage, futuristic, hand-drawn, geometric, flat design)
  23. Are there any design elements or styles you absolutely want to avoid? (e.g., overly complex, too childish)
  24. What is the name of your business, and does it have any meaning or symbolism that should influence the logo?
  25. Do you have a tagline or slogan that should be integrated into the visual identity?
  26. What is your brand?s primary product or service, and how should the identity reflect its key features?
  27. Describe your ideal customer?s biggest pain point that your product/service solves.
  28. What is your brand?s biggest differentiator compared to competitors?
  29. Do you have any existing customer feedback about your current brand that should inform the redesign?
  30. What is your brand?s current perception in the market, and what do you want it to be after the rebrand?
  31. Are there any specific design trends you?d like to incorporate, or do you prefer timeless elements?
  32. Do you have a preferred font style? (e.g., serif, sans-serif, script, display) If so, provide examples you like.
  33. What is your brand?s relationship with its customers? (e.g., transactional, community-driven, luxury service)
  34. Should the identity include any mascots, icons, or custom illustrations? If yes, describe your vision.
  35. Do you have any existing social media handles or hashtags that should be considered in the new identity?
  36. What is your brand?s stance on sustainability or ethical practices that could be reflected in the design?
  37. Are there any legal or trademark considerations for your brand name or logo that designers should be aware of?
  38. Do you have a preferred file format for the final logo (e.g., vector, PNG, SVG)?
  39. What is your process for approving design concepts, and how many rounds of revisions are included?
  40. Do you have a preferred communication style with your designer (e.g., weekly updates, detailed feedback)?
