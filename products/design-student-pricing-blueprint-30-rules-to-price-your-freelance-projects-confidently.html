Design Student Pricing Blueprint: 30 Rules to Price Your Freelance Projects Confidently

pricing cheat sheet for design students ? 30 rules.

  1. Charge 1.5x your hourly rate for logo design projects to account for revisions and client meetings.
  2. Set a flat fee for business card designs based on the number of variants (e.g., $150 for 2 variants, $250 for 5 variants).
  3. Offer a 10% discount for clients who pay 50% upfront and 50% upon project completion.
  4. Price social media templates at $120 per set (e.g., Instagram Story, Post, and Reel templates).
  5. Charge $200?$400 for a basic website wireframe, depending on complexity and number of pages.
  6. Add a 20% rush fee for projects due within 48 hours of the deadline.
  7. Set a minimum flat fee of $50 for any single-page flyer or poster design.
  8. Price app icon designs at $150 each, with a 10% discount for multiple icons (e.g., $270 for two).
  9. Charge $300?$600 for a full brand identity package (logo, color palette, typography, and style guide).
  10. Offer a 15% discount for students who book 3+ projects in a single month.
  11. Price custom illustration work at $75?$150 per hour, depending on style and complexity.
  12. Set a flat fee of $250 for a resume and cover letter redesign package.
  13. Charge $100 for each additional social media post template beyond the first set.
  14. Add a 10% fee for projects requiring stock images or fonts (client must provide the assets).
  15. Price a full website design at $800?$1,500, depending on the number of pages and custom features.
  16. Offer a 5% discount for clients who refer another student to your services.
  17. Charge $120 for a single landing page design and $200 for a multi-page landing site.
  18. Set a minimum fee of $75 for any packaging or label design project.
  19. Price infographic design at $200?$400, depending on data complexity and number of visuals.
  20. Add a 15% fee for projects requiring animated elements (e.g., GIFs or simple animations).
  21. Charge $250 for a full LinkedIn banner and profile picture redesign package.

  26. Set a minimum fee of $60 for any single icon design project.
  27. Price a full slide deck (e.g., PowerPoint or Google Slides) redesign at $250?$400, depending on the number of slides.
  28. Add a 25% fee for projects requiring custom typography or hand-drawn elements.
  29. Charge $350 for a full YouTube channel art and profile picture redesign package.
  30. Set a flat fee of $80 for a single Twitter/X header or Facebook cover photo design.
