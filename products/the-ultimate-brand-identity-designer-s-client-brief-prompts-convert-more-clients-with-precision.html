The Ultimate Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief Prompts (Convert More Clients with Precision)

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. List the client?s top 3 business competitors?identify their visual branding colors, fonts, and logo styles.
  2. Describe the client?s ideal customer persona in 3 sentences?include their age, profession, and aesthetic preferences.
  3. Provide 5 adjectives that best describe the brand?s personality (e.g., bold, elegant, playful, trustworthy).
  4. Share 3 existing brand assets (logos, color palettes, fonts) the client already uses and explain why they want to refresh them.
  5. Specify the primary and secondary use cases for the brand identity (e.g., website, packaging, social media, merchandise).
  6. Name 3 brands the client admires for their visual identity and explain what they like about each.
  7. Define the brand?s mission statement in one concise sentence.
  8. List the client?s top 3 business goals for the next 12 months?how will the new brand identity support these?
  9. Describe the tone of voice for the brand (e.g., formal, friendly, witty, authoritative) and provide 2 example sentences.
  10. Provide the client?s current and desired brand positioning (e.g., ?affordable luxury? vs. ?premium everyday?).
  11. Share any industry-specific design trends the client wants to incorporate or avoid (e.g., minimalism, retro, futuristic).
  12. List the client?s key stakeholders and their roles in the branding project (e.g., CEO, marketing director, product lead).
  13. Specify the primary and secondary color palettes?include hex codes, RGB values, and pantone references if available.
  14. Describe the client?s current visual identity pain points (e.g., colors clash, logo is too complex, fonts are outdated).
  15. Provide examples of 3-5 logo styles the client likes (e.g., wordmark, emblem, abstract, monogram).
  16. List the client?s preferred font categories (e.g., serif, sans-serif, script, geometric) and 2-3 font examples.
  17. Specify the client?s budget range for the branding project and any cost constraints (e.g., ?under $2,000? or ?must include trademark registration?).
  18. List the client?s timeline for the project?include key milestones (e.g., first drafts in 2 weeks, final delivery in 6 weeks).
  19. Describe the client?s expectations for brand guidelines?do they need a simple one-pager or a detailed 50-page manual?
  20. Share any existing brand guidelines or style sheets the client has, even if they?re outdated.
  21. Specify the client?s preferred file formats for deliverables (e.g., AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) and any specific software requirements (e.g., Adobe Illustrator).
  22. List the client?s target audience locations (e.g., global, local, specific countries/cities) and any cultural considerations.
  23. Describe the client?s social media presence?include platform names, handle formats, and current visual themes.
  24. Provide examples of 3-5 taglines or slogans the client has considered or used in the past.
  25. List the client?s preferred imagery style (e.g., photography, illustrations, 3D renders, stock photos) and provide 2-3 examples.
  26. Specify if the client has any trademark or legal considerations for the new brand identity (e.g., existing trademarks, domain availability).
  27. Describe the client?s packaging needs?include dimensions, materials, and any existing packaging designs to reference.
  28. List the client?s preferred logo variations (e.g., horizontal, vertical, icon-only, monochrome) and their use cases.
  29. Provide examples of 3-5 patterns or textures the client likes for use in secondary branding elements.
  30. Specify if the client needs additional collateral designs (e.g., business cards, letterheads, email signatures, signage).
  31. List the client?s preferred animation or motion graphics style for digital applications (e.g., smooth, kinetic, hand-drawn).
  32. Describe the client?s offline marketing needs (e.g., print ads, brochures, trade show booths) and their preferred styles.
  33. Provide the client?s current and desired brand recognition level (e.g., ?local known? vs. ?internationally recognized?).
  34. List the client?s preferred imagery sources (e.g., in-house photography, stock sites like Unsplash/Shutterstock, custom illustrations).
  35. Specify if the client has any accessibility requirements for the brand identity (e.g., color contrast, font size, screen reader compatibility).
  36. Describe the client?s preferred logo animation style for social media and digital platforms (e.g., fade-in, morphing, sticky).
  37. List the client?s preferred typography hierarchy for digital and print materials (e.g., H1, H2, body text, captions).
  38. Provide examples of 3-5 brand identities the client dislikes and explain why.
  39. Specify if the client needs a brand story or ?about us? content to accompany the new identity.
  40. List the client?s preferred methods for gathering feedback (e.g., email, Slack, in-person meetings, design review tools like InVision).
