Web Designer?s Client Brief Generator: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Attract Ideal Clients & Close Deals Faster

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary business goal of the client?s website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio)
  2. Who is the exact target audience for this website? (age, profession, interests, pain points)
  3. What are the top 3 competitors the client admires, and what specific elements do they want to replicate or avoid?
  4. What is the client?s preferred color palette and brand fonts, and can they provide a brand style guide?
  5. What is the client?s budget range for this project, and what payment milestones do they prefer?
  6. What is the expected timeline for the project from start to launch?
  7. Does the client have an existing website that needs to be redesigned, and if so, what are the main issues with it?
  8. What are the 5 key features or pages the client absolutely must have on the website?
  9. Does the client need an e-commerce functionality, and if so, which platform do they prefer (Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, etc.)?
  10. What is the client?s preferred content management system (CMS) for easy updates? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)
  11. What is the client?s stance on mobile responsiveness, and do they have specific mobile-first design requirements?
  12. Does the client require SEO optimization, and if so, do they have specific keywords or SEO strategies in mind?
  13. What is the client?s preferred tone for the website copy? (e.g., professional, friendly, humorous, minimalist)
  14. Does the client have a preferred layout or structure in mind for the homepage, and can they provide examples?
  15. What are the client?s expectations for the number of revisions included in the project?
  16. Does the client need a blog or news section, and if so, how often do they plan to post content?
  17. What is the client?s stance on animations, parallax scrolling, or other interactive elements?
  18. Does the client have a preferred hosting provider or domain registrar?
  19. What are the client?s expectations for website security and compliance (e.g., GDPR, SSL certificates)?
  20. Does the client require integration with third-party tools or APIs (e.g., payment gateways, CRM, email marketing)?
  21. What is the client?s preferred approach to user experience (UX) testing, and do they have any specific UX research they want included?
  22. Does the client have a preferred tone for error messages, 404 pages, or loading screens?
  23. What is the client?s stance on pop-ups, chatbots, or other conversion-focused elements?
  24. Does the client need a multilingual website, and if so, which languages are required?
  25. What is the client?s preferred approach to accessibility, and do they have specific accessibility standards they want met (e.g., WCAG 2.1)?
  26. Does the client require a style guide or design system to maintain consistency across future updates?
  27. What are the client?s expectations for ongoing maintenance and support after the website launch?
  28. Does the client have any specific legal or compliance requirements for the website (e.g., disclaimers, privacy policies)?
  29. What is the client?s preferred approach to analytics and tracking (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar, heatmaps)?
  30. Does the client need a membership or login area, and if so, what level of security is required?
  31. What is the client?s stance on social media integration and shareable content?
  32. Does the client require a newsletter signup form or email collection functionality?
  33. What is the client?s preferred approach to typography, and do they have specific font preferences or restrictions?
  34. Does the client need a portfolio or gallery section, and if so, how many items do they plan to showcase?
  35. What is the client?s stance on dark mode or alternative color schemes?
  36. Does the client require a contact form, and if so, what fields should be included?
  37. What is the client?s preferred approach to call-to-action (CTA) buttons (e.g., color, size, placement)?
  38. Does the client need a FAQ or help center section, and if so, what questions should be addressed?
  39. What is the client?s stance on micro-interactions, hover effects, or other subtle animations?
  40. Does the client require a sitemap or wireframes as part of the design process?
