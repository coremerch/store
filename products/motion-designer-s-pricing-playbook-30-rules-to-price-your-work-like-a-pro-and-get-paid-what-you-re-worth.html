Motion Designer's Pricing Playbook: 30 Rules to Price Your Work Like a Pro and Get Paid What You're Worth

pricing cheat sheet for motion designers ? 30 rules.

  1. Charge $100?$150 per second for high-end motion graphics in client presentations or broadcast ads, adjusting for complexity and client budget.

  11. Use a ?per-minute? pricing model for longer-form content like documentaries or training videos, charging $500?$2,000 per minute based on style and complexity.
  12. For looping social media graphics, charge $200?$800 per animation, with higher prices for custom illustrations or advanced motion effects.
  13. Set a minimum project fee of $1,000 for any client to ensure you?re not wasting time on low-value gigs that drain your resources.
  14. Offer a ?maintenance retainer? of $500?$1,500 per month for clients who need ongoing updates or small tweaks to existing motion assets.

  17. For motion graphics in email campaigns, charge $1,000?$3,000 per animation set, factoring in compatibility and optimization needs.
  18. Use a ?usage-based? pricing model for motion work intended for TV, film, or large-scale marketing?charge $2,000?$10,000+ depending on territory and duration.
  19. Price motion graphics for product demos or app onboarding at $1,500?$4,000, adjusting for interactivity and technical complexity.
  20. Charge $400?$1,200 for custom typography animations in title sequences, logos, or social posts, based on font complexity and animation style.
  21. For animated infographics, charge $800?$3,000 per project, with higher prices for data visualization or custom illustrations.
  22. Set a ?kill fee? of 30?50% if the client cancels mid-project, to compensate for time already invested and lost opportunities.
  23. Price motion graphics for e-learning modules at $1,000?$4,000 per module, depending on the length and interactivity level required.
  24. Charge $600?$2,000 for animated social media stories or Reels, factoring in platform-specific constraints and trends.
  25. Offer a ?starter pack? for small businesses at $500?$1,500, including 3?5 basic motion graphics assets to build their brand presence.
  26. For animated ads (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube), charge $1,200?$3,500 per ad set, including variations for A/B testing.
  27. Price motion graphics for trade show booths or kiosks at $3,000?$8,000, depending on duration and custom hardware integration needs.
  28. Charge $900?$2,500 for animated social media banners or headers, with higher prices for custom illustrations or interactive elements.
  29. Use a ?project scope? document to define deliverables, timelines, and pricing upfront?avoid vague agreements that lead to disputes.
  30. Always include a clause in your contract that grants you usage rights for your portfolio and future marketing until full payment is received.
