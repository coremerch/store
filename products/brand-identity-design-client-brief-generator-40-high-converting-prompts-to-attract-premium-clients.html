Brand Identity Design Client Brief Generator: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Attract Premium Clients

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What are the top 3 adjectives that describe the brand?s personality?
  2. Describe the target audience in 3-5 detailed personas including demographics, psychographics, and pain points.
  3. What is the brand?s mission statement in one clear and concise sentence?
  4. List 3 competitors and explain why this brand is different from them in terms of visual identity.
  5. What are the brand?s core values? Provide examples of how these values should be reflected in the design.
  6. Describe the tone of voice for the brand. Should it be friendly, professional, bold, or something else?
  7. What is the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)? How can this be communicated visually?
  8. Provide 3-5 brand mood boards or references that inspire the desired aesthetic (e.g., color palettes, typography styles, logos).
  9. What are the primary colors that should be avoided in this brand?s identity? Why?
  10. What is the brand?s tagline or slogan? If none exists, suggest a compelling one that aligns with the visual identity.
  11. Describe the brand?s ideal customer journey. How should the visual identity adapt across touchpoints (e.g., website, packaging, social media)?
  12. What is the brand?s story or origin? How can this narrative be incorporated into the visual identity?
  13. Provide examples of logos or symbols that align with the brand?s ethos. Are there any logos or symbols that should be avoided?
  14. What is the brand?s current visual identity? If applicable, list what elements need to be refreshed or discarded.
  15. Describe the brand?s industry or niche. Are there any design trends or conventions that should be followed or broken?
  16. What are the brand?s long-term goals? How should the visual identity evolve to reflect these aspirations?
  17. List 3-5 fonts that embody the brand?s personality. Should the typography be modern, classic, handwritten, or something else?
  18. What is the brand?s stance on social and environmental issues? Should this be reflected in the visual identity?
  19. Describe the brand?s visual hierarchy. What elements should stand out the most in marketing materials?
  20. What is the brand?s pricing strategy? Does the visual identity need to convey luxury, affordability, or something in between?
  21. Provide examples of successful rebranding projects. What elements from these examples should be incorporated or avoided?
  22. What is the brand?s preferred format for delivering the final identity? (e.g., style guides, digital assets, print-ready files)
  23. Describe the brand?s ideal customer avatar. What are their aspirations, fears, and daily habits?
  24. What are the brand?s key performance indicators (KPIs)? How can the visual identity support these metrics?
  25. List 3-5 design styles that the brand should avoid. Why don?t they align with the brand?s personality?
  26. What is the brand?s preferred color palette? Provide hex codes and examples of how these colors should be used.
  27. Describe the brand?s ideal customer interaction. Should the visual identity be minimalist, intricate, or playful?
  28. What are the brand?s legal or regulatory requirements that may impact the visual identity (e.g., trademarks, copyrights)?
  29. Provide examples of brands with strong visual identities. What elements from these examples should be emulated?
  30. What is the brand?s preferred imagery style? (e.g., photography, illustrations, abstract art)
  31. Describe the brand?s target audience?s media consumption habits. Where should the visual identity make the most impact?
  32. What are the brand?s secondary colors? Provide hex codes and examples of how they should be used.
  33. List 3-5 design elements that are non-negotiable for this brand (e.g., specific icons, patterns, or textures).
  34. What is the brand?s preferred approach to typography hierarchy? (e.g., headlines, subheadings, body text)
  35. Describe the brand?s ideal customer?s emotional response to the visual identity. What feelings should the design evoke?
  36. What are the brand?s plans for expansion or scaling? How should the visual identity accommodate future growth?
  37. Provide examples of brands that failed with their visual identity. What lessons can be learned from these examples?
  38. What is the brand?s preferred approach to negative space in the visual identity?
  39. Describe the brand?s ideal customer?s first impression of the visual identity. What should stand out immediately?
  40. List 3-5 design tools or software the brand prefers for creating or manipulating visuals (e.g., Adobe Illustrator, Procreate).
