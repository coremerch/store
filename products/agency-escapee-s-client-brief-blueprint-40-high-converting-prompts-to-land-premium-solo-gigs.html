Agency Escapee's Client Brief Blueprint: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land Premium Solo Gigs

  12. Request: 'Provide a list of 3-5 mandatory features or functionality your design must include.'

  14. Request: 'Send me any existing brand assets (logos, fonts, color codes, imagery) you have, even if they?re incomplete.'

  24. Request: 'Send me any past testimonials or case studies you?ve used. If none, describe your ideal client testimonial.'

  30. Request: 'Provide a list of 3-5 brands that represent your ideal aesthetic?include their website URLs.'
  31. Ask: 'What is your biggest concern about working with a designer who used to work at an agency? How can I overcome it?'
  32. Request: 'Send me any past project budgets and timelines?highlight what worked and what didn?t.'
  33. Ask: 'What is your preferred method for approving design phases? (e.g., email approval, live presentation, shared drive feedback)'
  34. Request: 'Provide a list of 5-10 industry-specific terms or phrases your audience uses?include their definitions.'

  36. Request: 'Send me any past project post-mortems or retrospectives?even if they?re informal.'
  37. Ask: 'What is your preferred method for receiving final deliverables? (e.g., cloud storage, physical media, digital formats)'
  38. Request: 'Provide a list of 3-5 brands that represent your worst aesthetic nightmares?include their website URLs.'
  39. Ask: 'What is your biggest challenge in articulating your design needs? Share an example of how you?ve struggled in the past.'
  40. Request: 'Send me any past project proposals or pitches you?ve received?highlight what stood out or fell flat.'
