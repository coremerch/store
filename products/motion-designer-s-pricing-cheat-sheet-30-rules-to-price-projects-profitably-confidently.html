Motion Designer's Pricing Cheat Sheet: 30 Rules to Price Projects Profitably & Confidently

pricing cheat sheet for motion designers ? 30 rules.

  1. Charge a flat project fee for simple logo animations under 5 seconds, starting at $300?$800 depending on complexity and client budget.
  2. Use a day rate of $600?$1,200 for small to medium motion graphics projects (e.g., social media ads, explainer videos under 30 seconds).
  3. For long-form projects (e.g., 1?3 minute corporate videos), quote a weekly rate of $3,000?$6,000 or a project fee based on estimated hours ? $120?$180.
  4. Set a retainer fee of $2,500?$5,000/month for ongoing motion design work like weekly social content or recurring video series.
  5. Break down motion graphics jobs into 5-second increments and charge $150?$300 per segment for clients who want modular content.
  6. Offer a rush fee of 50%?100% on top of the base rate for projects due in less than 48 hours.
  7. Include a 20%?30% deposit before starting any motion design project to secure your time and cover initial costs.
  8. For freelance motion design on platforms like Fiverr or Upwork, start with $50?$150 per video to attract initial clients, then raise rates as you build reviews.
  9. Charge $200?$600 for custom After Effects templates (MOGRTs) sold on marketplaces like Envato, depending on exclusivity and complexity.
  10. Offer a one-time licensing fee of $500?$2,000 for motion templates sold exclusively to a single client for their use.
  11. Set a minimum project fee of $250 for any motion design work, even small tweaks or revisions, to avoid undervaluing your time.
  12. Use a tiered pricing model: $500 for basic motion (e.g., text animation), $1,200 for intermediate (e.g., character animation), $2,500+ for advanced (e.g., 3D integration).
  13. Charge $100?$300/hour for motion design consulting or troubleshooting, with a 2-hour minimum.
  14. For motion design for live events or presentations, quote $1,000?$3,000 depending on duration, custom assets, and on-site support needed.
  15. Offer a package deal for new clients: 3 motion videos for $2,500 (saving them 20% vs. individual pricing).
  16. Use a 1.5x multiplier on your hourly rate for client-requested revisions after the initial draft to cover extra time.
  17. Charge $75?$200 per revision round for motion projects, with 2?3 rounds included in the base fee and additional rounds billed separately.
  18. For motion design in e-learning platforms, quote $800?$2,500 per module, depending on interactivity and animation complexity.
  19. Set a kill fee of 40%?60% of the total project cost if the client cancels after you?ve started work and provided assets.
  20. For motion design in ads, charge a percentage of the ad spend: 5%?10% for budgets under $10K, 3%?5% for budgets over $10K.
  21. Offer a subscription model for small businesses: $999/month for unlimited simple motion graphics (e.g., social posts, GIFs, short explainers).
  22. Charge $300?$1,000 for custom lower-thirds or title animations, depending on style complexity and animation length.
  23. For motion design in explainer videos, quote $2,000?$8,000 per video, with a breakdown of $1,000 for script/storyboard, $1,000 for voiceover, and $1,000?$6,000 for animation.
  24. Use a 10%?15% project fee for motion design work that requires 3D modeling or advanced rigging, on top of the base animation cost.
  25. For motion design in app onboarding, quote $1,500?$4,000 per sequence, depending on the number of screens and animation style.
  26. Charge $200?$500 for custom typography animations or kinetic text projects, priced per 10 seconds of animation.
  27. For motion design in product demos, quote $2,500?$7,000 per demo, with additional fees for interactive elements or 3D product models.
  28. Set a 5%?10% late fee for invoices paid after 30 days to encourage timely payments and improve cash flow.
  29. Offer a 10% discount for clients who pay 50% upfront and 50% upon delivery, incentivizing upfront commitment and reducing risk.
  30. For motion design in social media stories, quote $100?$400 per story segment, with discounts for bulk purchases (e.g., 10 stories for $2,000).
