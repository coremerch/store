Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief Generator: 40 High-Converting Prompts for Clear, Actionable & Sellable Design Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the name of your brand, and does it have any existing trademark or domain conflicts?
  2. Describe the core mission of your brand in one sentence?what problem does it solve or value does it deliver?
  3. List 3 adjectives that best describe the personality of your brand (e.g., bold, minimalist, playful).
  4. Who is your ideal customer? Include age range, lifestyle, and values they associate with your brand.
  5. What are the top 3 competitors you admire, and what visual or tonal elements do you want to emulate or avoid?
  6. Describe the primary emotion you want customers to feel when they encounter your brand for the first time.
  7. What is the main color palette you currently use (if any)? Provide hex codes or Pantone references if available.
  8. What type of typography style resonates with your brand?serif, sans-serif, script, or display? Provide examples.
  9. Are there any specific cultural or geographic influences that should shape the visual identity (e.g., Scandinavian minimalism, Japanese elegance)?
  10. Do you have existing brand assets (logo, fonts, imagery) that must be incorporated or updated? Upload samples if possible.
  11. What is the primary medium where your brand identity will be used most frequently (print, digital, packaging, signage)?
  12. Describe the tone of voice for your brand communications?professional, friendly, humorous, authoritative?
  13. List 3 keywords that encapsulate the brand?s essence (e.g., trustworthy, innovative, luxurious).
  14. What is the tagline or slogan your brand uses or plans to use? Provide current or draft versions.
  15. Describe any mandatory design elements that must appear in every brand application (e.g., specific icon style, pattern, or layout grid).
  16. What is the budget range for this branding project, including design, revisions, and final deliverables?
  17. How many rounds of revisions are included in your service package, and how do you prefer feedback to be delivered (written, visual, recorded)?
  18. Are there any industry-specific regulations or standards (e.g., FDA labeling, legal disclaimers) that must be reflected in the design?
  19. What is the timeline for this project? Include key milestones (research, sketches, drafts, final delivery).
  20. Do you have a preferred style guide or framework you want the identity to follow (e.g., Google?s Material Design, Apple?s Human Interface Guidelines)?
  21. List any fonts you dislike or want to avoid in the final identity design.
  22. Describe the logo style you envision?wordmark, monogram, emblem, abstract mark, or mascot?
  23. What is the aspect ratio or proportions required for your logo (square, circular, rectangular)? Provide examples if possible.
  24. Are there any historical or legacy branding elements that should be preserved or modernized?
  25. What is the primary demographic that will interact with your brand offline (e.g., retail customers, B2B clients)?
  26. Do you have a mood board or visual references that inspire you? Share images or describe styles and themes.
  27. What is the primary call-to-action associated with your brand (e.g., ?Shop Now,? ?Book a Consultation?)?
  28. Describe any specific symbols, motifs, or imagery that must be integrated into the identity (e.g., nature, technology, heritage).
  29. What is the file format preference for final logo deliverables (vector, PNG, SVG, PDF)?
  30. Are there any color accessibility requirements (e.g., WCAG compliance for digital or print use)?
  31. List 3 brands whose visual identity you admire and explain what specifically resonates with you.
  32. What is the primary use case for the new identity?launching a new product, rebranding, or refreshing an existing look?
  33. Describe any specific textures, gradients, or special effects you want to explore in the design.
  34. What is the expected lifespan of this branding project before a potential refresh is needed?
  35. Are there any social media platforms where the brand identity will be prominently featured? List them.
  36. Do you have a preferred designer or agency you?d like to collaborate with, or are you open to new talent?
  37. What is the primary message or story you want your brand identity to communicate to your audience?
  38. List any specific design tools or software you prefer the designer to use (e.g., Adobe Illustrator, Figma, Procreate).
  39. What is the primary KPI or success metric for this branding project (e.g., increased recognition, higher engagement, conversion rate)?
