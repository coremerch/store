Brand Identity Design Client Brief Pack: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Attract Ideal Clients & Streamline Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. List 5 adjectives that best describe the brand?s personality and tone of voice.
  2. Describe the brand?s mission in one sentence.
  3. Identify the top 3 competitors and explain why the client?s brand stands out from them.
  4. Specify the exact industry or niche the brand operates in (e.g., sustainable fashion, fintech SaaS, artisanal coffee).
  5. Provide 3-5 visual references (e.g., logos, color palettes, typography) that the client loves and wants to emulate.
  6. Define the primary target audience demographics (age, gender, location, income level, etc.).
  7. Outline the brand?s core values in a bulleted list.
  8. Clarify whether the brand has an existing logo or visual identity that needs to be refreshed or is starting from scratch.
  9. State the desired emotional response the brand aims to evoke in its audience (e.g., trust, excitement, luxury, nostalgia).
  10. List any specific cultural or regional influences that should be incorporated into the design.
  11. Specify the primary use cases for the brand identity (e.g., website, packaging, social media, print collateral).
  12. Provide the client?s preferred color palette or any colors they want to avoid.
  13. Describe the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) in 25 words or less.
  14. Indicate whether the brand identity needs to be scalable for future expansion (e.g., new product lines, international markets).
  15. List any existing brand assets (e.g., fonts, imagery style, taglines) that must be incorporated or avoided.
  16. Define the brand?s tone of voice for written content (e.g., professional, casual, humorous, authoritative).
  17. State the client?s budget range for the brand identity project (e.g., $1,000?$5,000, $10,000+).
  18. Specify the deadline for the project and key milestones (e.g., rough drafts, revisions, final delivery).
  19. List any specific design styles the client is drawn to (e.g., minimalist, retro, futuristic, hand-drawn).
  20. Clarify whether the brand identity should include a mascot or illustrated elements.
  21. Provide examples of brands the client admires (even if outside their industry) for aesthetic or conceptual inspiration.
  22. State the primary medium where the brand will be most visible (e.g., digital, print, outdoor, packaging).
  23. List any industry-specific symbols, icons, or motifs that should or should not be included in the design.
  24. Define the brand?s personality traits (e.g., bold, friendly, mysterious, sophisticated) in a single paragraph.
  25. Specify whether the brand identity needs to be gender-neutral or tailored to a specific gender.
  26. List any legal or trademark considerations that must be accounted for in the design (e.g., avoid certain symbols, check existing trademarks).
  27. Clarify the client?s preferred typography style (e.g., serif, sans-serif, script, custom lettering).
  28. Provide the client?s company name, tagline, and any other branding elements they want to highlight.
  29. List any specific cultural or social sensitivities that the design must respect or avoid.
  30. Specify whether the brand identity will include a secondary color palette for different use cases (e.g., gradients, monochrome).
  31. State the client?s preferred file formats for the final deliverables (e.g., AI, EPS, PNG, SVG).
  32. Define the brand?s key messaging pillars (e.g., sustainability, innovation, community, quality).
  33. List any specific imagery styles the client wants (e.g., photography, illustration, abstract, flat design).
  34. Clarify whether the brand identity needs to include a brand book or style guide for future use by the client?s team.
  35. Specify the client?s preferred layout or grid system for applications like business cards or social media posts.
  36. Provide examples of past design projects the client has worked on or admires for reference.
  37. Define the brand?s desired perception in the market (e.g., premium, accessible, cutting-edge, trustworthy).
  38. List any specific design trends the client wants to incorporate or avoid (e.g., glassmorphism, neon, vintage).
  39. Clarify whether the brand identity will include motion graphics or animated elements for digital use.
  40. State the client?s preferred approach to typography hierarchy (e.g., bold headlines, elegant body text).
