Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Secure Clear, Actionable Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. List the three primary business goals your client wants to achieve with this new brand identity.
  2. Describe the target audience in three detailed personas, including demographics, psychographics, and pain points.
  3. Provide the top five adjectives that should describe the brand?s personality in one word each.
  4. Share the brand?s current visual identity assets (logo, color palette, typography, imagery style) and explain which elements need updating.
  5. Specify the industry competitors and identify two brands your client admires and two they want to differentiate from.
  6. Outline the preferred communication tone (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist) with three example sentences in that tone.
  7. Define the primary and secondary target markets with their geographic locations and cultural nuances.
  8. Provide the client?s mission statement and explain how the brand identity should reflect or enhance it.
  9. List any existing brand guidelines or style guides that must be followed or updated.
  10. Specify the budget range for the brand identity project to align expectations early.
  11. Identify the key stakeholders who will approve the final designs and outline their roles in the approval process.
  12. Provide the timeline for the project, including key milestones (e.g., research, drafts, revisions, final delivery).
  13. Describe the client?s long-term vision for the brand (next 3?5 years) and how the identity should support that growth.
  14. Share any cultural, ethical, or legal considerations that must be reflected in the brand identity (e.g., accessibility, inclusivity).
  15. Provide the client?s top three brand values and explain how these should be visually or verbally communicated.
  16. Specify the formats in which the final brand identity will be used (e.g., digital, print, packaging, signage) and their relative importance.
  17. List any existing brand assets that cannot be changed or must be retained (e.g., a signature color, a founder?s signature).
  18. Describe the client?s ideal impression of their brand when customers first encounter it (e.g., trustworthy, innovative, luxurious).
  19. Provide examples of past branding projects the client has loved or disliked, including reasons why.
  20. Specify the preferred color palette direction (e.g., vibrant, muted, monochromatic) with five color codes or references.
  21. List the typography preferences (e.g., modern sans-serif, classic serif) with three font examples the client likes or dislikes.
  22. Describe the mood or vibe the brand should evoke, using three adjectives and one reference image or mood board.
  23. Provide the client?s social media handles and explain how the new identity should align with their online presence.
  24. Specify any industry-specific design trends the client wants to incorporate or avoid.
  25. List the deliverables required (e.g., logo variations, color palette, typography system, brand guidelines) and their priority order.
  26. Describe the client?s ideal logo style (e.g., wordmark, emblem, abstract) with three examples they like or dislike.
  27. Provide the client?s website URL and any existing visual assets (e.g., photography, illustrations) that must be considered.
  28. Specify the preferred icon style for the brand (e.g., line art, isometric, flat) with three examples the client likes or dislikes.
  29. List any existing taglines or slogans that must be incorporated into the identity or avoided.
  30. Describe the client?s ideal customer journey and how the brand identity should influence each touchpoint.
  31. Provide the client?s social media content style (e.g., formal, casual, humorous) and how it should reflect the new identity.
  32. Specify the preferred imagery style (e.g., candid photography, illustrated, stock photos) with three examples the client likes or dislikes.
  33. List any existing marketing materials (e.g., brochures, business cards) that need to be updated to align with the new identity.
  34. Describe the client?s ideal brand recognition elements (e.g., a mascot, a specific font, a color scheme) and why they matter.
  35. Provide the client?s archived branding materials (if any) and explain which elements should be preserved or discarded.
  36. Specify the preferred layout and composition style for the brand (e.g., grid-based, asymmetrical, modular) with three examples.
  37. List any industry-specific symbols or motifs the client wants to incorporate into the identity.
  38. Describe the client?s ideal brand evolution (e.g., subtle refresh vs. complete overhaul) and their comfort level with risk.
  39. Provide the client?s feedback preferences (e.g., written comments, live sessions, digital annotations) for revisions.
  40. Specify the preferred file formats for deliverables (e.g., AI, EPS, PNG, PDF) and their intended use (print vs. digital).
