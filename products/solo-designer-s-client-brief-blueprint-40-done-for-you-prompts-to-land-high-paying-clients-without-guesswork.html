Solo Designer?s Client Brief Blueprint: 40 Done-For-You Prompts to Land High-Paying Clients Without Guesswork

client brief prompts for agency escapees going solo ? 40 prompts.

  1. List the three biggest frustrations your ideal client faces when hiring designers (e.g., unclear timelines, scope creep, unmet expectations).
  2. Describe the exact job title your ideal client holds (e.g., Head of Marketing at a SaaS startup with 50-200 employees).
  3. Write the one sentence your ideal client would use to describe their company?s brand personality in a client brief.
  4. State the primary goal your ideal client wants to achieve with this project in one verb-noun phrase (e.g., increase lead conversions).
  5. Name the top three competitors your ideal client admires and why (e.g., Brand X?s website has a seamless mobile experience).
  6. Specify the primary deliverable your ideal client expects (e.g., a fully responsive landing page with three key sections).
  7. List the key performance indicators (KPIs) your ideal client will use to measure success (e.g., 20% increase in form submissions).
  8. Describe the current visual style or aesthetic your ideal client?s brand currently uses (e.g., minimalist, bold colors, geometric shapes).
  9. State the tone of voice your ideal client prefers for written content (e.g., professional yet approachable, conversational).
  10. Write the exact budget range your ideal client typically allocates for a project like this (e.g., $5,000?$15,000).
  11. List the three most important non-negotiables for your ideal client (e.g., must be mobile-first, must include animations).
  12. Describe the primary communication method your ideal client prefers (e.g., Slack for daily updates, email for formal reviews).
  13. State the deadline your ideal client expects for project completion (e.g., within 6 weeks of contract signing).
  14. List the stakeholders who must approve the final design and their roles (e.g., Marketing Director, CEO, Product Manager).
  15. Describe the current tools or platforms your ideal client uses for design collaboration (e.g., Figma, InVision, Trello).
  16. Write the exact file formats your ideal client needs for final delivery (e.g., Sketch files, PDF, optimized PNGs).
  17. State the preferred method for receiving feedback (e.g., inline comments in Figma, structured written feedback via email).
  18. List the top three pain points your ideal client has experienced with previous designers (e.g., missed deadlines, poor communication).
  19. Describe the primary audience your ideal client is targeting with this project (e.g., millennial professionals aged 25-34).
  20. Write the exact color palette your ideal client?s brand currently uses (e.g., #2A5CAA, #FFFFFF, #F7F7F7).
  21. State the primary font families your ideal client?s brand uses (e.g., Inter for headings, Open Sans for body text).
  22. List the key sections your ideal client wants included in the final design (e.g., hero banner, features grid, testimonials, FAQ).
  23. Describe the primary call-to-action (CTA) your ideal client wants to emphasize (e.g., ?Sign up for a free trial? in a bright button).
  24. Write the exact number of design revisions your ideal client typically requests (e.g., up to 3 rounds of revisions).
  25. State the preferred method for project handoff (e.g., Zeplin for developer handoff, PDF for stakeholder review).
  26. List the top three industry trends your ideal client wants to incorporate (e.g., dark mode, micro-interactions, accessibility compliance).
  27. Describe the primary imagery style your ideal client prefers (e.g., authentic photography, illustrated graphics, abstract shapes).
  28. Write the exact copy length requirements for key sections (e.g., hero headline: 6 words max, feature descriptions: 50 words max).
  29. State the preferred method for tracking project progress (e.g., weekly video calls, shared Trello board).
  30. List the top three tools or integrations your ideal client uses daily (e.g., HubSpot, Google Analytics, Slack).
  31. Describe the primary user journey your ideal client wants to optimize (e.g., from landing page to checkout in 3 steps).
  32. Write the exact number of pages or screens your ideal client expects for this project (e.g., 5-page website with mobile and desktop versions).
  33. State the preferred method for testing the final design (e.g., user testing with 10 participants, A/B testing via Google Optimize).
  34. List the top three content sources your ideal client wants to include (e.g., testimonials, case studies, blog excerpts).
  35. Describe the primary emotional response your ideal client wants the design to evoke (e.g., trust, excitement, curiosity).
  36. Write the exact social media platforms your ideal client will use to promote the final design (e.g., LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter).
  37. State the preferred method for archiving or storing project files post-delivery (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, company server).
  38. List the top three legal or compliance requirements your ideal client must adhere to (e.g., GDPR, ADA compliance).
  39. Describe the primary backup plan your ideal client has if the project faces unforeseen delays (e.g., extended deadline, phased rollout).
  40. Write the exact language your ideal client prefers for all project-related communications (e.g., English, bilingual English/Spanish).
