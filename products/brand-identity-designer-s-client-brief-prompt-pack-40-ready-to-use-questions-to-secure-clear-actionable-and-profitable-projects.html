Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Prompt Pack: 40 Ready-to-Use Questions to Secure Clear, Actionable, and Profitable Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is your company?s mission statement in one sentence? Provide the exact wording.
  2. Describe your brand personality in three adjectives. Give examples of brands that embody these traits.
  3. Who is your ideal customer? Include demographics like age, location, and income level.
  4. What are the top three values your brand stands for? Provide real-world examples of how these values are demonstrated.
  5. What inspired you to start this business or rebrand? Share the story behind it.
  6. What are the biggest challenges your brand currently faces? List them in order of priority.
  7. What colors do you currently associate with your brand? Provide hex codes or Pantone numbers if available.
  8. Describe your brand?s tone of voice. Should it be formal, friendly, authoritative, or playful? Provide sample sentences.
  9. Who are your top three competitors? Share their websites or social media handles for reference.
  10. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)? Explain how it differs from competitors.
  11. Do you have existing brand guidelines? If yes, attach them or summarize key points.
  12. What is your brand?s current tagline or slogan? If none, draft three options based on your brand?s essence.
  13. What emotions do you want your brand to evoke in customers? Provide examples of brands that achieve this.
  14. What type of logo style do you prefer? Minimalist, illustrative, vintage, modern, handcrafted, etc. Provide examples.
  15. Do you have any specific fonts or typography styles you like or dislike? Share examples or names.
  16. What is your budget range for this project? Be specific about design, implementation, and revisions.
  17. What is your expected timeline for this project? Include key milestones like research, design, and approvals.
  18. Do you have any existing brand assets (logos, color schemes, fonts) that must be incorporated? Describe or attach.
  19. What is your brand?s current market position? Are you a leader, challenger, or niche player?
  20. Have you worked with designers before? If so, what was the experience like? What worked or didn?t work?
  21. What is your brand?s primary communication channel? Website, social media, print, packaging, etc.
  22. Do you have any visual references (mood boards, Pinterest links, competitor materials) that inspire you?
  23. What is your brand?s story? Summarize it in 100 words or less.
  24. Are there any brands you admire for their identity? Share their websites or social media links.
  25. What is your brand?s current market share or customer base size? Provide numbers if possible.
  26. Do you have a brand mascot or character? If yes, describe its role and personality.
  27. What is your brand?s current visual identity lacking? Be specific about what needs improvement.
  28. Do you have a brand book or style guide? If yes, attach it or summarize its contents.
  29. What is your brand?s current social media presence like? Share handles and engagement metrics if available.
  30. Are there any cultural or regional considerations for your brand? Describe any relevant traditions or norms.
  31. What is your brand?s current tagline or slogan? If none, what message do you want to convey through it?
  32. Do you have any legal or trademark considerations for your brand?s name or logo? Share details.
  33. What is your brand?s current customer feedback or testimonials? Provide quotes or links.
  34. Are there any industry-specific design trends or standards your brand should adhere to?
  35. What is your brand?s current visual consistency across different platforms? Highlight any inconsistencies.
  36. Do you have a preferred style for your brand?s imagery? Realistic, abstract, minimalist, etc. Provide examples.
  37. What is your brand?s current customer journey? Describe the touchpoints where design plays a role.
  38. Are there any specific design elements you want to avoid? List them clearly.
  39. What is your brand?s current tone on social media? Provide examples of posts that reflect this tone.
  40. Do you have a brand?s existing voice or messaging guidelines? If yes, summarize or attach them.
