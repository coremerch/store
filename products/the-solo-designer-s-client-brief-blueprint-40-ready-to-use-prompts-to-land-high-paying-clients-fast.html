The Solo Designer?s Client Brief Blueprint: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Land High-Paying Clients Fast

client brief prompts for agency escapees going solo ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the client?s core business, and how does it make money? Ask for their revenue model, pricing strategy, and customer acquisition channels.
  2. What are the client?s top 3 business goals for the next 12 months? Force specificity?e.g., 'Increase online sales by 30%' or 'Reduce customer churn by 15%'.
  3. Who is the client?s ideal customer? Request demographic details, pain points, and buying behaviors.
  4. What does the client consider their biggest competitive advantage? Ask for proof?e.g., patents, exclusive partnerships, or proprietary data.
  5. What is the client?s current brand perception? Request recent customer reviews, social media sentiment, and direct competitor comparisons.
  6. What are the client?s top 3 brand values? Ensure they align with the designer?s own ethical or aesthetic standards.
  7. What is the client?s budget range for this project, broken down by phase (research, design, development, revisions)?
  8. What is the client?s timeline for launch or implementation? Include soft and hard deadlines for each milestone.
  9. Who are the key stakeholders in the project, and what are their roles? Request org charts or decision-making hierarchies.
  10. What is the client?s preferred communication style? Ask for their communication frequency, tools (e.g., Slack, email), and response-time expectations.
  11. What is the client?s tolerance for risk? Ask if they prefer bold, experimental designs or tried-and-true solutions.
  12. Does the client have a style guide or brand book? Request a copy to ensure design cohesion.
  13. What are the client?s top 3 favorite brands or designs, and why? Use these as reference points for inspiration.
  14. What are the client?s least favorite designs they?ve worked with? Avoid these pitfalls in the final deliverable.
  15. What is the client?s process for approving designs? Ask who has final sign-off and how feedback is structured (e.g., written vs. verbal).
  16. Does the client have a preference for specific design tools or platforms (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD, Webflow)?
  17. What is the client?s stance on accessibility and inclusivity in design? Request WCAG compliance requirements if applicable.
  18. Does the client require responsive design for multiple devices (desktop, mobile, tablet)? Specify breakpoints.
  19. What is the client?s preferred file format for deliverables (e.g., PDF, AI, Sketch)?
  20. Are there any third-party integrations or APIs that the design must accommodate? Request documentation or developer contacts.
  21. What is the client?s stance on stock imagery vs. custom photography or illustrations? Request examples of preferred styles.
  22. Does the client have a content management system (CMS) requirement? Ask if they need templates or modules designed.
  23. What is the client?s SEO strategy? Ensure design elements (e.g., meta tags, alt text) align with their goals.
  24. Does the client have a social media or email marketing strategy that the design must support? Request campaign calendars or templates.
  25. What is the client?s stance on motion graphics or micro-interactions? Request examples of preferred styles or tools (e.g., Lottie, After Effects).
  26. Does the client require a design system (e.g., components, tokens) for scalability? Ask for examples of existing systems they admire.
  27. What is the client?s stance on dark mode or alternative color schemes? Request accessibility testing requirements.
  28. Does the client have a preference for minimalist, maximalist, or retro design aesthetics? Request mood boards or Pinterest boards for reference.
  29. What is the client?s budget for additional rounds of revisions beyond the initial scope? Specify number of revisions and fees.
  30. Does the client require a handoff package for developers (e.g., Zeplin, Storybook, or annotated Figma files)?
  31. What is the client?s stance on sustainability in design? Ask about eco-friendly materials, printing processes, or digital carbon footprint.
  32. Does the client have a preference for hand-drawn, digital, or mixed-media illustrations? Request examples of past work.
  33. What is the client?s stance on user testing and data-driven design decisions? Request access to analytics tools or user research reports.
  34. Does the client require a project timeline with Gantt charts or Kanban boards? Request their preferred project management tool (e.g., Trello, Asana).
  35. What is the client?s stance on AI-generated content or tools in the design process? Ask if they require human-led creativity only.
  36. Does the client have a preference for serif, sans-serif, or custom typography? Request font licenses or usage rights.
  37. What is the client?s stance on social responsibility in design (e.g., representation, ethical messaging)? Request examples of campaigns they admire.
  38. Does the client require a post-launch strategy (e.g., analytics review, A/B testing, or iterative improvements)?
  39. What is the client?s stance on client education or training for new design systems or tools? Request a list of training materials they provide.
  40. Does the client have a preference for print or digital-first design? Specify deliverables for each medium (e.g., business cards, social media templates).
