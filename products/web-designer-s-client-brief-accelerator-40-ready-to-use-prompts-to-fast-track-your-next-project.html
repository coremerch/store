Web Designer?s Client Brief Accelerator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Fast-Track Your Next Project

client brief prompts for web designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the primary business goal of this website? (e.g., generate leads, sell products, build brand awareness)
  2. Describe the ideal user persona for this project in 3 sentences or less.
  3. List the top 3 competitors your client wants to outperform in design and functionality.
  4. What is the expected timeline for this project from kickoff to launch?
  5. Who are the key stakeholders involved in decision-making for this project?
  6. What is the budget range for this project? (e.g., $2,000?$5,000, $10,000?$20,000)
  7. Does the client already have a style guide or branding guidelines we must follow?
  8. What are the client?s top 3 must-have features for the website?
  9. Are there any existing websites or designs the client likes or dislikes? Provide links.
  10. What is the client?s preferred content management system (CMS)? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Shopify)
  11. Does the client require the website to be mobile-first, desktop-first, or responsive?
  12. What is the client?s target audience?s age range and tech-savviness level?
  13. Will the website need multilingual support? If yes, which languages?
  14. Does the client require e-commerce functionality? If yes, what types of products/services will be sold?
  15. What is the client?s preferred color palette for the website? Provide hex codes if available.
  16. Are there any specific fonts or typography styles the client wants to use?
  17. Does the client have a preference for minimalist, maximalist, or modern design aesthetics?
  18. What is the client?s stance on animations, micro-interactions, or parallax effects?
  19. Are there any accessibility standards the client requires compliance with? (e.g., WCAG 2.1 AA)
  20. Does the client need integration with third-party tools? (e.g., CRM, email marketing, analytics)
  21. What is the client?s preferred layout structure for the homepage? (e.g., hero section, grid, full-width)
  22. Will the website include a blog or news section? If yes, how often will content be updated?
  23. Does the client require a membership or login system for users?
  24. Are there any specific legal or compliance requirements for the website? (e.g., GDPR, cookie consent)
  25. What is the client?s preferred tone of voice for the website copy? (e.g., professional, casual, humorous)
  26. Does the client have a preferred hosting provider or server requirements?
  27. Will the website need a membership or subscription model? If yes, describe the model.
  28. Are there any specific security measures the client requires? (e.g., SSL, two-factor authentication)
  29. Does the client need a content strategy or SEO plan included in the project?
  30. What is the client?s preferred method of communication for updates? (e.g., Slack, email, weekly calls)
  31. Are there any specific performance benchmarks the client expects? (e.g., page load speed under 2 seconds)
  32. Does the client require a maintenance or support plan post-launch? If yes, describe the scope.
  33. What is the client?s preferred method for collecting user feedback post-launch?
  34. Are there any specific animations or transitions the client wants to include?
  35. Does the client have a preferred call-to-action (CTA) style for buttons and links?
  36. What is the client?s stance on dark mode or theme switching functionality?
  37. Are there any specific branding elements that must be prominently featured? (e.g., logo, tagline)
  38. Does the client require a sitemap or wireframe approval before design begins?
  39. What is the client?s preferred method for handling revisions during the design phase?
