Web Designer's Client Brief Accelerator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Secure Clear, Actionable Project Requirements in Minutes

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of this website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio)
  2. Who is the ideal user persona for this website? Describe their demographics, behaviors, and pain points.
  3. List the top 3 competitors in your industry. What specific features or designs do you admire in their websites?
  4. What is your brand?s color palette? Provide hex codes for each color.
  5. What is your brand?s typography preference? List the font families and where they should be used (headings, body text, etc.).
  6. Do you have existing brand guidelines or a style guide we should follow? If yes, provide the document or key points.
  7. What is the project timeline? Include key milestones like design drafts, revisions, and launch date.
  8. How many rounds of revisions are included in the project scope?
  9. What is your preferred communication method for feedback and updates? (e.g., email, Slack, project management tool)
  10. Who are the key stakeholders involved in this project? Provide their names, roles, and decision-making authority.
  11. What is the primary call-to-action (CTA) for this website? (e.g., 'Buy Now,' 'Sign Up,' 'Contact Us')
  12. Where will the website be hosted? Do you already have a domain and hosting provider?
  13. Do you have any existing content (text, images, videos) that must be included on the website? If yes, provide access or files.
  14. What is the preferred content management system (CMS)? (e.g., WordPress, Squarespace, Webflow, custom)
  15. Will the website require a blog or news section? If yes, describe the structure and frequency of updates.
  16. Do you need e-commerce functionality? If yes, list the products, payment gateways, and shipping options.
  17. What is the desired website structure? Provide a sitemap or list of pages (e.g., Home, About, Services, Contact).
  18. Do you have any specific design inspirations or examples of websites you like? Provide links or screenshots.
  19. What is the preferred layout style? (e.g., minimalist, modern, corporate, playful)
  20. Do you want a mobile-first or desktop-first design approach?
  21. What is the target audience?s primary device for accessing the website? (e.g., mobile, tablet, desktop)
  22. Will the website need a mobile app or responsive design for mobile users?
  23. Do you have any accessibility requirements? (e.g., WCAG compliance, screen reader compatibility)
  24. What is the budget range for this project?
  25. Are there any third-party integrations required? (e.g., CRM, email marketing, social media, analytics)
  26. Do you need a custom 404 error page designed?
  27. Will the website include a newsletter signup or email capture form?
  28. What is the tone of voice for the website copy? (e.g., professional, friendly, technical, humorous)
  29. Do you have a preferred navigation style? (e.g., horizontal menu, hamburger menu, sticky navigation)
  30. Will the website require a login or user account system?
  31. Do you need a contact form? If yes, list the fields required (e.g., name, email, message, phone number).
  32. What is the expected page load speed? Provide any benchmarks or tools you use to measure performance.
  33. Do you have any SEO requirements or preferences for on-page optimization?
  34. Will the website need a search functionality? If yes, describe the expected scope.
  35. Do you want any animations or micro-interactions included in the design?
  36. What is the preferred image style? (e.g., illustrations, photography, icons, abstract)
  37. Do you need a favicon designed for the website?
  38. Will the website include any video content? If yes, provide details on hosting and embedding preferences.
  39. Do you have any legal requirements for the website? (e.g., privacy policy, cookie consent, GDPR compliance)
  40. What is the process for approving final designs and content before launch?
