7-Figure UI/UX Proposal Swipe File: 40 Ready-to-Paste Client Winning Templates & Case Studies

proposal swipe file for ui and ux designers ? 40 lines.

  1. Headline: 'We don?t just design interfaces?we design conversions.' Use this as the first line of every proposal to immediately position your work as results-driven.
  2. Subheadline: 'Increase user engagement by 40% with a data-backed UI overhaul.' Include this statistic in proposals for SaaS or e-commerce clients to establish credibility.

  6. Deliverables List: 'Wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, micro-interactions, and a design system.' Specify exactly what the client receives to set clear expectations.
  7. Timeline Breakdown: 'Week 1: Discovery & Research, Week 2: Wireframing, Week 3: Visual Design, Week 4: Handoff.' Provide a Gantt-style breakdown to show professionalism.
  8. Pricing Structure: 'Fixed-price project: $5,000 for wireframes + $7,500 for full UI design.' Include tiered pricing to cater to different budgets.
  9. Testimonial: 'Their UX research saved us $50K in development costs.' Add a 1-sentence testimonial from a past client to build social proof.
  10. Client Quote: 'We needed a designer who understood both aesthetics and user psychology.' Include this in proposals to appeal to clients who value strategy over just visuals.
  11. Before & After Comparison: 'Old: 12-step checkout | New: 3-step checkout with progress bar.' Use visual examples to demonstrate design impact.

  18. Risk Mitigation: 'We?ll conduct usability testing at the mid-point to catch issues early.' Address potential risks to build client confidence.
