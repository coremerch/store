Ultimate Lettering Project Handover Checklist: Client-Ready Files, Smooth Delivery & Zero Headaches

handover checklist for lettering artists ? 24 steps.

  1. Verify all original lettering designs are saved in vector format (.ai, .eps, .svg).
  2. Ensure each lettering piece is on a separate layer in the source file.
  3. Organize layers by color, style, or component (e.g., ?Main Lettering,? ?Shadow,? ?Background?).
  4. Check that all paths are closed and free of overlapping nodes to prevent rendering issues.
  5. Remove any unused or hidden layers, brushes, or styles from the file.
  6. Export a high-resolution PNG (300+ DPI) of the final lettering for quick client review.
  7. Prepare a web-optimized JPG (72 DPI, max 2MB) for client sharing or social media previews.
  8. Include a PDF version with embedded fonts for cross-platform compatibility and print-ready checks.
  9. Provide a transparent PNG background option for easy overlay on different backgrounds.
  10. Create a style guide with font names, sizes, colors (HEX/RGB/CMYK), and spacing (kerning, leading).
  11. List all custom brushes or textures used in the project and their sources (with licenses if applicable).
  12. Include a readme.txt or PDF with step-by-step instructions for opening/editing the files in Adobe Illustrator/Photoshop.
  13. Provide a version with expanded outlines for clients who need editable text without fonts.
  14. Deliver a CMYK color-proof file for print clients to preview ink usage and color accuracy.
  15. Include a RGB color-proof for digital/screen use with safe web colors highlighted.
  16. Add a ?Before & After? comparison image showing the evolution of the lettering design.
  17. Provide a contact sheet of alternate compositions or variations (e.g., different layouts, colors).
  18. Include a list of recommended fonts (if client wants to recreate similar lettering) with links to download.
  19. Provide a short tutorial video (5-10 min) demonstrating how to use the files or replicate the style.
  20. Send a final invoice with a breakdown of deliverables, revisions, and additional services (e.g., social media kits).
  21. Include a client feedback form (Google Form or PDF) to gather input on the handover process and satisfaction.
  22. Deliver all files in a single zipped folder labeled with the project name and date (e.g., ?ClientName_Lettering_Final_20240515.zip?).
  23. Send a follow-up email within 24 hours with a download link, instructions, and a thank-you note for their business.
  24. Schedule a 15-minute call (if client opts in) to walk through the files and answer any questions about usage.
