Ultimate Lettering Project Handover Checklist: Ensure Flawless Delivery & Happy Clients Every Time

handover checklist for lettering artists ? 25 steps.

  1. Verify all client-provided reference materials and fonts are correctly licensed and legally used in the project.
  2. Confirm the final color palette matches client?s brand guidelines and is saved in Pantone, RGB, CMYK, and Hex formats.
  3. Export all lettering assets in multiple file formats (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG) with transparent backgrounds where applicable.
  4. Organize files into a clear folder structure (e.g., 'Final Assets', 'Source Files', 'Extras').
  5. Provide a PDF preview of the final lettering design at 100% and 50% scale for client approval.
  6. Include a style guide documenting font names, sizes, spacing (tracking/leading), and any custom adjustments made.
  7. Specify exact dimensions for each deliverable (e.g., social media posts, signage, app icons) in pixels or inches.
  8. Ensure all vectors are fully scalable and free of stray anchor points or hidden layers.
  9. Deliver a readme.txt file with step-by-step instructions for installing custom fonts or using editable files.
  10. Provide a list of all software versions used (e.g., Illustrator CC 2023) to ensure compatibility.
  11. Include a mockup showcase of the lettering in real-world scenarios (e.g., business cards, banners, merchandise).
  12. Double-check kerning pairs for consistency and readability, especially in critical phrases or slogans.
  13. Remove all unused swatches, styles, and brushes from source files to keep them clean and client-friendly.
  14. Add a watermark-free preview file for client approval before final delivery.
  15. Document any special techniques used (e.g., hand-drawn modifications, texture overlays) in a brief technical notes file.
  16. Ensure all linked images or embedded elements in the design are also provided in their original quality.
  17. Provide a separate file with alternate colorways (monochrome, gradient, or brand-compliant variations).
  18. Include a contact sheet with high-resolution thumbnails of all final assets for quick reference.
  19. Verify that all text layers are either expanded or outlined to prevent font substitution issues.
  20. Deliver a timeline breakdown of the project phases (concept, drafts, revisions, final) with dates for accountability.
  21. Include a client feedback form template to streamline the revision process for future projects.
  22. Provide instructions for resizing or adapting the lettering for different mediums (e.g., embroidery, engraving).
  23. Send a final invoice with a clear breakdown of hours worked, deliverables, and payment terms for transparency.
  24. Offer a 30-day post-delivery support window for minor tweaks or clarifications at no extra cost.
  25. Include a thank-you note or small bonus asset (e.g., alternate glyphs, decorative flourishes) to exceed client expectations.
