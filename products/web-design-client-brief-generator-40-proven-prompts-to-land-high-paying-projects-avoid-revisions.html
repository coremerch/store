Web Design Client Brief Generator: 40 Proven Prompts to Land High-Paying Projects & Avoid Revisions

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. Describe your business in one sentence. What is the one thing you want visitors to do after landing on your site?
  2. List the top 3 competitors in your industry. What colors, fonts, and layouts do they use on their homepage?
  3. Who is your ideal customer persona? Include demographics (age, location, income) and psychographics (interests, pain points).
  4. What are the 3 biggest challenges your business is facing right now? How could a new website help solve them?
  5. Provide 3 examples of websites you admire. Explain what specifically you like about each (layout, animations, functionality).
  6. Do you have a brand style guide? If yes, attach it. If no, describe your preferred color palette (primary and secondary colors).
  7. What type of imagery do you want to use on your site? (e.g., illustrations, photos, 3D renders, stock images). Provide 3 examples.
  8. List the 5 most important pages your website must have. Rank them by priority.
  9. Do you need e-commerce functionality? If yes, describe your product catalog size, payment gateways, and shipping integrations.
  10. What is your budget range for this project? Include separate amounts for design, development, and ongoing maintenance.
  11. What is your timeline for launching the website? Include key milestones (e.g., first draft, revisions, final delivery).
  12. Do you have a preferred content management system (CMS)? If yes, which one (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)? If no, do you want recommendations?
  13. Describe your ideal website navigation structure. How many main menu items do you want? What pages should they link to?
  14. Do you need a blog section? If yes, how often do you plan to publish new content?
  15. What are the 3 most valuable features your website must have? (e.g., live chat, booking system, membership portal).
  16. List any existing website URLs you want to reference for inspiration or migration purposes.
  17. Do you have a domain name and hosting provider? If yes, provide login details. If no, do you need recommendations?
  18. What is your preferred tone for the website copy? (e.g., professional, friendly, humorous, minimalist).
  19. Do you need a mobile app version of your website? If yes, describe the core functionalities.
  20. List any third-party integrations you require (e.g., Mailchimp, HubSpot, Google Analytics, Zapier).
  21. Do you have a preference for animation and micro-interactions? If yes, describe the types of animations you want.
  22. What is your preferred layout style? (e.g., grid-based, asymmetrical, single-page scroll).
  23. Describe your ideal call-to-action (CTA) buttons. Include color, size, placement, and text.
  24. Do you need a newsletter signup form? If yes, where should it be placed on the site?
  25. List any legal requirements your website must comply with (e.g., GDPR, ADA, CCPA).
  26. Do you have a preferred loading speed for your website? What is your target page load time?
  27. Describe your ideal website header and footer design. Include elements you want to include (e.g., logo, social links, contact info).
  28. Do you need a search functionality on your site? If yes, describe how it should work.
  29. List any specific fonts or typefaces you want to use. If unsure, describe the style (e.g., serif, sans-serif, handwritten).
  30. Do you need a sitemap or wireframes before the design process begins? If yes, describe the level of detail required.
  31. What is your preferred method of communication with the designer? (e.g., email, Slack, weekly calls).
  32. Do you have a preferred color scheme for buttons, links, and backgrounds? Provide hex codes if possible.
  33. List any existing branding assets you have (e.g., logo files, brand guidelines, previous marketing materials).
  34. Do you need a password-protected section on your website? If yes, describe the purpose and access requirements.
  35. What is your preferred method for handling revisions? How many rounds of revisions are included in the project?
  36. Do you need a multilingual version of your website? If yes, list the languages and preferred translation method.
  37. Describe any specific design trends you want to incorporate (e.g., dark mode, glassmorphism, neumorphism).
  38. Do you need a favicon and social media assets designed? If yes, describe the required sizes and styles.
  39. List any analytics tools you want to integrate (e.g., Hotjar, Google Tag Manager, Facebook Pixel).
  40. What is your preferred method for receiving the final deliverables? (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, HTML/CSS files).
