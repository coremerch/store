The Ultimate Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Prompt Pack: 40 High-Converting Questions to Secure Dream Projects & Accelerate Client Approval

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary emotion or feeling you want your brand to evoke in your audience? Be specific?love, trust, excitement, etc.
  2. Describe your ideal customer in three detailed sentences. Include demographics, psychographics, and pain points they face.
  3. What are the top three competitors you admire, and what visual or conceptual elements from their branding do you want to emulate or avoid?
  4. List five adjectives that should describe your brand?s personality. Ex: bold, sophisticated, playful, minimalist.
  5. What is your brand?s core mission statement in one sentence? Avoid vague language?be precise and inspiring.
  6. Do you have any existing brand assets (logo, colors, fonts) that must be incorporated into the new identity? List them and specify their purpose.
  7. What is your brand?s unique value proposition (UVP)? How does it differ from your competitors? UVPs?
  8. Describe the tone of voice you want your brand to use in communications (e.g., formal, casual, humorous, authoritative). Provide examples of brands with a similar tone.
  9. What are the three most important keywords that should be associated with your brand in search engines or social media?
  10. Do you have a brand story or origin that should be reflected in the identity? Share the key details in 2?3 sentences.
  11. What is your brand?s primary color palette? Provide hex codes for up to five colors, and explain the emotional or cultural associations you want them to evoke.
  12. List three fonts you like (or dislike) for your brand. Include whether they should be used for headings, body text, or both.
  13. What is your brand?s main business goal for the next 12 months? (e.g., launch a new product, expand to a new market, increase online sales.)
  14. Do you have any cultural, regional, or industry-specific symbols, motifs, or imagery that should influence the design? Provide examples.
  15. What type of logo do you prefer? (e.g., wordmark, monogram, emblem, abstract mark, mascot) Provide examples of logos you admire.
  16. Describe the visual style you want for your brand. Reference art movements, designers, or styles (e.g., Art Deco, Bauhaus, Swiss Design, retro, futuristic).
  17. What is your budget range for this project? Be transparent about what you can allocate for design, implementation, and revisions.
  18. Do you have any existing brand guidelines or style sheets that need to be updated or adhered to? Share them for reference.
  19. What is your timeline for this project? Include key milestones (e.g., drafts, revisions, final delivery, launch).
  20. List three brands outside your industry whose visual identity you admire. What specifically do you like about their design?
  21. What is your brand?s stance on social and environmental issues? Should this be reflected in the visual identity?
  22. Do you have a secondary audience you need to appeal to? Describe their characteristics and how they differ from your primary audience.
  23. What is your brand?s tagline or slogan? If you don?t have one, describe the message it should convey in three words or less.
  24. List any specific design elements you want to avoid (e.g., gradients, drop shadows, serif fonts, overly complex illustrations).
  25. What is your brand?s primary communication channel (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, print ads, website)? How should the identity adapt to each platform?
  26. Describe the physical environments where your brand will be used (e.g., retail stores, trade shows, packaging, digital screens). How should the design translate across these mediums?
  27. What is your brand?s approach to typography hierarchy? Should headings be bold and large, or subtle and minimal?
  28. Do you have any brand mascots or characters that need to be integrated into the identity? Provide examples or descriptions.
  29. What is your brand?s position on minimalism vs. maximalism in design? Provide examples of brands that align with your preference.
  30. List three adjectives that describe how you want your audience to perceive your brand after seeing the new identity.
  31. What is your brand?s approach to color psychology? Do you want to use warm, cool, or neutral tones predominantly?
  32. Do you have any legal or trademark restrictions that could impact the logo or brand elements? Share any registered marks or pending applications.
  33. What is your brand?s pricing strategy? Should the identity reflect a luxury, mid-range, or budget-friendly perception?
  34. Describe the level of detail you expect in the final deliverables (e.g., full brand guidelines, logo variations, social media templates, merchandise mockups).
  35. What is your brand?s approach to animations or motion graphics? Should the identity include dynamic elements, or remain static?
  36. List any specific cultural or religious symbols that should be avoided or included in the design.
  37. What is your brand?s stance on inclusivity and diversity? Should the identity reflect these values visually or conceptually?
  38. Do you have any existing social media profiles or digital assets that need to be updated to match the new identity? Provide links or screenshots.
  39. What is your brand?s approach to whitespace and negative space in design? Should the identity be airy and open, or dense and layered?
  40. Describe the level of abstraction you want in the logo design. Should it be literal, semi-abstract, or highly conceptual?
