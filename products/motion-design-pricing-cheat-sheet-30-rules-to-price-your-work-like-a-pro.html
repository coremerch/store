Motion Design Pricing Cheat Sheet: 30 Rules to Price Your Work Like a Pro

pricing cheat sheet for motion designers ? 30 rules.

  1. Price per second for explainer videos: $50?$150 for 1080p, $100?$250 for 4K, based on complexity of animation and client budget.
  2. For social media motion graphics (Instagram, TikTok, Reels), charge $300?$800 per 15?30 second clip, depending on brand guidelines and revisions.
  3. Logo animation packages: Basic ($150?$400), Intermediate ($400?$1,000), Premium ($1,000?$2,500) with variations and style guides.
  4. Motion design for ads (YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn): Charge 15?20% of the client?s ad spend budget if the ad is under $5K; negotiate a flat fee ($1K?$5K) for larger campaigns.
  5. Corporate presentation animations (PowerPoint, Keynote): $200?$600 per slide, with discounts for bulk orders (10+ slides).
  6. UI/UX micro-interactions: $200?$800 per interaction, priced based on complexity (e.g., hover effects vs. full transitions).
  7. Animated infographics: $500?$2,000 depending on data points, custom illustrations, and interactivity requirements.
  8. 3D motion graphics (product demos, architectural walkthroughs): $500?$3,000 per minute, factoring in modeling, rendering, and client revisions.
  9. Motion design for apps (onboarding flows, loading animations): $500?$2,500 per screen or flow, with price increases for custom illustrations.
  10. Animated email headers (GIF or HTML5): $150?$500 per email, with bulk discounts for 5+ emails ($100?$400 each).
  11. Pricing for revisions: Include 2?3 rounds in your base fee; charge $50?$150 per additional revision after that, capped at 5 total.
  12. Deliverables checklist: Always include source files (AE project files, fonts, assets) unless specified otherwise?charge $100?$300 for delivery separately if needed.
  13. Rush fees: Add 50?100% to your standard rate for projects due in under 48 hours, or offer a flat rush fee ($200?$1,000) for urgent work.
  14. Licensing fees: For client use beyond the original scope, charge 20?50% of your base fee for extended licenses (e.g., international use, multi-platform).
  15. Retainer model: Offer monthly motion design retainers ($1K?$5K/month) for ongoing work like social media content, ads, or updates.
  16. Project minimums: Set a $200?$500 minimum to avoid small, time-consuming gigs that aren?t profitable (e.g., single social media posts).
  17. Deposits: Require 30?50% upfront payment before starting work; final payment due upon delivery or before revisions begin.
  18. Package deals: Bundle services (e.g., logo animation + social media clips) for 10?20% discount to increase average order value.
  19. Upsell opportunities: Offer add-ons like custom soundtracks ($100?$500), voiceovers ($200?$1,000), or extra revisions ($150?$400).
  20. Scope creep protection: Clearly define deliverables in contracts; charge $100?$300/hour for out-of-scope requests after the initial agreement.
  21. Industry-specific pricing: Charge 20?30% more for motion design in high-budget industries (luxury brands, healthcare, finance) than for startups or indie projects.
  22. Freelance platform fees: Adjust your rates by 10?20% if working on platforms like Upwork or Fiverr to account for their commission (they take 10?25%).
  23. Client education: Include a pricing guide or FAQ in your proposal to justify your rates (e.g., ?Why does motion design cost more than static design??).
  24. Portfolio builders: Offer discounted rates (20?30% off) for non-profits, charities, or personal projects to build your portfolio, but cap it at 3 projects/year.
  25. Subscription model: Offer a $500?$1,500/month subscription for unlimited motion design requests (e.g., 3?5 deliverables/month with defined scope).
  26. Holiday/seasonal pricing: Increase rates by 15?25% for projects due during peak seasons (Q4, holidays, Black Friday) due to higher demand.
  27. Collaboration discounts: Partner with other freelancers (e.g., illustrators, sound designers) and offer bundled rates to clients, splitting the profit 50/50.
  28. Retainer renewals: Discounts for loyal clients: 10% off for 2nd project, 15% for 3rd+ or offer a free revision for repeat clients.
  29. Emergency motion design: Charge a premium ($300?$1,000) for last-minute requests with a 24-hour turnaround, even if it?s small (e.g., a logo animation fix).
  30. Avoid spec work: Never create free motion design samples for clients?charge a flat fee ($100?$300) for a test animation or proposal.
