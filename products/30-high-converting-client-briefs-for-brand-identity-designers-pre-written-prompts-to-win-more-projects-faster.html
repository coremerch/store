30 High-Converting Client Briefs for Brand Identity Designers ? Pre-Written Prompts to Win More Projects Faster

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. List the top 3 business goals the client wants the brand identity to support (e.g., increase premium pricing, attract Gen Z customers, expand into Europe).
  2. Describe the current brand voice using 3 adjectives (e.g., bold, playful, trustworthy) and provide 2 examples of how it?s expressed in existing marketing.
  3. Provide the exact hex codes of the client?s 2 favorite competitor brand colors they want to differentiate from.
  4. Share the client?s current brand tagline and explain why they feel it no longer resonates with their audience.
  5. Share 5 adjectives the client wants people to feel when they see their new brand identity.
  6. List the 3 most important brand values the identity must visually communicate at first glance.
  7. Provide the client?s ideal customer persona (age, location, job title, daily challenges) in a bulleted list.
  8. Share the client?s top 3 competitors and one key visual element from each that they hate or want to avoid.
  9. Describe the client?s ideal brand personality using a well-known celebrity or fictional character as a reference.
  10. Share the client?s brand story in 3 sentences or less?highlight the origin, mission, and unique twist.
  11. Provide the client?s preferred typography style (e.g., clean sans-serif, vintage serif, handwritten script) and 2 examples of brands they admire using it.
  12. Share the client?s current brand tagline and ask them to rewrite it in a way that better reflects their new direction.
  13. List 3 industry trends the client wants the identity to align with (e.g., minimalism, retro-futurism, sustainability cues).
  14. Provide the client?s ideal brand archetype (e.g., Hero, Sage, Explorer) and explain why it fits their business.
  15. Share the client?s brand tone of voice guide, including do?s and don?ts in communication style.
  16. List 3 visual metaphors or symbols the client wants the identity to subtly incorporate (e.g., wings for freedom, gears for innovation).
  17. Provide the client?s brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) in one clear sentence they want the identity to reinforce.
  18. Share the client?s ideal color palette mood (e.g., warm and inviting, cool and professional, high-contrast for accessibility).
  19. Provide the client?s current brand mood board or ask them to create one with 5 images that represent their desired brand feel.
  20. List 3 key emotions the client wants the brand identity to evoke in their target audience (e.g., excitement, calm, confidence).
  21. Share the client?s brand?s current tagline and ask them to describe how they want it to feel visually (e.g., bold, elegant, playful).
  22. Provide the client?s brand?s social media handles and top-performing post examples for tone and style inspiration.
  23. List 3 adjectives describing how the client wants their brand to feel different from competitors in the next 12 months.
  24. Share the client?s brand?s mission statement and ask them to simplify it into a 3-word phrase for the identity.
  25. Provide the client?s brand?s current logo file and ask them to highlight what they love and what they want changed.
  26. List 3 industry-specific symbols or icons the client wants to incorporate into the new identity (e.g., leaf for organic products, network nodes for tech).
  27. Share the client?s brand?s current primary and secondary color usage examples (e.g., packaging, website, ads).
  28. Provide the client?s brand?s ideal visual style references (e.g., Dribbble shots, Pinterest boards, or competitor examples).
  29. List 3 adjectives describing how the client wants their brand to feel in person (e.g., sleek, approachable, luxurious).
  30. Share the client?s brand?s current typography usage examples and ask them to identify one font they want to replace.
  31. Provide the client?s brand?s ideal customer journey and where the new identity will make the biggest impact (e.g., packaging, website, business cards).
  32. List 3 adjectives the client wants people to associate with their brand after seeing the new identity for the first time.
  33. Share the client?s brand?s current brand guidelines (if any) and ask them to highlight one outdated element to update.
  34. Provide the client?s brand?s ideal visual hierarchy for their website homepage (e.g., bold hero section, clean navigation, subtle accent colors).
  35. List 3 adjectives describing how the client wants their brand to feel in digital spaces (e.g., futuristic, cozy, cutting-edge).
  36. Share the client?s brand?s current packaging examples and ask them to describe what they want to change or improve.
  37. Provide the client?s brand?s ideal visual treatment for their social media graphics (e.g., minimalist, illustrated, photographic).
  38. List 3 adjectives describing how the client wants their brand to feel in print materials (e.g., tactile, premium, eco-friendly).
  39. Share the client?s brand?s current business card design and ask them to describe one element they want to modernize.
  40. Provide the client?s brand?s ideal visual style for their email newsletters (e.g., clean layout, bold typography, illustrated headers).
