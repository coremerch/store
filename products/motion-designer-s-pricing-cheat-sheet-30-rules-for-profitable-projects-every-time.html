  22. Include a 15% contingency buffer in all quotes for unexpected scope changes?clients appreciate transparency.
  23. Use a per-minute pricing model for kinetic typography (e.g., $250 per minute of fully animated text).
  24. Charge $150 for a single motion graphic element (e.g., animated icon, loader, progress bar) with 2 revisions.
  25. For UI/UX micro-interactions, price per component (e.g., $100 for a button hover animation, $300 for a full modal transition).
  26. Offer a ?maintenance retainer? for ongoing updates?e.g., $400/month for 4 minor tweaks or $800/month for unlimited small changes.
  27. Use a project-based fee for title sequences (e.g., $2,000 for a 20-second cinematic opener with 2 revision rounds).
  28. Charge $50 for file format conversions (e.g., MOV to MP4, ProRes to H.264) with a 24-hour turnaround.
  29. Include a 30-day guarantee on all deliverables?fix any issues within 30 days at no extra cost to build client loyalty.
