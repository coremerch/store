The Ultimate Web Design Client Brief Generator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Clear, Conversion-Focused Projects

client brief prompts for web designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of this website (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio)?
  2. Who is the ideal target audience for this website? Include demographics, interests, and pain points.
  3. List the top 3 competitors in your industry and describe what their websites do well or poorly.
  4. What features must this website include (e.g., e-commerce, blog, contact form, login portal)?
  5. Describe the tone and voice you want for the website?s content (e.g., professional, playful, technical).
  6. What is the preferred color palette for the website? Provide hex codes or examples.
  7. Are there any specific fonts or typography styles you want to use?
  8. What is the expected layout structure (e.g., hero section, grid-based, single-page scroll)?
  9. Do you have a sitemap or wireframe already sketched out? Share it or describe it.
  10. What is the expected loading speed for the website? Provide any benchmarks or priorities.
  11. Are there any accessibility requirements (e.g., WCAG compliance, screen reader optimization)?
  12. What is the preferred platform or CMS for this website (e.g., WordPress, Squarespace, custom-built)?
  13. Do you have a deadline for the website launch? Include key milestones if applicable.
  14. What is the budget range for this project? (This helps prioritize features.)
  15. Are there any existing branding elements (e.g., logo, brand guidelines) that must be incorporated?
  16. What is the primary call-to-action (CTA) for the homepage (e.g., 'Buy Now,' 'Sign Up,' 'Learn More')?
  17. How many pages will this website include? List them and describe their purpose.
  18. Do you have a preferred style of imagery (e.g., illustrations, photos, abstract graphics)?
  19. What is the expected mobile responsiveness approach (e.g., mobile-first, desktop-first adaptive)?
  20. Are there any third-party integrations required (e.g., payment gateways, CRM tools, social media feeds)?
  21. What is the expected style of animations or micro-interactions (e.g., subtle, bold, none)?
  22. Do you have a content management system (CMS) preference for updating the site post-launch?
  23. What is the expected SEO strategy for this website? Include keywords or content priorities.
  24. Are there any legal or compliance requirements (e.g., GDPR, cookie consent, age restrictions)?
  25. What is the preferred navigation structure (e.g., mega menu, hamburger menu, sticky header)?
  26. Do you have any examples of websites you like or dislike? Share links and describe why.
  27. What is the expected level of customization for the design (e.g., template-based, fully custom)?
  28. Are there any specific animations or transitions you want to include on the site?
  29. What is the expected blog or content section structure (e.g., grid layout, sidebar, featured posts)?
  30. Do you need a password-protected section or member-exclusive content?
  31. What is the expected form structure for lead generation or contact pages?
  32. Are there any specific social media or community features (e.g., live chat, forums)?
  33. What is the expected approach for handling 404 errors or broken links?
  34. Do you need a multilingual or multi-regional version of the website?
  35. What is the expected approach for handling customer support or FAQ sections?
  36. Are there any specific analytics or tracking tools you want to integrate (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar)?
  37. What is the expected approach for handling mobile-specific features (e.g., swipe gestures, geolocation)?
  38. Do you need a system for user accounts or personalized experiences?
  39. What is the expected approach for handling performance optimization (e.g., lazy loading, caching)?
