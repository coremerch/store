100 Client-Brief Prompts for Web Designers: Get Clear Requirements, Avoid Revisions, and Deliver Client-Satisfying Websites

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of this website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio, inform visitors)
  2. Who is the ideal visitor persona for this website? Describe their demographics, interests, and pain points.
  3. What are the top 3 competitors your client admires? Share links and highlight elements they like.
  4. What are the 3 main actions you want visitors to take on this website? (e.g., 'Buy now,' 'Subscribe to newsletter,' 'Book a consultation')
  5. What is the ideal tone of voice for this website? (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist, bold)
  6. What are the 5 key pages your client wants on this website? (e.g., Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact)
  7. Does your client have a preferred color palette or brand guidelines? Provide hex codes or upload a brand style guide.
  8. What are the 3 most important features your client wants to highlight? (e.g., live chat, appointment scheduling, product filters)
  9. Does the client have a logo? If yes, provide the file. If no, will they need one designed?
  10. What is the expected timeline for this project? Include milestones like design approvals, development, and launch.
  11. Will the website need a content management system (CMS)? If yes, which one? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)
  12. What is the budget range for this project? (e.g., $1,000?$3,000, $5,000?$10,000)
  13. Does the client have any existing website analytics data? If yes, share access or screenshots of key metrics.
  14. What are the 3 biggest challenges the client faces with their current website? (if applicable)
  15. Does the client want a mobile-first, desktop-first, or responsive design approach?
  16. What are the top 3 design styles your client likes? Provide examples or links to inspiration sites.
  17. Does the client have a preferred typography style? If yes, provide font names or upload a style guide.
  18. What is the domain name for the new website? Is it registered and ready to use?
  19. Does the client need help with writing or refining website copy? If yes, what tone or messaging guidelines should be followed?
  20. What are the 3 most important visual elements your client wants to include? (e.g., hero video, product galleries, testimonials)
  21. Does the client have a preferred layout for the homepage? Describe or sketch a rough wireframe idea.
  22. What is the client?s preferred image style? (e.g., stock photos, custom illustrations, minimalist, highly detailed)
  23. Does the client want to include a blog or news section? If yes, how often will new content be published?
  24. What are the 3 most critical user flows your client wants optimized? (e.g., checkout process, sign-up flow, contact form)
  25. Does the client have any specific security or compliance requirements? (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, SSL certificate)
  26. What is the hosting provider preference? (e.g., Bluehost, SiteGround, AWS, client?s own server)
  27. Does the client want to integrate any third-party tools or APIs? List them and describe the purpose.
  28. What are the 3 most important SEO keywords or phrases your client wants to target?
  29. Does the client want to include a newsletter signup form? If yes, where should it be placed on the site?
  30. What is the client?s preferred approach to animations or micro-interactions? (e.g., subtle, bold, none)
  31. Does the client have a style guide or brand assets from a previous designer? If yes, share them.
  32. What are the 3 most important calls-to-action (CTAs) your client wants to include on the homepage?
  33. Does the client want to include social media links or feeds? If yes, which platforms?
  34. What is the client?s preferred approach to accessibility? (e.g., WCAG compliance, alt text for images, keyboard navigation)
  35. Does the client need help with setting up Google Analytics, Search Console, or other tracking tools?
  36. What are the 3 most important trust signals your client wants to include? (e.g., testimonials, client logos, security badges)
  37. Does the client want a custom 404 error page? If yes, what should the design and message be?
  38. What is the client?s preferred approach to email integration? (e.g., contact forms, newsletter automation, CRM sync)
  39. Does the client want to include a live chat feature? If yes, which platform should be used? (e.g., Intercom, Zendesk)
  40. What are the 3 most important pages your client wants to rank highest on search engines?
