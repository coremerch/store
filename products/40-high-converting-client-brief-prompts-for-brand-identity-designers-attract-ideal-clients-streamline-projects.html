40 High-Converting Client Brief Prompts for Brand Identity Designers ? Attract Ideal Clients & Streamline Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the one word you want people to feel when they see your brand?
  2. Describe your brand?s personality in three adjectives.
  3. Who is your ideal customer avatar? Include demographics, behaviors, and pain points.
  4. What are 3 brands you admire and why? (Mention colors, fonts, and overall vibe.)
  5. What is your brand?s mission statement in one sentence?
  6. What are your top 3 competitors? How do you want to differentiate from them?
  7. What emotions should your brand evoke in your target audience?
  8. Do you have existing brand assets (logo, colors, fonts) that must be considered? Upload or describe them.
  9. What is your brand?s unique value proposition (UVP)?
  10. What industry does your brand operate in? Be as specific as possible.
  11. What is your brand?s current stage of growth (startup, scaling, established)?
  12. Do you have a tagline or slogan already? If so, what is it?
  13. What tone of voice should your brand use (professional, friendly, bold, minimalist)?
  14. What are your top 3 brand colors and why? Provide color codes or upload swatches.
  15. What are your brand?s top 3 fonts? Include typefaces and usage examples.
  16. What is your brand?s main product or service offering?
  17. What is your brand?s primary goal for this identity project (e.g., launch, rebrand, awareness)?
  18. What is your budget range for this project? (Be honest?it helps tailor the process.)
  19. What is your timeline for this project? Include key milestones (research, design, delivery).
  20. Do you have any brand guidelines or style references we should follow?
  21. What is your brand?s current logo? Upload it or describe its issues.
  22. What is your brand?s secondary logo or alternate logo? If none, describe your preference.
  23. What are your brand?s primary and secondary color palettes? Include hex codes.
  24. Do you prefer modern, classic, playful, or bold logo styles?
  25. What is your brand?s preferred typography style (serif, sans-serif, script, etc.)?
  26. What is your brand?s logo usage context (digital, print, merchandise)?
  27. What is your brand?s USPs (Unique Selling Points) in bullet points?
  28. What is your brand?s target market size (local, national, global)?
  29. What is your brand?s social media presence? List platforms and follower counts.
  30. What is your brand?s website URL? If none, describe your ideal site structure.
  31. What is your brand?s packaging style preference (minimalist, luxurious, eco-friendly)?
  32. What is your brand?s photography style (bright, moody, candid, staged)?
  33. What is your brand?s illustration style preference (flat, hand-drawn, 3D)?
  34. Do you have any brand mascots or characters? Describe or upload examples.
  35. What is your brand?s preferred icon style (line, solid, outlined)?
  36. What is your brand?s visual hierarchy preference (bold headers, subtle details)?
  37. What is your brand?s preferred grid or layout system (modular, asymmetrical)?
  38. What is your brand?s motion graphics or animation style preference?
  39. What is your brand?s preferred file formats for deliverables (AI, PDF, PNG, SVG)?
  40. What is your brand?s legal or trademark restrictions we should know about?
