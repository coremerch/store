Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Template: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Faster, Deeper Client Onboarding

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the name of your business, and does it already have an established name that cannot be changed?
  2. Describe your business in one sentence without using industry jargon or buzzwords.
  3. Who are your top three competitors, and what specific elements of their visual identities do you admire or want to avoid?
  4. What is the core emotional response you want customers to feel when they first see your brand?s visual identity?
  5. List three adjectives that should describe your brand?s personality (e.g., bold, trustworthy, playful).
  6. Do you have an existing logo or visual identity? If yes, share it and explain what you like or dislike about it.
  7. What colors (by name or hex code) do you already associate with your brand, even if they?re not finalized?
  8. Are there any colors you absolutely want to include in your new identity? Provide the reason (e.g., ?red for energy?).
  9. What colors do you want to avoid in your brand identity? Share any specific dislikes or past negative experiences.
  10. Do you have a preferred typography style (e.g., serif, sans-serif, handwritten, modern)? Provide examples if possible.
  11. Are there any fonts you already own or want to use for your brand? Include license details if applicable.
  12. What is your brand?s primary message or tagline? If you don?t have one, what should it communicate about your values?
  13. Who is your ideal customer? Describe their age, lifestyle, values, and what visual styles appeal to them.
  14. What tone should your brand voice use in all communications (e.g., formal, casual, humorous, inspirational)?
  15. Do you have any existing brand guidelines, style guides, or previous design work we should reference?
  16. Are there any cultural, religious, or ethical symbols we should avoid in your brand?s visual identity?
  17. What is your brand?s mission statement? If you don?t have one, what is the purpose of your business?
  18. Do you want your brand identity to feel traditional, modern, minimalist, maximalist, retro, or something else?
  19. Are there any specific design elements (e.g., geometric shapes, organic lines, abstract forms) you want included or avoided?
  20. What is your budget range for this brand identity project, including design, revisions, and final assets?
  21. What is your timeline for launching this new brand identity? Include key milestones (e.g., logo approval, website updates).
  22. Do you have a preferred file format for delivering the final logo (e.g., vector, PNG, SVG)?
  23. Are there any specific brand applications you need designs for immediately (e.g., business cards, website, social media)?
  24. Do you want your brand identity to include a secondary logo or alternate mark for different uses?
  25. Should your logo work in both color and black-and-white formats? Provide examples if you have preferences.
  26. Do you want to incorporate any existing imagery (e.g., illustrations, photos, patterns) into your brand identity?
  27. Are there any trends in branding that you love or hate? Share examples if possible.
  28. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)? How should this be reflected visually?
  29. Do you have a preferred method for presenting design concepts (e.g., digital mockups, physical prototypes, mood boards)?
  30. Are there any specific industries or professions your brand should appeal to visually?
  31. What is your company?s founding story? Are there any visual elements from this story we should incorporate?
  32. Do you want your brand identity to feel inclusive, diverse, or representative of a specific community? How?
  33. Are there any visual metaphors or symbols you associate with your brand (e.g., mountains for stability, waves for movement)?
  34. What is your brand?s stance on sustainability or social responsibility? Should this be reflected visually?
  35. Do you have any existing marketing materials (e.g., brochures, ads) that we should align with in the new identity?
  36. Are there any specific design tools or software you prefer for collaboration (e.g., Adobe Illustrator, Figma, Canva)?
  37. What is your preferred method for receiving feedback (e.g., written notes, video calls, annotated files)?
  38. Do you want to include any custom illustrations or icons in your brand identity? Provide examples if you have them.
  39. Are there any legal considerations (e.g., trademark conflicts, copyright issues) we should be aware of before finalizing designs?
  40. What is your brand?s long-term vision? How should the identity evolve over the next 3?5 years?
