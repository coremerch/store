Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief Template: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Streamline Onboarding & Deliver Exceptional Work

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is your brand?s mission statement in one sentence?
  2. List three adjectives that best describe the personality of your brand.
  3. Who is your primary target audience? Include demographics (age, gender, location) and psychographics (values, interests, lifestyle).
  4. Describe your brand?s current visual identity (colors, fonts, logo usage) and why you feel it?s not resonating with your audience.
  5. What are the top three emotions you want your brand to evoke in customers?
  6. Provide examples of 3-5 brands you admire and explain what specifically appeals to you about their branding.
  7. Do you have an existing brand style guide? If yes, attach it or summarize its key elements.
  8. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)? How do you differentiate from competitors?
  9. What is the tone of voice for your brand? (e.g., professional, playful, authoritative, friendly) Provide examples of how this tone should sound in written content.
  10. What are the core values of your brand? Rank them in order of importance.
  11. Describe your ideal customer persona in detail. Include their pain points, goals, and how your brand can solve their problems.
  12. What is the primary goal of this rebranding project? (e.g., attract a new demographic, modernize the brand, increase trust, etc.)
  13. Do you have any specific design preferences or non-negotiables? (e.g., minimalist, retro, bold colors, specific fonts)
  14. What is your budget range for this project? Be as specific as possible (e.g., $1,000?$3,000, $5,000?$10,000).
  15. What is your preferred timeline for completion? Include key milestones (e.g., logo drafts in 2 weeks, final delivery in 6 weeks).
  16. Do you have any existing brand assets (e.g., logos, social media graphics, packaging) that need to be updated or replaced? Describe them.
  17. What industry does your brand operate in? Are there any industry-specific design trends or conventions you?d like to incorporate?
  18. Do you have any brand guidelines for logo usage (e.g., minimum size, color variations, clear space requirements)?
  19. What is the name of your brand, and does it have any special meaning or pronunciation quirks?
  20. Are there any colors you absolutely want to avoid in your branding? Why?
  21. Do you have a tagline or slogan? If yes, what is it, and should it be incorporated into the visual identity?
  22. What is the primary platform where your brand will be most visible? (e.g., digital, print, social media, packaging) How should the design adapt to these platforms?
  23. Describe any cultural or regional considerations that should influence the design (e.g., symbolism, color meanings, language nuances).
  24. Do you have any existing marketing materials (e.g., website, business cards, brochures) that the new identity should complement?
  25. What is your brand?s current market position? (e.g., luxury, budget-friendly, niche, mainstream) How should the rebrand reflect this?
  26. Are there any competitors you want to distance your brand from visually? Provide examples.
  27. Do you have any specific imagery or photography style in mind for your brand? (e.g., candid, staged, minimalist, vibrant)
  28. What is your brand?s story? Share key milestones, challenges, or inspirations that shaped its identity.
  29. Do you have any brand mascots, illustrations, or custom icons that need to be integrated into the new identity?
  30. What is your brand?s social media presence like? Should the new identity align with or update the tone of your social content?
  31. Do you have any existing customer feedback or survey data about your brand?s perception? Share key insights.
  32. Are there any legal considerations for your brand?s name or logo? (e.g., trademark conflicts, domain availability)
  33. What is your brand?s pricing strategy? Does it align with a premium, mid-range, or budget positioning?
  34. Do you have any specific fonts or typography styles you love or hate? Provide examples if possible.
  35. What is your brand?s relationship with sustainability or ethical practices? Should this influence the design?
  36. Are there any seasonal or time-sensitive elements that should be considered in the branding (e.g., holiday promotions, limited editions)?
  37. Do you have any existing brand ambassadors, influencers, or partnerships that should be reflected in the identity?
  38. What is your brand?s customer acquisition strategy? Should the design appeal to a specific stage of the customer journey?
  39. Do you have any specific print or digital files you need delivered (e.g., CMYK, RGB, vector files, social media templates)?
  40. What are your expectations for post-launch support? (e.g., usage guidelines, template updates, troubleshooting)
