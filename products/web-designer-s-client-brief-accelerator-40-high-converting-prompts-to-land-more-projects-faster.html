Web Designer's Client Brief Accelerator: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land More Projects Faster

client brief prompts for web designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What are the top 3 business goals this website needs to achieve within the first 6 months?
  2. List all the primary user personas who will visit this site and their top pain points.
  3. What are the 3 most important actions you want visitors to take on every page?
  4. Describe the current brand style (colors, fonts, imagery) and provide 3 examples you love.
  5. What?s the one feature or functionality that will make this website stand out from competitors?
  6. Share 3 examples of websites you admire and explain why each one resonates with you.
  7. What?s the budget range for this project (include design, development, and ongoing maintenance)?

  9. List all the pages that must be included in the initial design phase (e.g., Home, About, Services, Contact).
  10. What?s the tone of voice you want the website to convey (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist)?
  11. Provide 3 examples of call-to-action buttons or sections you?ve seen that you?d like to replicate.
  12. What?s the primary device your target audience uses most (desktop, mobile, or tablet)?
  13. Do you have existing brand assets (logo files, style guides, or brand guidelines) we can use?
  14. What?s the deadline for the first design round and final delivery?
  15. List any third-party integrations required (e.g., payment gateways, CRM, email marketing tools).
  16. What?s the ideal loading speed you?re aiming for, and which tools will you use to test it?
  17. Do you have a sitemap or wireframe already created? If yes, share it.
  18. What?s the SEO strategy for this website? Are there specific keywords or search terms to target?
  19. Describe the competitive landscape: Who are your top 3 competitors, and what do their websites do well?
  20. What?s the primary navigation structure you envision (e.g., dropdown menus, mega menus, hamburger menu)?
  21. Do you have any legal or compliance requirements (e.g., GDPR, accessibility standards like WCAG)?
  22. What?s the expected lifespan of this website before a redesign is likely needed?
  23. List any specific animations, micro-interactions, or visual effects you?d like to include.
  24. What?s the process for gathering and approving content (text, images, videos) for the website?
  25. Do you have a preferred hosting provider or domain registrar?
  26. What?s the plan for ongoing updates and maintenance after the website launches?
  27. List any specific fonts or typefaces you want to avoid due to brand alignment or readability issues.
  28. What?s the role of social media in driving traffic to this website? Should social feeds be integrated?
  29. Do you have any existing customer testimonials, case studies, or portfolio pieces to feature?
  30. What?s the approach to mobile responsiveness? Should we prioritize mobile-first or desktop-first design?
  31. List any specific color palettes or gradients you?d like to incorporate (or avoid).
  32. What?s the strategy for collecting user feedback post-launch (e.g., surveys, heatmaps, analytics)?
  33. Do you have a preferred project management tool (e.g., Trello, Asana, ClickUp) for collaboration?
  34. What?s the budget allocation breakdown for design vs. development vs. content creation?
  35. List any specific design tools or software you?d like the designer to use (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch).
  36. What?s the approach to scalability?should the design accommodate future growth or feature additions?
  37. Do you have a preferred method for delivering feedback (e.g., written comments, video recordings, live calls)?
  38. What?s the plan for testing cross-browser compatibility and performance optimization?
  39. List any specific industry standards or trends you want the website to follow or stand out from.
