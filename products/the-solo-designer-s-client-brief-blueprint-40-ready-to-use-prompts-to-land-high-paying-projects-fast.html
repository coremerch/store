The Solo Designer?s Client Brief Blueprint: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Land High-Paying Projects Fast

client brief prompts for agency escapees going solo ? 40 prompts.

  1. List the client?s top 3 business goals in their own words.
  2. Ask the client to describe their ideal customer in one sentence.
  3. Request a link to the client?s current website or portfolio.
  4. Find out the client?s primary competitors and their recent campaigns.
  5. Ask for the client?s brand style guide, fonts, and color codes.
  6. Request a mood board or examples of designs they love.
  7. Ask how quickly they need the final deliverables.
  8. Find out if they have a preferred project management tool (e.g., Trello, Asana).
  9. Ask for a single sentence that defines their brand personality.
  10. Request a list of 3-5 adjectives that describe their desired tone.
  11. Ask if they have existing brand assets (logos, images, videos) to reuse.
  12. Find out their budget range in a single number (e.g., $3,000?$5,000).
  13. Ask for their preferred timeline broken into phases (e.g., research, design, revisions).
  14. Request a list of 3 key stakeholders who must approve the final design.
  15. Ask if they have a style guide or brand standards document.
  16. Find out if they need social media templates included in the project.
  17. Ask for their preferred file formats for final deliverables (e.g., AI, PDF, PNG).
  18. Request a list of 3 recent projects they admire for inspiration.
  19. Ask if they have a brand story or mission statement to incorporate.
  20. Find out if they need the design to be responsive across devices.
  21. Ask for their preferred communication method (email, Slack, calls).
  22. Request a list of 3-5 websites they want their design to outperform.
  23. Ask if they have a brand audit or competitive analysis report.
  24. Find out if they need a style guide created alongside the design.
  25. Ask for their preferred color palette in hex codes.
  26. Request a list of 3-5 pain points their customers currently face.
  27. Ask if they have a preferred typography hierarchy for their content.
  28. Find out if they need a brand refresh or a completely new identity.
  29. Ask for their top 3 KPIs for measuring the success of the project.
  30. Request a list of 3-5 industry trends they want the design to reflect.
  31. Ask if they have a preferred layout or grid system for the design.
  32. Find out if they need a one-page summary of the project scope for internal approval.
  33. Ask for their preferred tone of voice in written content (e.g., formal, casual).
  34. Request a list of 3-5 design elements they want to include (e.g., illustrations, icons).
  35. Ask if they have a preferred file naming convention for deliverables.
  36. Find out if they need a brand voice guide as part of the project.
  37. Ask for their preferred method of presenting design concepts (e.g., PDF, live demo).
  38. Request a list of 3-5 legal or compliance requirements for the design (e.g., accessibility).
  39. Ask if they have a preferred method for gathering stakeholder feedback (e.g., surveys).
  40. Find out if they need a project timeline with milestones and deadlines.
