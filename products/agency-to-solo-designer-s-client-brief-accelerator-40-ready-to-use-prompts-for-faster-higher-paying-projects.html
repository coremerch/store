Agency-to-Solo Designer?s Client Brief Accelerator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Faster, Higher-Paying Projects

client brief prompts for agency escapees going solo ? 39 prompts.

  1. List the client?s top three business goals in their exact words.
  2. What is the client?s biggest pain point with their current brand or website?
  3. Describe the client?s ideal customer in three bullet points with demographics and psychographics.
  4. What specific KPIs will the client measure to determine the project?s success?
  5. Provide the client?s current brand guidelines, tone of voice examples, and any existing style references.
  6. What are the client?s competitors doing that they admire (or want to avoid)?
  7. Ask the client to share 3-5 examples of designs they love and explain why.
  8. What is the client?s budget range for this project (be transparent)?
  9. What is the client?s timeline for launch or delivery (include key milestones)?
  10. Does the client have a preferred communication style (e.g., Slack, email, weekly calls)?
  11. List the client?s must-have features or functionalities for the project.
  12. What are the client?s deal-breakers or non-negotiables for the design?
  13. Ask the client to describe their dream customer?s journey from first touch to conversion.
  14. What type of content (e.g., copy, images, videos) will the client provide, and what will you create?
  15. Does the client have any existing assets (logos, fonts, imagery) we can repurpose?
  16. What is the client?s stance on trends (e.g., minimalism, bold colors, retro)?
  17. Ask the client to share any past project feedback they received that shaped their preferences.
  18. What is the client?s preferred platform for the final deliverable (e.g., website, app, print)?
  19. Does the client have any legal or compliance requirements (e.g., accessibility, GDPR)?
  20. What is the client?s approach to user experience (e.g., intuitive, experimental, data-driven)?
  21. Ask the client to define their brand personality in 3 adjectives.
  22. What are the client?s expectations for post-launch support or revisions?
  23. Does the client have any internal stakeholders who need to approve the design?
  24. What is the client?s stance on revisions (e.g., unlimited, 2 rounds, fixed)?
  25. Ask the client to share their brand?s origin story or mission statement.
  26. What is the client?s preferred file format for final deliverables (e.g., Sketch, Figma, PDF)?
  27. Does the client have any specific color palettes or fonts they want to avoid?
  28. What is the client?s stance on animations or micro-interactions in the design?
  29. Ask the client to describe their ideal user flow for the project.
  30. What are the client?s expectations for SEO or discoverability in the final product?
  31. Does the client have any social media or marketing channels tied to this project?
  32. What is the client?s approach to typography (e.g., bold headlines, clean serifs)?
  33. Ask the client to share any past project failures and what they learned from them.
  34. What is the client?s preferred method for delivering feedback (e.g., annotated screenshots, live sessions)?
  35. Does the client have any budget constraints for specific features (e.g., illustrations, custom icons)?
  36. What is the client?s stance on dark mode or alternative color schemes?
  37. Ask the client to define their ideal customer?s biggest frustration and how the design addresses it.
  38. What are the client?s expectations for ongoing maintenance or updates after launch?
  39. Does the client have any specific design systems or libraries they want to align with?
