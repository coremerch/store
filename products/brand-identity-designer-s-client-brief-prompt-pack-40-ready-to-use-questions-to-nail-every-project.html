Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Prompt Pack: 40 Ready-to-Use Questions to Nail Every Project

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the company name, and how should it be stylized (e.g., all caps, lowercase, monogram)?
  2. Describe the core mission of the business in one sentence.
  3. List the top 3 adjectives that best describe the brand personality (e.g., bold, elegant, playful).
  4. Who is the primary target audience? Include age range, gender, location, and key interests.
  5. What are the three biggest competitors, and how should this brand differentiate itself?
  6. What is the primary color palette currently used by the company (if any)?
  7. Are there any colors that should be avoided due to cultural, personal, or industry reasons?
  8. What fonts (if any) are already associated with the brand, and should they be retained or updated?
  9. Should the logo be wordmark, symbol, or combination mark?
  10. What is the preferred logo style (e.g., minimalist, vintage, futuristic, hand-drawn)?
  11. Are there any specific symbols, icons, or imagery styles that should be incorporated or avoided?
  12. What is the brand?s tone of voice (e.g., professional, friendly, humorous, authoritative)?
  13. Should the logo work well in black and white, and does it need a simplified version for favicons?
  14. What is the primary medium where the logo will appear (e.g., digital, print, merchandise)?
  15. Are there any existing brand guidelines or style references that must be followed?
  16. What is the budget range for this project, and does it include print or digital assets?
  17. What is the expected timeline for logo design and final delivery of all brand materials?
  18. Are there any industry-specific regulations or standards that the logo must comply with?
  19. What is the company?s unique selling proposition (USP)?
  20. Should the brand identity feel traditional, modern, or somewhere in between?
  21. Are there any specific cultural or regional preferences that should influence the design (e.g., lucky symbols, colors)?
  22. What is the preferred layout style for business cards, letterheads, and social media assets?
  23. Should the brand identity include custom illustrations or photography, or rely on stock assets?
  24. What is the primary call-to-action for the brand (e.g., sign up, buy now, learn more)?
  25. Are there any existing taglines or slogans that should be incorporated into the design?
  26. What is the company?s stance on sustainability, and should it influence the color palette or materials?
  27. Should the logo design be scalable to work on a billboard, mobile app icon, and embroidery?
  28. Are there any specific design trends the client likes or dislikes?
  29. What is the client?s opinion on minimalist vs. detailed design approaches?
  30. Should the brand identity include a mascot or character, and if so, what should it represent?
  31. What is the primary emotion or feeling the brand wants to evoke in its audience?
  32. Are there any historical or legacy elements that should be preserved in the rebrand?
  33. What is the client?s opinion on serif vs. sans-serif fonts for primary branding?
  34. Should the brand identity include a secondary color palette for accents or highlights?
  35. What is the preferred format for delivering final files (e.g., AI, EPS, PNG, SVG)?
  36. Are there any specific design mockups the client wants to see (e.g., t-shirt, package, website)?
  37. What is the company?s social media strategy, and should it influence the brand identity?
  38. Should the logo design be abstract, literal, or a mix of both?
  39. Are there any specific design elements that the client absolutely loves or hates?
