Design Pricing Mastery: 30 Rules to Price Like a Pro for Students & New Freelancers

pricing cheat sheet for design students ? 29 rules.

  1. Always price by project scope, not hours, to avoid undervaluing your creativity.
  2. Research local market rates for students to set a realistic baseline before pitching clients.
  3. Charge a 20-30% premium for rush projects to compensate for overtime and stress.
  4. Offer three pricing tiers (Basic, Pro, Elite) to give clients clear options and upsell naturally.
  5. Use a flat fee for logo designs instead of hourly to prevent scope creep and client hesitation.
  6. Add a 10-15% deposit before starting any project to secure commitment and cover initial costs.
  7. Factor in software subscriptions (Adobe, Figma, etc.) into your pricing to avoid absorbing hidden costs.
  8. Round prices to the nearest $5 or $10 to make invoicing cleaner and more professional.
  9. Charge extra for revisions beyond the agreed-upon 2-3 rounds to protect your time and workflow.
  10. Include a 15% project management fee for clients who require frequent meetings or updates.
  11. Price social media templates per platform (e.g., Instagram Story pack = $X, TikTok template = $Y).
  12. Offer a 10% discount for students who book three or more projects upfront to build long-term relationships.
  13. Set a minimum project fee of $150?$300 for beginners to avoid tiny, unprofitable jobs.
  14. Charge a 50% upfront payment for custom illustrations to cover materials and time investment.
  15. Use a project-based pricing calculator (like those from The Futur or Bonsai) to justify your rates to clients.
  16. Never work for exposure?always demand payment, even if it?s a small fee for student portfolios.
  17. Price business card designs per quantity (e.g., 50 cards = $X, 200 cards = $Y) with a bulk discount.
  18. Add a 20% fee for last-minute cancellations within 48 hours of the project start date.
  19. Offer a ?rush fee? of $50?$100 for projects delivered within 24 hours to compensate for urgency.
  20. Price branding packages (logo + color palette + typography guide) as a bundle to increase perceived value.
  21. Charge a 10% fee for international clients to cover currency conversion and payment processing costs.
  22. Use a sliding scale for non-profits or fellow students, but never go below your minimum viable rate.
  23. Price website mockups per page (e.g., Homepage = $X, About Page = $Y) with a discount for full-site projects.
  24. Always include a 5?10% buffer in your quote for unexpected revisions or client feedback loops.
  25. Charge separately for stock assets (images, fonts, icons) if they?re not part of your original design work.
  26. Price packaging designs per side (e.g., Front + Back = $X, Full sleeve = $Y) to reflect complexity.
  27. Offer a ?maintenance plan? for $50?$100/month to handle small updates post-project without renegotiating rates.
  28. Never negotiate your price below 80% of your quoted rate?walk away if the client insists on a lower budget.
  29. Use a ?kill fee? of 30?50% of the project cost if the client cancels mid-way through to recover lost time.
