The Ultimate Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief Generator: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land Your Dream Projects Fast

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. Ask the client to describe their brand in 3 words and explain why each word was chosen.
  2. Request a list of 5 brands they admire and describe what specific elements resonate with them.
  3. Ask for their brand?s mission statement in one sentence?if they don?t have one, help them craft it.
  4. Inquire about their brand?s core values and ask them to prioritize the top 3 in order of importance.
  5. Request examples of visual styles they like (e.g., minimalist, retro, modern) with links or descriptions.
  6. Ask for their preferred color palette (or request they name 3 colors they love and 3 they hate).
  7. Request their brand?s tone of voice (e.g., professional, friendly, playful) and provide 3 example sentences.
  8. Ask for their target audience demographics (age, gender, location, interests) in bullet points.
  9. Request their competitors? names and ask them to highlight one thing they want to do differently.
  10. Ask for their brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) in one clear sentence.
  11. Request their preferred typography styles (e.g., serif, sans-serif, script) and any fonts they love or hate.
  12. Ask for their logo usage guidelines (e.g., color variations, minimum size, preferred file formats).
  13. Request their brand?s personality traits (e.g., bold, trustworthy, creative) with examples of how it manifests.
  14. Ask for their social media handles and their top-performing post examples they?d like to emulate.
  15. Request their website URL and ask them to point out 3 elements they want to improve visually.
  16. Ask for their brand?s tagline or slogan (or request they brainstorm 3 options for you to refine).
  17. Request their business goals for the next 1-3 years and how branding will help achieve them.
  18. Ask for their budget range for the project and their preferred payment schedule.
  19. Request their timeline for the project, including key milestones and deadlines.
  20. Ask if they have a brand style guide or previous branding materials they?d like to incorporate or update.
  21. Request their preferred communication method (e.g., email, Slack, weekly calls) and response time expectations.
  22. Ask for their feedback preferences (e.g., do they prefer detailed notes or high-level overviews?).
  23. Request their brand?s industry and ask how they want to stand out within it.
  24. Ask for their brand?s story or origin?how and why the business was founded.
  25. Request their thoughts on sustainability or ethical branding and if they want this reflected in the design.
  26. Ask for their brand?s key messaging pillars (e.g., innovation, reliability, luxury) and supporting proof points.
  27. Request their thoughts on incorporating mascots, illustrations, or custom icons into the branding.
  28. Ask if they have any existing brand assets (e.g., old logos, marketing materials) they?d like to discard or replace.
  29. Request their thoughts on using abstract shapes, geometric patterns, or organic forms in the design.
  30. Ask for their preferred imagery style (e.g., photorealistic, hand-drawn, 3D-rendered) and examples.
  31. Request their opinion on animation or motion graphics for digital branding (e.g., social media, website).
  32. Ask if they want the branding to feel nostalgic, futuristic, or timeless, with examples of each.
  33. Request their thoughts on custom typography versus using existing font families for their logo.
  34. Ask for their brand?s secondary and tertiary audiences (if different from the primary target).
  35. Request their preferred design software or tools they?d like the final files to be compatible with.
  36. Ask if they have any cultural or regional sensitivities that should be considered in the design.
  37. Request their thoughts on incorporating interactive elements (e.g., QR codes, augmented reality) into the branding.
  38. Ask for their brand?s social proof (e.g., customer testimonials, awards) they?d like highlighted in the visuals.
  39. Request their opinions on minimalism versus maximalism in branding and examples that align with their preference.
  40. Ask if they want the branding to be gender-neutral, masculine, feminine, or adaptable to all genders.
