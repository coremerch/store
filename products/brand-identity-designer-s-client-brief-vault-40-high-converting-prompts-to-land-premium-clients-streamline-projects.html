Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Vault: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land Premium Clients & Streamline Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What are the top three adjectives that best describe the personality of the brand you want to create?
  2. Describe the ideal customer persona for this brand in three sentences or less.
  3. List three brands you admire and explain why their visual identity resonates with you.
  4. What is the primary emotion or message you want customers to feel when they interact with your brand?
  5. Specify the core values of your business and how they should be reflected in the brand identity.
  6. What is the name of your business, and does it have any historical or cultural significance we should incorporate?
  7. Provide the exact hex codes for your brand?s primary and secondary colors, or describe the mood you want to evoke with color.
  8. Do you have any existing brand assets (logos, fonts, imagery) that must be incorporated into the new identity? If so, attach or describe them.
  9. What is your brand?s mission statement in one concise sentence?
  10. Describe your brand?s tone of voice in three words (e.g., playful, authoritative, friendly).
  11. What are the top three competitors in your industry, and how do you want your brand to differentiate visually?
  12. Do you have a specific style guide or visual references (e.g., Pinterest board) you?d like us to follow?
  13. What is the primary industry or niche your brand operates in, and does it have any visual clich?s we should avoid?
  14. Describe the target audience?s demographics (age, gender, location, income level, etc.).
  15. What is the budget range for this brand identity project?
  16. Do you have a preferred timeline for delivery, including key milestones (e.g., drafts, revisions, final delivery)?
  17. Should the brand identity feel modern, classic, minimalist, maximalist, retro, or something else? Be specific.
  18. Are there any cultural, religious, or ethical sensitivities we need to consider when designing the brand identity?
  19. What are the must-have deliverables for this project (e.g., logo, color palette, typography, brand guidelines)?
  20. Do you have a tagline or slogan that needs to be integrated into the visual identity?
  21. What is the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) that sets it apart from competitors?
  22. Describe the physical and digital environments where the brand identity will be applied (e.g., packaging, website, social media, signage).
  23. Are there any specific fonts or font families you like or dislike for this project?
  24. What is the brand?s current perception in the market, and how do you want to change or reinforce it?
  25. Do you have any existing customer feedback or surveys that highlight brand perception strengths or weaknesses?
  26. What is the brand?s long-term vision in five years, and how should the identity evolve to support it?
  27. List three mood boards or visual references that inspire the direction you want for this brand.
  28. Should the brand identity include any mascots, illustrations, or custom icons? If so, describe the style.
  29. What is the primary use case for the brand identity (e.g., e-commerce, B2B services, local business)?
  30. Do you need the brand identity to work well in both digital and print formats? If so, specify any constraints.
  31. What is the name of the person who will be the main point of contact for this project?
  32. Are there any legal considerations (e.g., trademark conflicts, copyright issues) we should account for in the design?
  33. Describe the brand?s current logo (if applicable) and why you?re seeking a redesign or refresh.
  34. What is the brand?s current market position, and are you aiming for a luxury, mid-range, or budget-conscious perception?
  35. Do you have any specific design trends you?d like to incorporate or avoid in this identity?
  36. What is the brand?s primary communication channel (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, email) and how should the identity adapt to it?
  37. Are there any historical or regional design elements that should influence the brand?s visual identity?
  38. What is the brand?s approach to sustainability or social responsibility, and should it be reflected in the identity?
  39. Provide the contact details of the key decision-maker who will approve the final brand identity.
  40. What is the brand?s elevator pitch, and how can the visual identity help communicate it quickly?
