Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Accelerator: 40 High-Converting Prompts for Faster, Stronger Client Work

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the one word that should immediately come to mind when someone sees your brand?
  2. List three brands your client admires and describe what they like about each one.
  3. What emotions or feelings should the brand evoke in its target audience?
  4. Describe the brand?s personality using three adjectives (e.g., bold, playful, sophisticated).
  5. Who is the primary target audience for this brand? Include demographics like age, gender, location, and income level.
  6. What are the top three competitors of this brand, and how does the client want to differentiate?
  7. What is the brand?s mission statement? If none exists, draft one based on the client?s core values.
  8. What are the brand?s core values? Provide a list of 3-5 foundational principles.
  9. Does the brand have an existing tagline, slogan, or key messaging? If so, what is it?
  10. What is the brand?s current visual style (if any)? Provide examples of logos, colors, or imagery they like or dislike.
  11. What type of typography resonates with the client?s vision? (e.g., modern sans-serif, classic serif, handwritten, bold display)
  12. What color palette does the client envision? Provide 2-3 color families or specific hues they prefer.
  13. Are there any colors the client wants to avoid? List them explicitly.
  14. What is the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)? How does it solve a problem for its customers?
  15. What are the brand?s long-term and short-term business goals? How should design support these goals?
  16. Does the brand have a specific industry or niche? How should the design reflect this?
  17. What is the brand?s tone of voice? (e.g., friendly, professional, humorous, authoritative)
  18. Are there any cultural or regional considerations that should influence the design?
  19. What are the brand?s current strengths and weaknesses in terms of visual identity?
  20. Does the client have any existing brand guidelines or style sheets? If so, share them.
  21. What is the brand?s story? Provide a brief narrative about its origin, purpose, or journey.
  22. Are there any specific symbols, icons, or imagery styles the client wants to incorporate?
  23. Should the brand identity include a mascot or character? If so, describe its personality and style.
  24. What is the client?s preferred format for the final brand identity deliverables? (e.g., digital files, print-ready files, brand books)
  25. Are there any technical constraints (e.g., file formats, resolution requirements, color modes) for the project?
  26. What is the deadline for the first phase of the brand identity project?
  27. Does the client have a budget range for this project? If so, what is it?
  28. Are there any legal considerations (e.g., trademarks, copyrights) that need to be addressed?
  29. What is the client?s preferred method of communication during the project (e.g., email, Slack, Zoom)?
  30. Does the client have any internal stakeholders or team members who need to approve the design? If so, list their names and roles.
  31. What is the brand?s current market position? (e.g., market leader, challenger, niche player)
  32. Are there any seasonal or promotional events where the brand identity will be prominently featured?
  33. What is the brand?s relationship with its current or past customers? How should the identity reflect this?
  34. Does the client want the brand identity to be flexible for future expansions or product lines?
  35. What are the client?s expectations for post-launch support (e.g., updates, additional assets, brand audits)?
  36. Are there any specific industry trends the client wants the brand identity to align with?
  37. What is the brand?s approach to sustainability or ethical practices? Should this be reflected in the design?
  38. Does the client have a preference for minimalist, maximalist, or somewhere-in-between design styles?
  39. What is the brand?s primary communication channel (e.g., social media, website, print ads)? How should the identity support this?
  40. Are there any existing brand assets (e.g., logos, fonts, imagery) that must be preserved or incorporated into the new identity?
