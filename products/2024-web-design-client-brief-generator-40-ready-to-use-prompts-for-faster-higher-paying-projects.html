2024 Web Design Client Brief Generator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Faster, Higher-Paying Projects

client brief prompts for web designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of this website (e.g., sell products, generate leads, build brand awareness)?
  2. Who is the ideal visitor persona for this website? Include age, profession, and online behavior.
  3. List 3 competitor websites you admire and explain why their design works for their audience.
  4. What are the top 3 features or functionalities your website must include (e.g., booking system, e-commerce cart, interactive map)?
  5. Describe the brand?s visual identity in 3 words (e.g., modern, playful, luxurious).
  6. What is the brand?s color palette? Provide hex codes or Pantone references if available.
  7. What are the brand?s primary and secondary typography choices? Include font names and weights.
  8. Do you have an existing style guide, brand book, or design system? If yes, share it.
  9. What is the preferred layout style (e.g., minimalist, grid-based, asymmetrical, dark mode)?
  10. Should the website be mobile-first, desktop-first, or responsive with equal priority?
  11. What is the expected user journey for a first-time visitor? Map it out step by step.
  12. Where will the primary call-to-action (CTA) buttons be placed (e.g., above the fold, in the header, after a section)?
  13. What tone should the copy convey (e.g., professional, friendly, humorous, authoritative)?
  14. Do you have any existing content (text, images, videos) that must be included, or will new content be created?
  15. What is the deadline for the first draft? Include milestones for revisions and final delivery.
  16. How many rounds of revisions are included in the project scope?
  17. What is the total budget range for this project? Be as specific as possible.
  18. Who are the key stakeholders involved in the approval process?
  19. Should the website include animations or micro-interactions? If yes, describe the style (e.g., subtle, bold, looped).
  20. Do you need a content management system (CMS) like WordPress, Webflow, or a custom solution?
  21. Will the website require integration with third-party tools (e.g., CRM, email marketing, payment gateways)?
  22. What is the expected load time for the website? Should performance optimization be a priority?
  23. Should the website include a blog, news section, or resource library?
  24. Do you need a sitemap or wireframe draft before the design begins?
  25. What is the preferred navigation structure (e.g., mega menu, dropdown, hamburger menu)?
  26. Should the website include a dark mode toggle?
  27. What is the brand?s tone of voice for error messages, notifications, and success states?
  28. Do you have any accessibility requirements (e.g., WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, screen reader optimization)?
  29. Should the website include a chatbot, live chat, or contact form?
  30. What is the expected typography hierarchy (e.g., H1, H2, H3 sizes and weights)?
  31. Do you need a favicon, social media assets, or other brand collateral designed?
  32. What is the preferred image style (e.g., illustrations, photography, 3D renders, abstract)?
  33. Should the website include user-generated content (e.g., testimonials, social media feeds)?
  34. What is the brand?s stance on pop-ups, interstitials, or overlays?
  35. Do you need a custom icon set designed for the website?
  36. What is the preferred spacing system (e.g., 8px grid, consistent margins, modular scales)?
  37. Should the website include a newsletter signup form or email capture functionality?
  38. What is the brand?s stance on social sharing buttons (e.g., mandatory, optional, none)?
  39. Do you need a project handoff document with design files, style guides, and technical specifications?
