Pricing Power: 30 No-Fluff Rules to Price Your Designs Like a Pro (Even as a Student)

pricing cheat sheet for design students ? 29 rules.

  1. Charge at least $25/hr for beginner design work to cover costs and time.
  2. Offer a 10% discount for students if they need a cheaper rate but still want professional work.
  3. Never work for free?always offer a discounted rate or a smaller project instead.
  4. Use a flat fee for small projects (e.g., $50 for a simple logo) to avoid hourly confusion.
  5. For social media graphics, charge $30?$50 per post depending on complexity and usage rights.
  6. Bundle services (e.g., 3 logos for $150) to increase perceived value and lock in clients.
  7. Always ask for a 50% deposit before starting any project to secure payment and commitment.
  8. Use a contract for every project, even small ones?include scope, timeline, and revision limits.
  9. Charge an extra $20?$50 for rush projects to compensate for tight deadlines.
  10. Offer a ?portfolio builder? package for $100?$200 to attract students and non-profits needing design work.
  11. Set a minimum project fee of $100 to avoid lowballing yourself on tiny tasks.
  12. Track your time meticulously for hourly projects?use Toggl or Clockify to justify rates.
  13. Raise your rates by 10?15% every 6 months to account for skill growth and inflation.
  14. Charge $150?$300 for a full branding package (logo, color palette, fonts) for student startups.
  15. For web design, charge $500?$1,000 depending on pages, custom graphics, and CMS setup.
  16. Never revise a design more than 3 times?include this in your contract to avoid scope creep.
  17. Offer a ?student discount? of 15% for classmates but limit it to one project per person.
  18. Use a tiered pricing model: Basic ($100), Pro ($200), and Premium ($350) for standard services.
  19. Charge an extra $50 for source file delivery (AI/PSD files) to cover additional work.
  20. For print design (flyers, brochures), charge $75?$150 based on size, color, and print readiness.
  21. Always include a 5?10% ?buffer? in quotes to account for unexpected revisions or changes.
  22. Charge $200?$400 for a full social media kit (cover photo, profile pic, post templates).
  23. Never accept ?exposure? as payment?this devalues your work and the design industry.
  24. Offer a ?first-time client? discount of 20% to attract new customers but don?t make it a habit.
  25. For UI/UX design, charge $30?$50 per wireframe or $200?$500 for a full prototype project.
  26. Always send a digital invoice with clear payment terms (e.g., ?Due within 7 days?).
  27. Charge $100?$200 for a one-time ?design audit? (feedback on an existing project).
  28. Use a ?kill fee? of 30% of the project cost if the client cancels mid-project.
  29. For motion graphics, charge $150?$300 per 30-second clip depending on complexity.
