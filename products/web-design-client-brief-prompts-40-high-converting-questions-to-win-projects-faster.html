Web Design Client Brief Prompts: 40 High-Converting Questions to Win Projects Faster

client brief prompts for web designers ? 38 prompts.

  1. What is your primary goal for this website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio)
  2. Who is your ideal customer? Describe their age, profession, and online behavior in detail.
  3. What are the top 3 features or functionalities you must have on this website?
  4. Do you have a preferred color palette or brand guidelines we should follow?
  5. Are there any competitor websites you admire? What specifically do you like about them?
  6. What tone should the website convey? (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist, bold)
  7. Will you need an e-commerce section? If yes, what payment gateways do you prefer?
  8. Do you have a sitemap or a list of pages you want included? Share it directly.
  9. What is your budget range for this project? (Be specific if possible.)
  10. What is your preferred timeline for launch? Include key milestones like design, development, and revisions.
  11. Do you have a hosting provider in mind, or should we recommend one?
  12. Will you need a content management system (CMS)? If yes, which one? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)
  13. What type of imagery or graphics do you want to use? (e.g., stock photos, custom illustrations, photography)
  14. Do you have a logo or branding assets we should incorporate?
  15. What is your preferred layout style? (e.g., grid-based, asymmetrical, single-page scroll)
  16. Will the website need to be mobile-responsive? If yes, what?s your priority for mobile UX?
  17. Do you have any technical requirements or integrations? (e.g., CRM, email marketing tool, API connections)
  18. What is your preferred font style? Share specific font names or categories you like.
  19. Do you have any specific animations or micro-interactions you want included?
  20. What is your preferred navigation structure? (e.g., mega menu, hamburger menu, sticky header)
  21. Will the website need a blog or news section? If yes, how should it be structured?
  22. Do you have any legal or compliance requirements? (e.g., GDPR, accessibility standards, privacy policy)
  23. What is your preferred approach to SEO? Do you have any keywords you want to target?
  24. Will you need to integrate social media feeds or sharing buttons?
  25. What is your preferred style for buttons and call-to-action elements?
  26. Do you have a style guide or brand book we should reference?
  27. What is your preferred approach to typography hierarchy? (e.g., H1, H2, body text sizes and weights)
  28. Will the website need a contact form? If yes, what fields should it include?
  29. What is your preferred approach to loading speed optimization?
  30. Do you have any specific security requirements? (e.g., SSL certificate, two-factor authentication)
  31. What is your preferred approach to accessibility? (e.g., color contrast, alt text for images)
  32. Will the website need a membership or login system? If yes, what features should it include?
  33. What is your preferred approach to cross-browser compatibility testing?
  34. Do you have any specific analytics or tracking tools you want integrated?
  35. What is your preferred approach to A/B testing and conversion optimization?
  36. Will the website need a booking or scheduling system? If yes, what features should it include?
  37. What is your preferred approach to error handling and user feedback messages?
  38. Do you have any specific requirements for the checkout process? (e.g., one-page checkout, multi-step checkout)
