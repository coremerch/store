Motion Designer's Pricing Cheat Sheet: 30 Rules to Price Projects Like a Pro

pricing cheat sheet for motion designers ? 29 rules.

  12. Charge $100?$300 per hour for custom motion graphics not covered by project rates.

  15. Price logo animations at $200?$800 depending on style (2D, 3D, kinetic typography).
  16. Charge $500?$1500 for title sequences in films or documentaries (per sequence).

  20. Charge $400?$1200 for kinetic typography videos (per minute).

  26. Charge $300?$1000 for animated GIFs (per GIF, based on complexity).

  29. Charge $1500?$5000 for full motion branding packages (logo, intro, lower thirds, social media).
