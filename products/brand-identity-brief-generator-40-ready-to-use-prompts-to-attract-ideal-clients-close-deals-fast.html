Brand Identity Brief Generator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Attract Ideal Clients & Close Deals Fast

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the company name, tagline, and mission statement?
  2. Describe the core values and personality traits of the brand (e.g., innovative, trustworthy, playful).
  3. Who are the primary and secondary target audiences (age, gender, profession, interests)?
  4. What are the top three adjectives the client wants their brand to evoke?
  5. What industries or competitors should the brand identity stand out from?
  6. What are the five key brand messages or key phrases that need to be communicated?
  7. What is the desired tone of voice for the brand (e.g., formal, casual, humorous, inspirational)?
  8. Are there any existing brand assets (logo, colors, fonts) that must be incorporated or updated?
  9. What are the client?s biggest pain points or challenges they want the new brand identity to solve?
  10. What is the client?s long-term vision for the brand (next 5-10 years)?
  11. What are the specific deliverables the client expects (logo, color palette, typography, brand guidelines, etc.)?
  12. What is the budget range for this project, and does it include revisions?
  13. What is the project timeline (start date, milestones, final delivery date)?
  14. Are there any cultural or regional considerations that should influence the design (e.g., symbolism, colors)?
  15. What are the client?s biggest competitors, and how should the new identity differentiate from them?
  16. What emotions should the brand identity evoke in the target audience?
  17. Are there any existing marketing materials (website, social media, packaging) that need to align with the new identity?
  18. What is the client?s preferred design style (minimalist, vintage, modern, grunge, etc.)?
  19. What are the client?s least favorite design trends or styles they want to avoid?
  20. What is the primary use case for the brand identity (digital, print, packaging, signage)?
  21. Are there any specific colors, fonts, or symbols the client is drawn to (even if just as a starting point)?
  22. What is the client?s stance on sustainability or ethical branding (e.g., eco-friendly materials, inclusive imagery)?
  23. Are there any legal or trademark considerations that could impact the design (e.g., existing trademarks)?
  24. What is the client?s preferred format for delivering the final brand identity (digital files, print-ready, interactive PDF)?
  25. What are the client?s expectations for the logo (e.g., wordmark, monogram, abstract, mascot)?
  26. Are there any specific industry standards or conventions the design must adhere to?
  27. What is the client?s preferred approach to typography (serif, sans-serif, script, custom)?
  28. Are there any specific imagery or photography styles the client wants to incorporate (e.g., candid, staged, illustrated)?
  29. What is the client?s stance on the use of negative space or minimalism in the design?
  30. What are the client?s thoughts on animated or motion elements for the brand identity?
  31. Are there any specific motifs, patterns, or textures the client wants to explore?
  32. What is the client?s preferred approach to color psychology (e.g., calming blues, energetic reds)?
  33. Are there any accessibility requirements (e.g., color contrast for readability, screen reader compatibility)?
  34. What is the client?s stance on the use of gradients, duotones, or bold color blocking?
  35. Are there any specific cultural references or local elements that should be included?
  36. What is the client?s preferred approach to consistency across different platforms (web, social media, print)?
  37. What are the client?s thoughts on the use of negative space or geometric shapes in the logo?
  38. Are there any specific metaphors or symbols the client wants to incorporate into the design?
  39. What is the client?s preferred approach to handling feedback and revisions during the design process?
