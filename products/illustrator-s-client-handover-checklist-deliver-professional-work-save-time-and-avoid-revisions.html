Illustrator?s Client Handover Checklist: Deliver Professional Work, Save Time, and Avoid Revisions

handover checklist for illustrators for hire ? 25 steps.

  1. Verify final illustration file format matches client contract (AI, EPS, SVG, or PNG at 300 DPI for print, 72 DPI for web).
  2. Include a layered source file (PSD, AI, or PDF with all editable layers, groups, and masks).
  3. Export a flattened high-resolution TIFF or JPEG for print-ready backup (CMYK color mode).
  4. Provide a web-optimized PNG at 1920px width with transparency for digital use.
  5. Deliver a transparent PNG cutout with no background (PNG-24 format).
  6. Include a color palette swatch file (ASE or AI Swatches) matching your illustration?s colors.
  7. Provide a style guide (PDF) listing Pantone, HEX, RGB, and CMYK color values used.
  8. Export and deliver a PDF presentation sheet with multiple illustration angles or variations.
  9. Send a low-resolution JPG preview (72 DPI, 1200px max) for quick client review.
  10. Include a readme.txt file with file naming conventions and usage instructions.
  11. Label all files clearly (e.g., ?ClientName_IllustrationName_Version_Date.extension?).
  12. Organize files in a zipped folder with a logical folder structure (e.g., /Finals, /Sources, /Exports).
  13. Double-check that all text is converted to outlines or provided in a separate editable file.
  14. Ensure no hidden layers, unused elements, or non-printable objects remain in source files.
  15. Confirm that the client?s logo or any branded elements are correctly integrated and positioned.
  16. Deliver a version with and without client-provided branding if modifications were made.
  17. Export a grayscale or duotone version if the client requested monochrome options.
  18. Include a vector version (AI/EPS) if the client needs scalability for future use.
  19. Provide a usage rights document (PDF) outlining licensing terms, attribution requirements, and restrictions.
  20. Send a final invoice with a clear breakdown of deliverables and any additional services rendered.
  21. Include a contact sheet with your name, email, website, and social media links for future collaborations.
  22. Deliver a short video or GIF (15-30 seconds) showing the illustration process or key features.
  23. Provide a checklist confirmation that the client has received and reviewed all files.
  24. Send a thank-you email with a follow-up timeline for feedback or additional requests.
  25. Offer a 30-day post-delivery support window for minor adjustments or clarifications.
