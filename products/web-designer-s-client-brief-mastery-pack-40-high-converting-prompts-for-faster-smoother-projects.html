Web Designer's Client Brief Mastery Pack: 40 High-Converting Prompts for Faster, Smoother Projects

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of this website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio)
  2. Who is the ideal target audience? Define age range, interests, and online behavior.
  3. What are the top 3 websites your client admires and why?
  4. List 3 must-have features the website must include from day one.
  5. What is the budget range for this project? (e.g., $1,000 - $5,000, $10,000+)
  6. What is the desired timeline for project completion? (e.g., 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months)
  7. Are there any existing branding guidelines (colors, fonts, logos) that must be followed?
  8. Does the client have a preferred content management system (CMS) like WordPress, Webflow, or Squarespace?
  9. What is the client?s preferred tone of voice? (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist, bold)
  10. Are there any competitors the client wants to outperform? Share website links if possible.
  11. What are the top 3 things the client does NOT want on their website?
  12. Does the client have a preference for mobile-first or desktop-first design approach?
  13. What is the primary call-to-action (CTA) the website should drive visitors toward? (e.g., Buy Now, Sign Up, Contact Us)
  14. Are there any specific pages that must be included? (e.g., Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact)
  15. Does the client have a style guide or mood board for inspiration?
  16. What is the client?s preferred navigation structure? (e.g., dropdown menus, mega menus, minimalist)
  17. Are there any third-party integrations required? (e.g., payment gateways, CRM tools, email marketing)
  18. Does the client need multilingual support or localization features?
  19. What are the key performance indicators (KPIs) the client will use to measure success? (e.g., bounce rate, conversion rate, time on site)
  20. Does the client have a preference for animations or micro-interactions? Provide examples if possible.
  21. Are there any legal or compliance requirements? (e.g., GDPR, accessibility standards like WCAG)
  22. Does the client need a blog section? If so, how often will they post content?
  23. What is the client?s preferred method of communication? (e.g., Slack, email, weekly calls)
  24. Are there any specific images, videos, or graphics the client wants to use? Provide links or examples.
  25. Does the client need a custom 404 error page or other error page designs?
  26. What is the client?s preferred loading speed benchmark? (e.g., under 2 seconds, under 1 second)
  27. Are there any specific SEO requirements? (e.g., keyword targeting, meta descriptions, schema markup)
  28. Does the client need a newsletter signup form or email capture mechanism?
  29. What is the client?s preferred method for handling user feedback or reviews?
  30. Are there any specific font pairings or typography preferences?
  31. Does the client need a sitemap or wireframe before the design phase begins?
  32. What is the client?s preferred method for handling hosting and domain setup?
  33. Are there any specific security features required? (e.g., SSL certificate, firewall, backup system)
  34. Does the client need a membership or login system? If so, what level of access?
  35. What is the client?s preferred method for handling social media integration?
  36. Are there any specific color palette preferences or restrictions?
  37. Does the client need a custom favicon or app icon design?
  38. What is the client?s preferred method for handling e-commerce functionality? (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce, custom)
  39. Are there any specific accessibility features required? (e.g., high contrast mode, screen reader compatibility)
  40. Does the client need a custom dashboard or user portal for their audience?
