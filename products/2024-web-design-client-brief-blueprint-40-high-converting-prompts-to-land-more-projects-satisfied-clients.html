2024 Web Design Client Brief Blueprint: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land More Projects & Satisfied Clients

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of the website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio, inform visitors)
  2. Who is the ideal target audience for this website? (Include demographics like age, profession, income level, and online behavior)
  3. What are the top 3 competitors in this industry, and how does this website need to differentiate itself?
  4. What are the key brand elements (colors, fonts, tone of voice, logo usage) that must be reflected in the design?
  5. What is the desired user experience (UX) flow? Map out the main user journey from landing to conversion.
  6. What specific pages will the website include? (e.g., Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Blog, Contact, FAQ)
  7. What type of content will each page feature? (e.g., text, images, videos, testimonials, case studies, pricing tables)
  8. What is the tone of the content? (e.g., professional, friendly, humorous, minimalist, corporate)
  9. What is the website?s core message or unique selling proposition (USP) that must be communicated immediately?
  10. What is the preferred navigation style? (e.g., horizontal menu, hamburger menu, sticky header, mega menu)
  11. Where should the main call-to-action (CTA) buttons be placed for maximum engagement?
  12. What are the specific features required? (e.g., contact forms, booking system, e-commerce integration, live chat, social media feeds)
  13. What are the integrations needed with third-party tools? (e.g., CRM, email marketing, analytics, payment gateways)
  14. What is the preferred layout structure for the homepage? (e.g., hero section, features grid, testimonial slider, CTA banner)
  15. How many images or videos will be provided by the client, and what style are they looking for?
  16. What is the desired loading speed target for the website? (e.g., under 2 seconds, optimized for mobile)
  17. What are the SEO requirements? (e.g., keyword strategy, meta titles, descriptions, alt text for images)
  18. What is the budget range for this project, and how does it align with the scope of work?
  19. What is the expected timeline for completion? Include key milestones (e.g., design approval, development, testing, launch).
  20. What is the client?s preferred communication method and frequency? (e.g., weekly updates, Slack, email, video calls)
  21. Does the client have any existing design assets (logos, brand guidelines, style guides) that must be incorporated?
  22. What is the client?s stance on animations and micro-interactions? (e.g., hover effects, scroll animations, loading spinners)
  23. Are there any specific design trends the client wants to incorporate or avoid? (e.g., dark mode, glassmorphism, brutalism)
  24. What is the client?s preference for mobile responsiveness? (e.g., mobile-first design, progressive enhancement)
  25. Does the client require a blog or news section? If so, what categories and posting frequency?
  26. What is the client?s approach to accessibility? (e.g., WCAG compliance, screen reader compatibility, keyboard navigation)
  27. Are there any legal requirements to consider? (e.g., GDPR, cookie consent, terms and conditions, privacy policy)
  28. What is the client?s preferred color palette, and are there any colors to avoid?
  29. What type of typography hierarchy is needed? (e.g., heading styles, body text, accent fonts, Google Fonts integration)
  30. Does the client want a CMS (Content Management System) integrated, and if so, which one? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)
  31. What is the client?s stance on social media integration? (e.g., embedded feeds, share buttons, social login)
  32. Are there any specific animations or illustrations required for storytelling or engagement?
  33. What is the client?s backup plan for website maintenance post-launch? (e.g., updates, security patches, hosting provider)
  34. Does the client require multilingual support, and if so, which languages?
  35. What is the client?s preferred hosting solution? (e.g., shared, VPS, dedicated, cloud-based)
  36. Are there any specific security measures needed? (e.g., SSL certificate, firewalls, regular backups)
  37. What is the client?s plan for analytics and tracking? (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar, heatmaps)
  38. Does the client want a coming soon or pre-launch page for marketing purposes?
  39. What is the client?s post-launch support request? (e.g., training, troubleshooting, updates)
  40. Are there any industry-specific standards or certifications the website must comply with? (e.g., medical, legal, e-commerce)
