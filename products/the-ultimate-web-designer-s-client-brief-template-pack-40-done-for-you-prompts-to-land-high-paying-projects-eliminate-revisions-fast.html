client brief prompts for web designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of the website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio)
  2. Who is the ideal customer persona for this website? (age, occupation, pain points)
  3. What are the top 3 competitors in this industry, and what makes their website stand out?
  4. What is the client?s brand personality? (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist, bold)
  5. What are 5 keywords or phrases the target audience uses to find this business?
  6. What color palette does the client prefer? Provide hex codes or Pantone references.
  7. Are there any specific fonts the client wants to use? If not, what mood should the typography convey?
  8. What is the client?s budget range for this project? (e.g., $3K?$5K, $10K?$15K)
  9. What is the project timeline? (e.g., launch by Q3, 4-week deadline)
  10. Does the client have an existing style guide or brand assets? (logo, mockups, previous designs)
  11. What are the top 3 features the website must include? (e.g., booking system, e-commerce, blog)
  12. Are there any functionalities the client wants to avoid? (e.g., no animations, no parallax)
  13. What is the client?s preferred layout style? (e.g., grid-based, asymmetrical, magazine-style)
  14. Does the client have a preference for mobile-first design or desktop-first?
  15. What is the client?s stance on pop-ups, sliders, or auto-playing media?
  16. Are there any industry-specific design trends the client wants to incorporate? (e.g., dark mode, glassmorphism)
  17. What is the client?s preferred tone for the copy? (e.g., formal, conversational, humorous)
  18. Does the client have a preferred CMS? (WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, custom)
  19. What is the client?s stance on accessibility? (e.g., WCAG compliance, keyboard navigation)
  20. Are there any legal or compliance requirements? (e.g., GDPR, ADA, COPPA)
  21. Does the client have a preferred hosting provider? (e.g., SiteGround, AWS, Vercel)
  22. What is the client?s plan for ongoing maintenance and updates?
  23. Are there any third-party integrations required? (e.g., Mailchimp, Stripe, Zapier)
  24. Does the client want a blog or news section? If so, how often will it be updated?
  25. What is the client?s preferred navigation structure? (e.g., mega menu, hamburger menu, sticky nav)
  26. Are there any specific pages the client wants to prioritize? (e.g., About Us, Contact, Pricing)
  27. Does the client want a custom 404 page? If so, what should it include?
  28. What is the client?s preferred approach to micro-interactions? (e.g., hover effects, button animations)
  29. Are there any specific imagery or illustration styles the client wants to use?
  30. Does the client want a dark mode toggle? If so, should it be manual or automatic?
  31. What is the client?s stance on video backgrounds or hero sections?
  32. Are there any specific call-to-action (CTA) colors or styles the client prefers?
  33. Does the client want a newsletter signup form? If so, where should it be placed?
  34. What is the client?s preferred approach to social media integration? (e.g., embedded feeds, share buttons)
  35. Are there any specific animations or transitions the client wants to include?
  36. Does the client want a custom favicon and social media preview images?
  37. What is the client?s preferred method for collecting user feedback? (e.g., surveys, contact forms)
  38. Are there any specific user personas or journeys the client wants the designer to consider?
  39. Does the client want a membership or login system? If so, what?s the purpose?
