Web Design Client Brief Generator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Clear, Actionable Project Briefs

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of the website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, provide information)
  2. Who is the target audience? Include demographics like age, gender, location, and interests.
  3. What are the key competitors in the industry, and what are their websites missing that we can improve?
  4. What is the unique value proposition (UVP) of the client?s business or website?
  5. What are the top 3 functionalities the website must have? (e.g., contact forms, e-commerce integration, booking system)
  6. Does the website need to include a blog or news section for content marketing?
  7. What is the preferred style or aesthetic for the website? (e.g., minimalist, modern, retro, corporate, playful)
  8. Are there any specific brand colors or fonts that must be used?
  9. Should the design be responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop, or is mobile-first a priority?
  10. What is the expected loading speed requirement for the website? (e.g., under 2 seconds for key pages)
  11. Are there any specific design elements that must be avoided? (e.g., no animations, no autoplay videos)
  12. What is the budget range for this project?
  13. What is the proposed timeline for completion? Include milestones for design, development, and testing.
  14. Does the client have an existing style guide or brand book we should reference?
  15. Are there any third-party integrations required? (e.g., payment gateways, CRM tools, social media APIs)
  16. What is the preferred content management system (CMS)? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, custom-built)
  17. Does the website need to support multilingual content?
  18. Are there any accessibility requirements? (e.g., WCAG 2.1 compliance, screen reader optimization)
  19. What is the client?s preferred approach to user experience (UX)? (e.g., card sorting, user personas, usability testing)
  20. Are there any specific navigation requirements? (e.g., mega menus, sticky headers, hamburger menus)
  21. What is the desired tone of voice for the website copy? (e.g., formal, casual, humorous, inspirational)
  22. Does the website need to include a customer testimonial or review section?
  23. Are there any legal or compliance requirements to consider? (e.g., GDPR, cookie consent banners)
  24. What is the preferred hosting provider for the website?
  25. Does the client want to include a live chat or chatbot feature?
  26. Are there any specific SEO requirements for the website? (e.g., keyword targeting, meta tags, schema markup)
  27. What is the client?s preferred approach to visual hierarchy? (e.g., bold headlines, large images, whitespace)
  28. Does the website need to include a membership or login system?
  29. What is the expected level of interactivity for the website? (e.g., hover effects, parallax scrolling, micro-interactions)
  30. Are there any specific image or video requirements? (e.g., stock photos, custom illustrations, video backgrounds)
  31. Does the client want to include a newsletter signup form?
  32. What is the preferred method for gathering client feedback during the design process? (e.g., Figma comments, email, video calls)
  33. Are there any specific security requirements? (e.g., SSL certificates, secure payment processing, data encryption)
  34. Does the website need to include a search functionality with autocomplete?
  35. What is the client?s preferred approach to typography? (e.g., Google Fonts, custom typography, limited font choices)
  36. Are there any specific call-to-action (CTA) buttons that must be prominently displayed?
  37. Does the client want to include a progress tracker or funnel for user journeys? (e.g., for e-commerce checkout)
  38. What is the client?s preferred method for analytics tracking? (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar, custom dashboards)
  39. Are there any specific social media integrations required? (e.g., Instagram feed, Facebook pixel)
  40. What is the client?s preferred method for handling ongoing updates and maintenance after launch?
