Web Design Client Brief Generator: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Secure Lucrative Projects Fast

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. List the client's top 3 business goals for this website redesign (e.g., increase leads by 30%, improve brand credibility).
  2. Identify the primary audience personas for the website (include age, job title, pain points, and online behavior).
  3. Specify the client?s top 3 competitors and the exact elements they want to outperform (e.g., faster load time, better mobile UX).
  4. Clarify the client?s preferred style direction (e.g., minimalist, bold, playful, corporate) with 2-3 visual examples.
  5. Define the client?s brand voice and tone (e.g., professional, friendly, authoritative) with 3 adjectives and a sentence explaining each.
  6. List the client?s existing website?s 3 biggest usability issues (e.g., confusing navigation, slow checkout process).
  7. Identify the client?s must-have pages (e.g., Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact) and their purpose (e.g., ?Services page to showcase portfolio?).
  8. Specify the client?s call-to-action (CTA) strategy for the homepage (e.g., ?Sign up for a free trial,? ?Download our eBook?).
  9. Determine the client?s preferred layout structure for key pages (e.g., hero section with video, grid of services, testimonials section).
  10. Clarify the client?s content needs (e.g., copy length, tone, whether they have existing content or need it written).
  11. List the client?s brand colors (hex codes) and any colors they want to avoid, plus 2-3 color palettes they like.
  12. Specify the client?s preferred typography (e.g., fonts for headings, body text, and accent text) with 2-3 font pairings they love.
  13. Identify the client?s image style preferences (e.g., realistic photos, illustrated graphics, abstract backgrounds) with 3 examples.
  14. Clarify the client?s accessibility requirements (e.g., WCAG compliance level, color contrast, alt text for images).
  15. Determine the client?s preferred navigation structure (e.g., 5-item main menu, dropdown submenus, sticky header).
  16. List the client?s social media links and how they want them integrated (e.g., top-right corner, footer, inline with content).
  17. Specify the client?s blog or news section requirements (e.g., layout, featured images, sidebar widgets, sharing options).
  18. Clarify the client?s contact page preferences (e.g., form fields, map integration, hours of operation display).
  19. Identify the client?s eCommerce needs (if applicable) including payment gateways, product categories, and checkout flow preferences.
  20. Determine the client?s SEO goals (e.g., target keywords, on-page SEO requirements, blog integration strategy).
  21. List the client?s integrations with third-party tools (e.g., Mailchimp, HubSpot, Zapier) and where they should appear on the site.
  22. Specify the client?s hosting and domain preferences (e.g., WordPress.org, Squarespace, custom-built) and budget for ongoing costs.
  23. Clarify the client?s timeline for the project (e.g., ?Launch in 6 weeks,? ?Phased rollout over 3 months?).
  24. Determine the client?s budget range for design, development, and ongoing maintenance (e.g., ?$5K?$10K for design,? ?$2K/year for updates?).
  25. Identify the client?s preferred project management tool (e.g., Trello, Asana, Slack) and communication frequency (e.g., weekly check-ins).
  26. List the client?s existing branding assets (e.g., logo files, brand guidelines, past design files) and whether they?re reusable.
  27. Clarify the client?s mobile-first or desktop-first design preference and any specific mobile UX requirements (e.g., touch-friendly buttons).
  28. Specify the client?s use of animations or micro-interactions (e.g., hover effects, loading spinners, parallax scrolling).
  29. Determine the client?s content management system (CMS) preference (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, custom-built) and ease-of-use needs.
  30. List the client?s legal requirements (e.g., privacy policy, terms of service, GDPR compliance, cookie consent banner).
  31. Identify the client?s security preferences (e.g., SSL certificate, regular backups, security plugins for eCommerce).
  32. Clarify the client?s analytics and tracking needs (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar, Facebook Pixel) and reporting frequency.
  33. Specify the client?s preferred visual hierarchy for key pages (e.g., ?Hero section should take 60% of the screen, CTA button in contrasting color?).
  34. List the client?s ideas for unique or differentiating features (e.g., interactive product configurator, live chat widget, membership portal).
  35. Determine the client?s backup and disaster recovery plan (e.g., ?Weekly automated backups stored on cloud,? ?Manual backups before major updates?).
  36. Clarify the client?s expectations for post-launch support (e.g., ?3 months of free updates,? ?Priority email support for 6 months?).
  37. Identify the client?s social proof elements to include (e.g., client logos, testimonials, case studies, trust badges).
  38. Specify the client?s use of video content (e.g., background video, product demos, explainer videos) and hosting preferences (e.g., Vimeo, YouTube, self-hosted).
  39. List the client?s off-page elements to consider (e.g., favicon, Apple touch icon, social media preview images, 404 page design).
  40. Clarify the client?s expectations for responsive design (e.g., ?All pages must look pixel-perfect on mobile, tablet, and desktop?).
