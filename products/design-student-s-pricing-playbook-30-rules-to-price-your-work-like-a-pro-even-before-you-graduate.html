Design Student?s Pricing Playbook: 30 Rules to Price Your Work Like a Pro (Even Before You Graduate)

pricing cheat sheet for design students ? 30 rules.

  1. Always quote per project, not per hour?your portfolio value grows faster than your experience.
  2. Break pricing into three tiers: student discount (50% off), standard (20% off market rate), and premium (full rate for fast turnaround).
  3. Use a flat fee for logo design: $150 for students, $400 for standard, $750 for premium?adjust based on complexity.
  4. Charge $50?$150/hour for illustration work, with a 20% premium for urgent deadlines.
  5. Set a minimum project fee of $200 for branding packages to avoid undervaluing your time.
  6. Offer a 10% discount for upfront payment?cash flow matters more than you think.
  7. Never accept ?exposure? as payment?redirect to a $500+ project fee instead.
  8. Price social media templates at $75?$200 each, depending on customization depth.
  9. Charge $300?$800 for a full website (5?10 pages) on Squarespace/Wix, with a 30% rush fee.
  10. Use a 1.5x multiplier for last-minute requests?your sleep is worth more than a client?s urgency.
  11. Bundle services: Offer ?Brand Starter? for $800 (logo + business card) and ?Brand Pro? for $1,500 (logo + website + templates).
  12. Price print design at $100?$300 per piece, with a 25% premium for CMYK setup and bleed.
  13. Charge $150?$400 for a single social media post design (static or carousel).
  14. Set a $50 fee for file organization and delivery?clients love ?free? files, but they?ll pay for clarity.
  15. Offer a 15% referral bonus to clients who bring you new work?turn happy customers into recruiters.
  16. Price motion graphics at $200?$500 per 10-second clip, depending on complexity.
  17. Never negotiate below your minimum?if a client insists, walk away or increase scope.
  18. Use a deposit system: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery?protects you from non-payment scams.
  19. Charge $200?$600 for a full social media calendar (30 days of content) with 3 variations per post.
  20. Price UX/UI audits at $250?$750, with a 10% discount for students who include a redesign proposal.
  21. Offer a ?Student Rescue? package for $300 (3 logo concepts + 1 revision) to help peers build portfolios.
  22. Set a $100 fee for color palette and typography guides?sell your process, not just the deliverables.
  23. Charge $150?$400 for a full brand style guide (PDF + editable files), with a 20% rush fee.
  24. Use a sliding scale for nonprofits: 60% of your standard rate, but cap at $500 per project.
  25. Price portfolio website design at $1,000?$2,500, with a 15% discount for early-bird students.
  26. Offer a ?Quick Fix? service for $50/hour to tweak existing designs?great for clients with tight budgets.
  27. Charge $200?$500 for a full package of 5?10 custom icons, with a 25% premium for animated versions.
  28. Set a $75 fee for a one-hour brand strategy call?position yourself as a consultant, not just a designer.
  29. Always include a 14-day revision window in contracts?avoid endless ?tweaks? after delivery.
  30. Use a ?kill fee? of 30?50% for projects canceled mid-flow?protects your time and sunk costs.
