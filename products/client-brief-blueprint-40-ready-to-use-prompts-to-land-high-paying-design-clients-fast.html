Client Brief Blueprint: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Land High-Paying Design Clients Fast

client brief prompts for agency escapees going solo ? 40 prompts.

  1. Describe your ideal client in 3 sentences: their industry, company size, and biggest design challenge.
  2. List 5 specific design projects your ideal client has completed in the last year.
  3. What?s the #1 problem your ideal client faces when hiring designers? Provide a direct quote if possible.
  4. Write the exact job title of the person who approves your design work (e.g., ?Marketing Director?).
  5. What?s the most common objection your ideal client has to outsourcing design? How can you preempt it?
  6. Describe the tone your ideal client uses in their marketing?provide 3 sample sentences from their website.
  7. What?s the average project budget range your ideal client typically spends on design? (Be specific: $1K?$5K, $5K?$10K, etc.)
  8. List 3 pain points your ideal client mentions in their LinkedIn posts or company blog.
  9. What?s the first question your ideal client asks when they reach out to a designer? Write it verbatim.
  10. Describe the decision-making process for hiring a designer?who else is involved besides the primary contact?
  11. What?s the most frustrating part of working with in-house designers according to your ideal client? Cite a review or testimonial.
  12. Write a 1-sentence elevator pitch that your ideal client would use to describe their business to a stranger.
  13. List 3 competitors of your ideal client?how do they differentiate themselves in their branding?
  14. What?s the most expensive marketing channel your ideal client invests in? (e.g., out-of-home ads, PPC, influencer campaigns)
  15. Describe the style of design your ideal client currently uses?provide 3 examples from their website or social media.
  16. What?s the biggest gap in their current design strategy? (e.g., lack of animation, poor typography, inconsistent branding)
  17. Write the exact email subject line your ideal client would use to request a design proposal.
  18. What?s the most common deadline pressure your ideal client faces? (e.g., product launches, seasonal campaigns, quarterly reports)
  19. Describe the onboarding process your ideal client prefers?do they want a quick call, a detailed proposal, or a portfolio review first?
  20. List 3 tools or software your ideal client uses daily?how can your design process integrate with them?
  21. What?s the most underutilized design asset your ideal client has? (e.g., their Instagram Stories, email templates, or packaging)
  22. Write a 1-paragraph case study of a time your ideal client succeeded because of great design?use their own words.
  23. What?s the biggest risk your ideal client fears when outsourcing design? How can you address it preemptively?
  24. Describe the most frequent feedback loop your ideal client has with their design team?what?s the turnaround time for revisions?
  25. What?s the most overlooked design opportunity in your ideal client?s industry? Provide a competitor example.
  26. List 3 questions your ideal client would ask in a design discovery call?answer them proactively in your brief.
  27. What?s the most expensive design mistake your ideal client has made in the past? How can you prevent them from repeating it?
  28. Write the exact budget breakdown your ideal client would expect for a [specific project type, e.g., website redesign].
  29. Describe the most common design deliverable your ideal client requests?provide 3 examples from their past projects.
  30. What?s the biggest time-waster in your ideal client?s current design workflow? How can you streamline it?
  31. List 3 KPIs your ideal client tracks for design projects?how will your work impact those metrics?
  32. Describe the most effective way to showcase your work to your ideal client?do they prefer live demos, PDFs, or video walkthroughs?
  33. What?s the most common contract or legal concern your ideal client has when hiring freelancers?
  34. Write the exact timeline your ideal client would expect for a [specific project type, e.g., logo refresh].
  35. What?s the most expensive part of your ideal client?s design budget? (e.g., stock assets, software, revisions)
  36. Describe the most frustrating part of their current design process?provide a direct quote from their team if possible.
  37. List 3 ways your ideal client measures the success of a design project?how will you tie your deliverables to those?
  38. What?s the most common scope creep issue your ideal client has encountered? How can you prevent it in your projects?
  39. Write the exact follow-up email subject line your ideal client would use to check on a project?s progress.
  40. Describe the most underused design channel your ideal client has?how can you propose a project to leverage it?
