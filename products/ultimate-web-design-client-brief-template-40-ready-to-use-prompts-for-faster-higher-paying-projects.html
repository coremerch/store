Ultimate Web Design Client Brief Template: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Faster, Higher-Paying Projects

client brief prompts for web designers ? 39 prompts.

  1. What are the primary business goals for this website? (e.g., lead generation, e-commerce sales, brand awareness)
  2. Who is the ideal target audience for this website? Provide demographics (age, gender, location) and psychographics (interests, pain points).
  3. What are the top 3 competitors? websites you admire, and why?
  4. List 3-5 must-have features this website must include to stand out from competitors.
  5. What is the desired user flow for the homepage? Sketch or describe key steps from landing to conversion.
  6. What tone of voice should the website reflect? (e.g., professional, casual, humorous, inspirational)
  7. Provide 2-3 examples of websites with a similar aesthetic or style you like.
  8. What are the brand colors (hex codes) and fonts (typefaces and weights) you want to use?
  9. Do you have a brand style guide or mood board we should reference?
  10. What is the primary call-to-action (CTA) for the homepage? (e.g., 'Sign Up Now', 'Shop Today')
  11. How should the navigation menu be structured? List all top-level and secondary pages.
  12. Do you have any existing content (text, images, videos) to repurpose for this site?
  13. What is the preferred layout style for key pages? (e.g., minimalist, grid-based, asymmetrical)
  14. Should the website be mobile-first, desktop-first, or responsive by default?
  15. What are the key performance indicators (KPIs) for this project? (e.g., bounce rate < 30%, conversion rate > 5%)
  16. Do you have any technical requirements or integrations needed? (e.g., API, CRM, payment gateway)
  17. What is the expected timeline for this project? Include milestones for design, development, and launch.
  18. Who will be the main point of contact for feedback and approvals?
  19. What is the budget range for this project? (e.g., $2,000?$5,000, $10,000+)
  20. Do you have any accessibility standards this website must meet? (e.g., WCAG 2.1 AA, screen reader compatibility)
  21. Should the website include a blog or news section? If so, who will manage content updates?
  22. What are the top 3 things you dislike about your current website (if applicable)?
  23. Do you have a preferred content management system (CMS)? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)
  24. What is the preferred hosting provider or domain registrar for this project?
  25. Should the website include social media integration? If so, which platforms?
  26. What is the desired loading speed for the website? (e.g., under 2 seconds, under 1 second)
  27. Do you have any legal or compliance requirements? (e.g., GDPR, cookie consent, terms of service)
  28. What is the preferred method for collecting user feedback? (e.g., surveys, heatmaps, analytics)
  29. Should the website include a newsletter signup or lead magnet? If so, what incentive will be offered?
  30. What is the desired color palette for the website? Provide 3-5 color options or a brand guide.
  31. Do you have any existing brand assets (logos, icons, illustrations) that must be used?
  32. What is the preferred image style for the website? (e.g., minimalist, candid, stock photos)
  33. Should the website include animations or micro-interactions? If so, provide examples.
  34. What is the desired button and form styling? (e.g., rounded corners, gradient, shadow effects)
  35. Do you have any specific SEO requirements? (e.g., keyword targets, meta descriptions, alt text)
  36. What is the preferred method for handling 404 errors and broken links?
  37. Should the website include a FAQ or help section? If so, what topics should be covered?
  38. What is the desired approach for handling mobile responsiveness? (e.g., adaptive, fluid, mobile-first)
  39. Do you have any additional notes, constraints, or wildcards for this project?
