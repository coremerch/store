50 High-Converting Client Brief Prompts to Attract Premium Brand Identity Projects

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the core mission of the brand in one sentence?
  2. List the top 3 adjectives that describe the brand?s desired personality.
  3. Describe the target audience?s demographics in detail (age, profession, income level, interests).
  4. What are the three most important values the brand wants to communicate through its identity?
  5. Provide 3-5 real-world brands that the client admires for their visual identity (include links if possible).
  6. Specify the primary color palette the client envisions (provide hex codes).
  7. What emotions or associations should the brand?s visual identity evoke in its audience?
  8. Does the client have an existing brand identity? If yes, describe its strengths and weaknesses.
  9. What is the brand?s tagline or slogan (if it has one)?
  10. List any specific cultural, historical, or industry references the brand wants to incorporate.
  11. What is the brand?s tone of voice (e.g., professional, playful, authoritative, friendly)?
  12. Describe the client?s ideal customer persona in detail (include pain points and aspirations).
  13. Are there any logos or symbols the client wants to avoid in the new identity?
  14. What is the primary medium where the brand identity will be used (e.g., digital, print, packaging, signage)?
  15. List 3-5 keywords that describe the brand?s desired first impression.
  16. Does the client have a preference for minimalist, maximalist, or somewhere-in-between design styles?
  17. What is the brand?s current market position (e.g., luxury, budget-friendly, niche, mainstream)?
  18. Are there any specific fonts or typography styles the client dislikes?
  19. Describe the client?s long-term brand vision (where do they see the brand in 5 years?).
  20. What is the budget range for this project?
  21. Does the client have a preference for hand-drawn, digital, or hybrid illustration styles?
  22. List any competitors the client wants to differentiate from visually.
  23. What is the expected timeline for this project (e.g., 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months)?
  24. Are there any specific cultural or regional design elements the brand wants to incorporate?
  25. What is the primary goal of this rebrand (e.g., refresh outdated look, attract younger audience, reposition brand)?
  26. Describe the client?s ideal client?s online behavior (e.g., social media platforms they use, content they engage with).
  27. Does the client have any existing brand guidelines? If yes, attach them for reference.
  28. What is the brand?s unique selling proposition (USP) in one sentence?
  29. List any symbols, icons, or imagery that the client wants to avoid in the new identity.
  30. What is the desired level of complexity for the logo (e.g., simple monogram, detailed illustration, abstract)?
  31. Describe the client?s ideal customer journey from first awareness to purchase.
  32. Are there any specific design trends the client wants to avoid?
  33. What is the brand?s current visual identity lacking, in the client?s opinion?
  34. List any specific design elements the client wants to include (e.g., gradients, duotones, 3D effects).
  35. What is the brand?s secondary color palette (if applicable)?
  36. Describe the client?s ideal brand experience for their customers (e.g., premium, accessible, exclusive).
  37. Are there any specific file formats or deliverables the client requires (e.g., vector files, social media kits, brand books)?
  38. What is the client?s biggest concern about this rebrand project?
  39. List any inspirational mood boards, Pinterest links, or reference images provided by the client.
  40. What is the brand?s current visual identity (attach examples if possible)?
