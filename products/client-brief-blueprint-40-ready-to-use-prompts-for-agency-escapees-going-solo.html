Client Brief Blueprint: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Agency Escapees Going Solo

client brief prompts for agency escapees going solo ? 39 prompts.

  1. What is the client?s primary business goal for this project? (e.g., increase sales, launch a product, rebrand)
  2. What is the client?s budget range for this project? (Be specific: e.g., $2,000?$5,000)
  3. What is the client?s timeline for this project? (Include key milestones and deadlines)
  4. Who are the client?s top 3 competitors? (Provide links to their websites or portfolios)
  5. What are the client?s 3 biggest pain points or challenges they want to solve with this project?
  6. Who is the primary decision-maker for this project? (Name, title, and contact info)
  7. Who else will be involved in the approval process? (List roles and responsibilities)
  8. Does the client have a preferred style or aesthetic? (Provide examples or mood boards)
  9. Are there any design styles the client explicitly wants to avoid? (Include examples if possible)
  10. What is the target audience for this project? (Demographics, psychographics, and key behaviors)
  11. What are the client?s 3 main objectives for the design? (e.g., modern, professional, playful)
  12. Does the client have an existing brand identity? (Include logo, colors, fonts, and guidelines)
  13. Does the client want to update or refresh an existing design? (If yes, what elements should stay or change?)
  14. What platforms will this design be used for? (e.g., website, social media, print, packaging)
  15. What is the expected deliverable format? (e.g., AI files, PDF, PNG, video)
  16. Does the client need multiple versions or variations of the design? (Specify: e.g., desktop vs. mobile, dark mode vs. light mode)
  17. Are there any third-party integrations or technical requirements? (e.g., APIs, CMS platforms)
  18. What is the client?s preferred communication method? (e.g., email, Slack, phone calls)
  19. How often does the client want progress updates? (e.g., weekly, biweekly, only at milestones)
  20. Does the client have a preferred file-sharing method? (e.g., Dropbox, Google Drive, WeTransfer)
  21. Are there any legal or compliance requirements for this design? (e.g., copyright, accessibility standards)
  22. Does the client have a content strategy in place? (e.g., copy, images, videos to be provided)
  23. What tone or voice should the design convey? (e.g., formal, casual, humorous, authoritative)
  24. Does the client have any specific imagery or photography in mind? (Provide examples or references)
  25. Are there any branding elements that must be included in the design? (e.g., taglines, slogans, logos)
  26. Does the client want a responsive design? (If yes, specify breakpoints for mobile, tablet, and desktop)
  27. What is the client?s preferred color palette? (Provide hex codes or examples)
  28. Does the client have a preferred font or typeface? (Include examples or links)
  29. Are there any specific icons or illustrations the client wants to use? (Provide references or sketches)
  30. Does the client need the design to be scalable? (e.g., for future expansion or multiple uses)
  31. What is the client?s preferred layout or grid system? (e.g., single-column, multi-column, asymmetrical)
  32. Does the client have any specific calls-to-action (CTAs) they want included? (e.g., 'Buy Now,' 'Sign Up')
  33. Are there any animations or interactive elements the client wants to include? (e.g., hover effects, transitions)
  34. Does the client want the design to be minimalist, maximalist, or somewhere in between?
  35. What is the client?s preferred aspect ratio for the design? (e.g., 16:9, 4:3, square)
  36. Does the client want the design to be print-ready? (If yes, specify DPI, bleed, and color mode)
  37. Are there any specific user experience (UX) requirements? (e.g., navigation flow, user journey maps)
  38. Does the client want the design to be SEO-friendly? (If yes, specify keywords or SEO goals)
  39. What is the client?s preferred method for receiving feedback? (e.g., annotations, comments, live reviews)
