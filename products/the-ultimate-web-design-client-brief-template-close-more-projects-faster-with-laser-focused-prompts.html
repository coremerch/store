The Ultimate Web Design Client Brief Template: Close More Projects Faster with Laser-Focused Prompts

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of the website? (e.g., sell products, generate leads, showcase portfolio, provide information)
  2. Who is the target audience of the website? Include demographics, interests, and pain points.
  3. What are the top 3 competitors in the industry? Provide URLs and describe their key design and UX features.
  4. Does the client have existing brand guidelines? Provide the brand style guide, logo files, and color palette.
  5. What is the preferred tone and style of the website? (e.g., modern, minimalist, playful, corporate)
  6. What is the budget range for this project? (e.g., $2,000 - $5,000, $5,000 - $10,000)
  7. What is the expected timeline for the project? Include key milestones (e.g., discovery, design, development, launch).
  8. Are there any specific features or functionalities required? (e.g., e-commerce, booking system, membership portal)
  9. Does the client need a content management system (CMS)? If yes, which one? (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)
  10. What are the primary and secondary navigation menus? Provide a list of pages and their hierarchy.
  11. Are there any specific pages that need to be prioritized or designed first? (e.g., homepage, about, contact)
  12. Does the client have any existing website analytics data? Provide access to Google Analytics or other tracking tools.
  13. What is the preferred layout structure for the homepage? (e.g., hero section, features grid, testimonials, call-to-action)
  14. Are there any specific images, icons, or illustrations the client wants to include? Provide examples or mood boards.
  15. Does the client have a preferred typography style? Provide font families and sizes for headings, body text, and buttons.
  16. What is the color scheme preference? Provide HEX codes or Pantone colors if available.
  17. Are there any accessibility requirements? (e.g., WCAG compliance, screen reader optimization)
  18. Does the client have any legal or compliance requirements? (e.g., GDPR, cookie consent, privacy policy)
  19. What is the expected number of revisions included in the project scope?
  20. Are there any third-party integrations required? (e.g., payment gateways, CRM, email marketing tools)
  21. Does the client have a preferred hosting provider or domain registrar?
  22. What is the client?s preferred method of communication? (e.g., email, Slack, Trello, Zoom)
  23. Are there any specific design trends or styles the client dislikes? (e.g., parallax scrolling, neon colors)
  24. Does the client have any existing social media profiles? If yes, provide URLs for reference.
  25. What is the client?s preferred approach to mobile responsiveness? (e.g., mobile-first design, desktop-first design)
  26. Are there any specific call-to-action (CTA) buttons or elements the client wants to highlight?
  27. Does the client have any specific SEO requirements? (e.g., keyword integration, meta tags, alt text)
  28. Are there any specific animations or micro-interactions the client wants to include?
  29. What is the client?s preferred method for collecting user feedback? (e.g., surveys, heatmaps, user testing)
  30. Does the client have any specific security requirements? (e.g., SSL certificates, secure payment processing)
  31. Are there any specific loading speed requirements? (e.g., under 2 seconds for homepages)
  32. Does the client have any specific social proof elements to include? (e.g., client testimonials, case studies, trust badges)
  33. What is the client?s preferred method for handling errors or 404 pages?
  34. Are there any specific color schemes or themes for error pages or loading states?
  35. Does the client have any specific requirements for multi-language support?
  36. What is the client?s preferred method for handling form submissions? (e.g., email notifications, CRM integration)
  37. Are there any specific requirements for video or audio elements on the site?
  38. Does the client have any specific requirements for dark mode or light mode design?
  39. What is the client?s preferred method for handling user authentication? (e.g., login/logout, password recovery)
  40. Are there any specific requirements for data visualization or charts on the site?
