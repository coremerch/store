50 Proven Client Brief Prompts for Brand Identity Designers to Secure Premium Projects & Stand Out

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. Ask: ?What 3 adjectives best describe how you want your brand to feel to customers?? Note the answers verbatim.
  2. Request: ?Send me 5 brands you admire. For each, describe what specifically clicks with you.?
  3. Require: ?Provide your brand?s origin story in 60 words or less. Focus on the struggle or insight that led to it.?
  4. Ask for: ?Your brand?s top 3 competitors. List one thing each does better than you and one thing you do better than them.?
  5. Request: ?Share your brand?s mission statement. If none exists, ask them to draft one in 20 words or fewer.?
  6. Ask: ?What?s the one emotion you want people to feel when they see your logo?? Get a single word or short phrase.
  7. Require: ?List 3 existing visuals from your brand (e.g., colors, fonts, imagery style). Specify which one you want to keep.?
  8. Ask: ?What?s a brand you hate, and exactly why? Be specific about the visual or messaging that turns you off.?
  9. Request: ?Describe your ideal customer in one sentence: who they are, what they value, and their biggest frustration.?
  10. Ask for: ?Your brand?s current elevator pitch. If it?s longer than 30 words, ask them to shorten it to 15.?
  11. Require: ?Show me any past design files, even rough sketches. Note which elements resonate with you and which don?t.?
  12. Ask: ?What?s the core promise your brand makes to customers? Express it in 7 words or fewer.?
  13. Request: ?Share a brand (in any industry) that nailed visual identity. Explain what makes it work.?
  14. Ask for: ?Your brand?s tone of voice in 3 words. If unsure, ask them to pick one from: bold, warm, playful, serious, quirky.?
  15. Require: ?List 3 colors you associate with your brand. Ask which one is non-negotiable and why.?
  16. Ask: ?What?s a visual trend you want to avoid at all costs? Name it or describe it.?
  17. Request: ?Describe your brand?s personality as if it were a person. Give 3 traits and one flaw.?
  18. Ask for: ?Your brand?s tagline, if any. If not, ask them to brainstorm 3 options in 5 words or less.?
  19. Require: ?Share a piece of customer feedback that made you proud. Note the exact words used.?
  20. Ask: ?What?s the #1 business goal for your brand in the next 12 months? Tie it to design if possible.?
  21. Request: ?List 3 fonts you like and 3 you hate. Ask for reasons to understand preferences.?
  22. Ask for: ?Your brand?s current tagline. If outdated, ask them to draft a new one aligned with your mission.?
  23. Require: ?Describe your brand?s visual style in 3 words. If vague, prompt examples like: minimalist, retro, futuristic.?
  24. Ask: ?What?s a memorable visual from a brand you?ve worked with? Recreate it mentally to understand their taste.?
  25. Request: ?Share your brand?s values. If none exist, ask them to list 3 core beliefs in one sentence each.?
  26. Ask for: ?Your ideal customer?s biggest pain point. Get a specific example or quote from a real customer.?
  27. Require: ?List 3 design elements you want included in your identity (e.g., geometric shapes, handwritten font).?
  28. Ask: ?What?s a brand you followed for visuals but outgrew? Explain what changed in your taste.?
  29. Request: ?Describe your brand?s legacy in one sentence. Focus on the impact you want to leave.?
  30. Ask for: ?Your brand?s current color palette. Ask which shades are sacred and which are flexible.?
  31. Require: ?Share a mood board or Pinterest link. Ask which image best captures your vision and why.?
  32. Ask: ?What?s a visual clich? in your industry you want to avoid? Name it or describe it.?
  33. Request: ?List 3 adjectives that should never describe your brand. Use these to rule out design directions.?
  34. Ask for: ?Your brand?s origin date. Ask if it?s important to reflect this in the identity (e.g., retro vs. modern).?
  35. Require: ?Describe a brand identity project you admired. Note the designer?s name and key takeaways.?
  36. Ask: ?What?s a symbol or icon you associate with your brand? Even if vague, capture the essence.?
  37. Request: ?Share your brand?s social media handles. Ask for one post that best represents your voice.?
  38. Ask for: ?Your brand?s industry jargon. Ask them to simplify it for a 10-year-old.?
  39. Require: ?List 3 design tools you?re comfortable with (e.g., Illustrator, Procreate). Note any limitations.?
  40. Ask: ?What?s a brand you think has a weak identity? Explain what?s missing visually.?
