Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Accelerator: 40 Ready-to-Use Prompts to Jumpstart Every Project

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the core mission of your business in one sentence?
  2. List three adjectives that best describe the personality of your brand.
  3. Who is your ideal customer, and what do they value most?
  4. Describe the visual style of your top three competitors using three words each.
  5. What are the top three emotions you want your brand to evoke?
  6. What is the one thing your brand must be remembered for?
  7. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)?
  8. Do you have existing brand guidelines we should follow or adapt?
  9. What are the primary colors that represent your brand?s values?
  10. What fonts or typography styles do you associate with your brand?
  11. What is your brand?s tone of voice (e.g., professional, friendly, quirky)?
  12. Describe your brand?s ideal visual aesthetic (e.g., minimalist, retro, futuristic).
  13. What are the key messages you want to communicate to your audience?
  14. What is the name of your brand, and does it have any hidden meanings or significance?
  15. What are the main pain points your product or service solves for customers?
  16. What is your brand?s history, and are there any milestones worth highlighting?
  17. Do you have any logos, symbols, or icons that represent your brand?
  18. What is your brand?s position in the market compared to competitors?
  19. What are the biggest challenges your brand faces in terms of recognition?
  20. Who are your brand?s biggest advocates or influencers?
  21. What is the lifespan you expect for this brand identity (e.g., short-term campaign, long-term)?
  22. Do you have any specific cultural or regional references that should influence the design?
  23. What is your brand?s story, and how should it be reflected visually?
  24. What is your brand?s relationship with sustainability or social responsibility?
  25. Are there any visual or conceptual elements to avoid in your brand identity?
  26. What is your budget range for this brand identity project?
  27. What is your preferred timeline for delivering the final brand identity?
  28. Do you have any existing marketing materials we can reference for consistency?
  29. What are the primary platforms where your brand will be displayed (e.g., digital, print, packaging)?
  30. What is your brand?s relationship with color psychology, and do you have preferences?
  31. Are there any specific symbols, motifs, or patterns that represent your brand?s ethos?
  32. What is your brand?s stance on trends?do you want to follow them or set them?
  33. Do you have any existing customer feedback that highlights brand perception?
  34. What is your brand?s relationship with humor or wit in communication?
  35. Are there any historical or industry-specific elements that should inspire the design?
  36. What is your brand?s approach to inclusivity and accessibility in design?
  37. Do you have any specific imagery styles (e.g., photography, illustration) that align with your brand?
  38. What is your brand?s relationship with technology and innovation in design?
  39. Are there any legal or trademark considerations we need to account for in the design?
  40. What is your brand?s preferred level of detail in its visual identity (e.g., detailed, abstract)?
