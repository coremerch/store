Brand Identity Designer?s Client Brief: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Nail Every Project

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary emotion or personality trait you want your brand to evoke in your target audience?
  2. List three adjectives that describe how you want people to feel when they interact with your brand.
  3. Describe your brand?s mission in one sentence?what problem do you solve for your customers?
  4. Who is your ideal customer? Provide demographics (age, gender, location) and psychographics (values, interests, lifestyle).
  5. What are the top three brands you admire, and why? (Use these as visual or tonal inspiration.)
  6. Do you have existing brand assets (logo, colors, fonts) that must be incorporated into the new identity? If so, provide files or descriptions.
  7. What industries or competitors should your brand identity differentiate from? Provide 3-5 examples.
  8. Is there a specific color palette you love or hate? Share examples or hex codes to avoid or include.
  9. What type of typography (serif, sans-serif, script, etc.) aligns with your brand?s personality? Show 2-3 examples.
  10. Should the brand identity feel modern, classic, playful, or minimalist? Provide visual references if possible.
  11. What tone of voice should your brand use in communications? (Friendly, professional, quirky, authoritative, etc.)
  12. Do you have a tagline or slogan that must be integrated into the identity? If not, do you want one created?
  13. What is the primary use case for your brand identity? (Logo, packaging, website, social media, merchandise, etc.)
  14. Describe your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)?what makes you stand out from competitors?
  15. What are the key messages you want to communicate through your visual identity?
  16. Do you have any cultural, historical, or regional influences that should inspire the design?
  17. What is your budget range for this project? (This helps prioritize deliverables.)
  18. What is your timeline for completing this project? Include hard deadlines or milestones.
  19. Should the brand identity include custom illustrations, icons, or patterns? If so, provide examples or themes.
  20. Do you need guidelines for brand usage (e.g., logo spacing, color applications, font hierarchy)?
  21. What file formats do you need for your final brand assets? (AI, EPS, PNG, SVG, PDF, etc.)
  22. Are there any legal or trademark considerations we should be aware of for the logo or name?
  23. Do you want the brand identity to feel luxury, affordable, tech-forward, or artisanal? Provide visual references.
  24. What is your brand?s current perception in the market? How do you want to change or reinforce it?
  25. Should the identity include a mascot or character? If so, describe its personality or style.
  26. What are the non-negotiables for your brand identity? (E.g., must include a specific symbol, must be gender-neutral, etc.)
  27. Do you need social media-specific assets (profile pictures, cover photos, story templates)?
  28. What is your brand?s stance on sustainability or social responsibility? Should this influence the design?
  29. Are there any colors or symbols that have cultural or personal significance to include or avoid?
  30. What is the primary platform where your brand will be most visible? (E.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, billboards.)
  31. Do you want the identity to feel nostalgic, futuristic, retro, or timeless? Provide examples.
  32. What are your brand?s core values? How can these be reflected in the visual identity?
  33. Do you have any existing customer feedback or reviews that highlight your brand?s strengths or weaknesses?
  34. Should the identity include a secondary color palette for different applications (e.g., gradients, neutrals)?
  35. What is your brand?s relationship with humor or wit? Should the design include playful elements?
  36. Do you need a brand story or narrative to accompany the visual identity? If so, provide key points.
  37. What is your brand?s relationship with tradition vs. innovation? Should this influence the design direction?
  38. Are there any trends in your industry that you want to embrace or avoid in the identity?
  39. What is the scale of your business? (Startup, small business, enterprise) How should this influence the identity?
  40. Do you need a full brand book, or will a basic style guide suffice for your needs?
