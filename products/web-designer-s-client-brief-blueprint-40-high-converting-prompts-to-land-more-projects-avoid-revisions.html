Web Designer?s Client Brief Blueprint: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land More Projects & Avoid Revisions

client brief prompts for web designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the primary goal of this website (e.g., sell products, collect leads, showcase portfolio)?
  2. Who is the ideal target audience for this website (demographics, interests, pain points)?
  3. What are the top 3 competitors? websites you admire, and what specific elements do you like?
  4. What is the desired tone of the website (e.g., professional, playful, minimalist, bold)?
  5. Do you have brand guidelines (colors, fonts, logo usage) that must be followed?
  6. What is the main message or value proposition you want visitors to remember?
  7. How many pages do you envision for this website (e.g., Home, About, Services, Contact)?
  8. What type of content will each page include (e.g., text, images, videos, testimonials)?
  9. Do you have existing content (text, images, videos) that should be used, or will new content be created?
  10. What is the preferred layout style for the homepage (e.g., hero section, services grid, featured testimonials)?
  11. Do you need a blog or news section, and if so, how often will it be updated?
  12. What is the desired loading speed benchmark (e.g., under 2 seconds, under 3 seconds)?
  13. Do you need e-commerce functionality, and if so, which payment gateways should be integrated?
  14. What is the expected budget range for this project?
  15. What is the timeline for project completion (e.g., 4 weeks, 8 weeks)?
  16. Do you have any specific design elements you want to include (e.g., animations, parallax scrolling, dark mode)?
  17. What is the desired user experience flow (e.g., simple 3-step process, interactive journey)?
  18. Do you need a mobile app or responsive design for all devices?
  19. What is the preferred content management system (CMS) (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace)?
  20. Do you need SEO optimization built into the design from the start?
  21. What are the top 3 must-have features for this website?
  22. Do you need integration with third-party tools (e.g., Mailchimp, Slack, CRM)?
  23. What is the desired color palette for the website (provide hex codes if available)?
  24. Do you have any existing logos or branding materials that should be incorporated?
  25. What is the preferred font pairing for headings and body text?
  26. Do you need a custom illustration or icon set created for this project?
  27. What is the desired spacing and grid system for the layout (e.g., 8px grid, 12-column layout)?
  28. Do you need a contact form, live chat, or other lead generation tools?
  29. What is the desired navigation structure (e.g., mega menu, hamburger menu, sticky header)?
  30. Do you need a membership or login area for users?
  31. What is the desired visual hierarchy for key elements (e.g., CTAs, headlines, buttons)?
  32. Do you need a newsletter signup form or email capture functionality?
  33. What is the desired style for buttons and interactive elements (e.g., rounded corners, shadows, hover effects)?
  34. Do you need a custom 404 page or error handling design?
  35. What is the desired style for image galleries or portfolios (e.g., masonry, carousel, grid)?
  36. Do you need a custom favicon or social media sharing icons?
  37. What is the desired style for testimonials or case studies (e.g., card layout, carousel, grid)?
  38. Do you need a custom design for error messages or validation messages?
  39. What is the desired style for footers (e.g., minimalist, informative, decorative)?
  40. Do you need a custom design for pop-ups or modal windows?
