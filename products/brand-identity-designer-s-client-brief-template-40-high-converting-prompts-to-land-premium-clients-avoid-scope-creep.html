Brand Identity Designer's Client Brief Template: 40 High-Converting Prompts to Land Premium Clients & Avoid Scope Creep

client brief prompts for brand identity designers ? 40 prompts.

  1. What is the core mission of your business in one sentence?
  2. Describe your ideal customer persona in three detailed sentences.
  3. List the top three adjectives you want people to associate with your brand.
  4. What are the three biggest competitors in your space, and what do they do well or poorly?
  5. Provide 3-5 visual brand references (logos, colors, typography) that inspire you and explain why.
  6. What is the primary emotion you want your brand to evoke in customers?
  7. Do you have an existing brand style guide? If yes, attach it. If no, share any current brand elements (colors, fonts, imagery).
  8. What is your brand?s unique selling proposition (USP)?
  9. What is your brand?s tone of voice? (e.g., professional, playful, bold, minimalist)
  10. Do you have a tagline or slogan in mind? If so, share it. If not, describe the message you want it to convey.
  11. What is the name of your business, and how did you come up with it?
  12. What are the main products or services you offer?
  13. Who is your primary target audience? (Include demographics like age, gender, location, income level, etc.)
  14. What are the primary and secondary colors you envision for your brand? Provide hex codes if possible.
  15. What type of typography do you prefer for your logo and brand materials? (e.g., serif, sans-serif, script, bold, light)
  16. Do you have any specific imagery or photography style in mind? (e.g., minimalist, vibrant, vintage, modern)
  17. What is your brand?s personality? (e.g., trustworthy, innovative, luxurious, eco-friendly)
  18. Are there any brands you admire and want to emulate in terms of visual identity?
  19. What is your budget range for this project?
  20. What is your preferred timeline for completion?
  21. Do you need variations of your logo for different uses (e.g., social media, packaging, embroidery)?
  22. Will you need brand guidelines for internal team use?
  23. Are there any colors, symbols, or design elements you absolutely want to avoid?
  24. What is the story behind your brand? How did it come to be?
  25. What is your brand?s elevator pitch? (A 30-second summary of what you do and why you?re unique.)
  26. Do you have any existing marketing materials (business cards, website, ads) that represent your brand well? If so, share them.
  27. What is your brand?s stance on sustainability or social responsibility? Should this be reflected in the identity?
  28. Are there any cultural or regional sensitivities to consider in your brand?s visual identity?
  29. What is your brand?s relationship with its customers? (e.g., friendly, authoritative, community-driven)
  30. Do you have any specific industry standards or regulations that must be reflected in the design?
  31. What is your brand?s color palette beyond primary colors? (Include secondary and accent colors.)
  32. Are there any fonts you love or hate for this project?
  33. Do you want your brand identity to feel classic, modern, retro, or futuristic?
  34. What is your brand?s main communication channel? (e.g., digital, print, social media, packaging)
  35. Do you need a mascot or character to represent your brand?
  36. Are there any specific design trends you want to incorporate or avoid?
  37. What is your brand?s key message or promise to customers?
  38. Do you have any specific examples of design styles that you do not want to replicate?
  39. What are the key milestones or deliverables you expect from this project?
  40. Is there anything else you?d like me to know about your brand or vision that hasn?t been covered?
