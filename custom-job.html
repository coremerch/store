A clear, structured process from brief to handoff. No ambiguity. No scope creep. Fixed price agreed before a single line of code is written.

We review your brief and respond within 24 hours with either a written scope document or a request for a short call to clarify requirements. We will ask questions until we understand exactly what you need.

You receive a detailed scope document with a fixed price, delivery timeline, and list of exactly what will be delivered. You approve it or request revisions. No work starts until you confirm.

⏱ Per agreed timeline
06
Post-Handoff Support

Every custom project includes a 30-day support window after handoff. If anything does not work as specified, we fix it at no additional cost within that window.

FAQ

Custom Work Questions

Do I pay before or after the work is done?

+

For projects under $500 — full payment upfront. For projects $500–$1,500 — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. For projects above $1,500 — 40% upfront, 40% at midpoint milestone, 20% on final delivery. All payments are in cryptocurrency via NOWPayments.

Who owns the code after delivery?

+

You do. Completely. All source code, all configuration files, all credentials are handed to you at delivery. We retain no copies and have no ongoing access to your systems after the project is complete.

Can you work with my existing system?

+

Yes. If you already have a website, a store, or an existing backend, we can integrate with it or extend it. Share details about your current setup in the brief form and we will advise on the best approach.

What if the project is not what I expected?

+

We agree on a detailed scope before any work begins. If the delivered system does not match the agreed scope, we fix it at no cost within the 30-day support window. Scope changes requested after agreement are quoted separately.

Can I resell what you build for me?

+

Standard custom builds are for your use. If you want resale rights — to sell the system to your own clients or use it as a white-label product — specify this in your brief. We offer white-label builds with full resale rights at an additional licensing fee.

Do you sign NDAs?

+

Yes. For projects above $500 we are happy to sign a mutual NDA before discovery begins. Mention this in your brief and we will include it in the scope process.
