## What Is a Freelancer Kit in Notion?

A **Freelancer Kit** is a **Notion template** designed specifically for independent designers (or any freelancer) to manage their business. It typically includes:

  • **Client database** (contact info, contracts, invoices)
  • **Project tracker** (deadlines, status, deliverables)
  • **Task management** (to-do lists, priorities)
  • **Time tracking** (hours logged per project)
  • **Finance tracking** (income, expenses, profitability)
  • **Portfolio & proposals** (case studies, pitch decks)

These templates are **pre-built databases, calendars, and boards** that you can **duplicate** into your own Notion workspace and customize.

## Step-by-Step: How to Set Up a Freelancer Kit in Notion

### Step 1: Choose a Template

Pick one that matches your needs:

  • **Minimalist:** Use Notion?s built-in "Freelancer Dashboard."
  • **All-in-one:** Try "FreelanceOS" from GitHub or Reddit.
  • **Visual:** Use a template with gallery views for portfolio work.

### Step 2: Duplicate the Template

  • Click **"Duplicate"** in the top-right corner of the template page.
  • It will open in **your Notion workspace** under a new name.

### Step 3: Customize the Structure

Every freelancer?s workflow is different. Here?s what to adjust:

#### ? Example: FreelanceOS Customization

1. **Clients Database**

  • Add columns: **Email**, **Contract Link**, **Project Status**, **Budget**
  • Use **select tags** like: "Active", "On Hold", "Completed"

2. **Projects Database**

  • Add: **Deadline**, **Priority (High/Medium/Low)**, **Assigned Client**
  • Use **relation property** to link to Clients database

3. **Tasks Kanban Board**

  • Columns: **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Review**, **Done**
  • Add **due dates** and **assignees**

4. **Finance Tracker**

  • Add: **Income**, **Expenses**, **Profit**
  • Use **formulas** to auto-calculate totals:
  • `prop("Income") - prop("Expenses") = Profit`

? **Tip:** Use **Notion?s "Advanced" settings** to enable **formulas**, **relations**, and **rollups**.

## How to Import a .zip Template into Notion

Some templates come as **.zip files** with a `.csv` or `.json` export.

1. **Download** the .zip file.

2. **Unzip** it to find a `.csv`, `.json`, or `.md` file.

3. In Notion:

  • Click **"Import"** in the left sidebar.
  • Choose the file type (.csv for databases, .md for pages).
  • Follow prompts to map data fields.

?? **Note:** Not all templates support import. If a template is meant to be duplicated directly, skip the import step.
