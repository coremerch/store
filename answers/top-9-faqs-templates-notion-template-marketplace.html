## How to Choose the Right Template

1. **Identify your audience:** A developer?s FAQs will differ vastly from a nonprofit?s.

2. **Check the structure:** Do you need categories, toggles, or embedded content?

3. **Test usability:** Click through the template to see if it?s intuitive.

4. **Customize thoroughly:** Replace all placeholder text and links with your own.

## Pro Tips for Customizing Your FAQs

  • **Add a search bar** (using Notion?s `/inline` search) for large FAQs pages.
  • **Embed related pages** (e.g., a ?Terms of Service? page) for deeper context.
  • **Use icons** (via `/emoji` or third-party icon sets) to improve visual hierarchy.
  • **Link to your FAQs** from your homepage, footer, and contact page.
