## How to Choose the Right Template

1. **Identify your biggest pain point.** If you struggle with client management, prioritize templates with strong CRM features. If finances are the issue, focus on templates with built-in income/expense tracking.

2. **Check the setup time.** If you need something quick, go for templates labeled "Simple" or "Starter." If you?re willing to invest time in customization, choose advanced templates like "Freelance OS" or "Freelance Workspace."

3. **Look for tutorials.** Many templates include video walkthroughs or documentation. Prioritize these if you?re new to Notion.

4. **Test compatibility.** Not all templates work well on mobile. If you freelance on the go, check if the template has a mobile-friendly layout.

---

## How to Import a Notion Template

1. Click the download link for your chosen template.

2. In Notion, open the template page and click the **"Duplicate"** button (top-right corner).

3. Rename the duplicated page to avoid confusion.

4. Customize the template by adding your clients, projects, and deadlines.

5. Delete any unused sections to keep your workspace clean.
