FAQ

Honest Answers.
Real Questions.

Do my buyers need to own crypto?

+

Yes. Your buyers need to hold cryptocurrency. This is the correct audience — and it is large and growing. Crypto adoption is fastest in Sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and Latin America — exactly where COREMERCH infrastructure is most relevant. The freebie funnel captures non-crypto buyers so you can convert them as adoption grows.

Are there really no monthly fees?

+

No monthly fees from COREMERCH. The only ongoing fee is 0.5% per transaction charged by NOWPayments. On a $50 sale that is 25 cents. Buyers pay blockchain network fees when sending — we recommend low-fee networks like BSC, Solana, or TRON where fees are typically under $0.10.

How does automatic payment verification work?

+

NOWPayments monitors the blockchain in real time. When your buyer sends payment, NOWPayments detects the on-chain confirmation and fires a webhook to your backend automatically. Your backend logs the order and delivers the product. You verify nothing manually. The blockchain verifies. NOWPayments bridges. Your system delivers.

What do I own after deployment?

+

Everything. Your store code, customer list, order history, product files, wallet, NOWPayments account, domain, and analytics — all under your accounts. We have no access after delivery. If COREMERCH closed tomorrow, your store would run exactly as it does today.

What if I am not technical?

+

Choose the Done-For-You Stack Deployment ($197) or the Full Business Build ($497). We configure everything under your accounts and hand you a working store. No technical knowledge required — just the ability to create a free account on GitHub or Cloudflare and share credentials with us securely during setup.
