Common Questions

Before You Write To Us —
Check These First.

📧 I paid but did not receive my product.

Check your spam and promotions folders first. Automated delivery emails frequently land there. If the product is not in spam, contact us with your transaction ID and email address and we will resend immediately.

⏱️ How long does Done For You take?

The clock starts when we receive your completed setup questionnaire. The Starter Stack and Edge Stack take 48 hours. The Ghost Stack takes 72 hours. The Full Business Build takes 5–7 business days.

🔐 My NOWPayments webhook is not firing.

Check that your Google Apps Script Web App URL is correctly set as the IPN callback URL in NOWPayments. Make sure the Web App is deployed as "Anyone" access. The B2 NOWPayments Masterclass covers this in detail.

💳 Can I get a refund?

Yes, in the right circumstances. Done-for-you deployments carry a delivery guarantee. Digital downloads are non-refundable after download unless the product does not match its description. See our full Refund Policy for details.

🌍 Does this work in my country?

Yes. COREMERCH infrastructure works in all 195 countries. The crypto payment layer has no geographic restrictions. The only requirement is that your buyers hold cryptocurrency.

💡 Which stack should I choose?

Take the Stack Selector Quiz on the free resources page — it asks 5 questions and recommends the right stack for your situation. Or contact us and we will advise based on your specific requirements.
