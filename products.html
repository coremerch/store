Product FAQ

Questions Before You Buy.

What happens after I pay?

+

For blueprints and templates — you receive the download link in your email within minutes of payment confirmation. For done-for-you deployments — we send you a setup questionnaire within 2 hours. You fill it in, we build, we hand you a working store within the stated delivery window.

Do I need technical knowledge for the Done For You options?

+

No. You need to create a free account on GitHub and Google for Stack 01, or Cloudflare and Supabase for Stack 02, and share credentials with us securely during setup. We handle everything else. You receive a working store with clear instructions on how to use it.

What cryptocurrencies will my store accept?

+

NOWPayments supports over 300 cryptocurrencies. We configure your store with the most commonly used — USDT on BSC, TRON, and Solana, plus BTC, ETH, BNB, and SOL. We recommend enabling low-fee networks as your primary options to minimize friction for buyers.

Can I add more products after deployment?

+

Yes. Adding products involves uploading a file, updating a configuration entry, and adding a product page. The blueprint documentation covers this in full. For Done For You clients, adding products beyond the included count is available as an add-on service.

What is the refund policy?

+

For Done For You deployments — if your store is not fully operational within the stated delivery window, you pay nothing. For blueprints and templates — due to the digital nature, we do not offer refunds after download. Contact us before purchasing if you have questions about whether a product is right for you.
